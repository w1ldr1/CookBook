## 1. THE PROVIDER’S OATH
The kitchen is not a domestic annex; it is the **Sustainment Center** for the Unit. The man who cooks provides more than calories; he provides security, routine, and leadership.

## 2. THE SERIES ROADMAP (Future Deployments)
This manual is Volume 1 of a multi-part series:
*   **Vol 1: FOUNDATIONS (Current):** Essential kitchen readiness and indoor ops.
*   **Vol 2: FLAME & STEEL:** Outdoor engagement, wood-fire management, and advanced BBQ.
*   **Vol 3: DEEP FREEZE:** Tactical meal prep, vacuum sealing, and long-term pantry fortification.

## 3. THE COMMAND BRIEFING (The Family Table)
The evening meal is the "Daily Brief." 
*   **Rule 01:** Phones are "EMPs" (Electromagnetic Pulses). They are neutralized (placed in a basket/different room) during the meal.
*   **Rule 02:** Every meal begins with a "Unit Status Check" (How was the day? What is the objective for tomorrow?).
