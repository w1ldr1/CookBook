# INTELLIGENCE: FIELD REPAIR SOP
**Subject:** Mission Salvage and Error Neutralization
**Mission Objective:** To identify and correct operational failures in real-time.

---

## 1. THERMAL OVERSHOOT (Overcooked Meat)
*   **The Threat:** The protein is dry and "Target Temperature" has been exceeded.
*   **The Salvage:** Slice the protein as thin as possible against the grain. Submerge in a warm "Extraction Fluid" (SOP-033 Pan Sauce or SOP-011 Broth) for 5 minutes before serving. Moisture must be reintroduced externally.

## 2. SALINITY OVERLOAD (Too Salty)
*   **The Threat:** Excessive salt application in Phase 2.
*   **The Salvage:** 
    *   *For Liquids:* Add an acidic "Catalyst" (Lemon juice or Vinegar) or a fat (Heavy Cream). This doesn't remove salt but "re-calibrates" the palate's perception.
    *   *The Potato Maneuver:* Drop a peeled, quartered potato into the simmer. It acts as a "Salt Sponge." Extract and discard after 10 minutes.

## 3. TEXTURAL FAILURE (Broken Sauces)
*   **The Threat:** Fat is separating from the liquid (looks greasy/grainy).
*   **The Salvage:** 
    *   *For Cold Sauces:* Add a teaspoon of warm water and whisk violently. 
    *   *For Hot Sauces:* Remove from heat. Add a splash of cold heavy cream or a "Roux" to re-bind the molecules.

## 4. ADHESION FAILURE (Meat Sticking to Pan)
*   **The Threat:** Protein was deployed before "Thermal Saturation" of the hardware.
*   **The Salvage:** Do not force extraction. Add a tablespoon of water or wine to the base of the pan. The steam will initiate a "Micro-Deglaze" and release the protein without tearing the "Armor" (crust).

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Philosophy:** A mistake is only a failure if it hits the trash bin. Every mission can be salvaged into "Field Rations" if the "Hostage Rescue" (presentation) is compromised.
