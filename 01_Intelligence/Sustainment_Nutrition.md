# INTELLIGENCE: SUSTAINMENT NUTRITION
**Subject:** Biological Fuel Management and Body Composition
**Mission Objective:** To treat food as "Internal Logistics" for performance, recovery, and longevity.

---

## 1. THE MACRO-NUTRIENT HIERARCHY
In the Tactical Kitchen, we categorize macros by their operational function:

*   **PROTEIN (The Armor):** Essential for muscle repair and structural integrity. 
    *   *Target:* 0.8g – 1g per lb of body weight.
    *   *Sources:* SOP-001 (Steak), SOP-007 (Bird), SOP-008 (Salmon).
*   **CARBOHYDRATES (The Fuel):** High-octane energy for high-intensity missions (Work/Gym). 
    *   *Target:* Adjusted based on daily activity level.
    *   *Sources:* SOP-009 (Rice), SOP-040 (Potatoes), SOP-019 (Bread).
*   **FATS (The Lubricant):** Hormone regulation and long-burn energy.
    *   *Sources:* SOP-036 (Avocado/Nuts), SOP-001 (Animal fats).

---

## 2. MISSION PROFILES (Eating for the Objective)

### [ ] Profile A: RECON (Fat Loss / Leaning Out)
*   **Strategy:** Prioritize Protein (The Armor) and Green Cover (Fiber). 
*   **Deployment:** Double the "Vegetable Payload" (SOP-006) and reduce "Grain Silo" (SOP-009) by 50%.
*   **Field Intel:** Drink 16oz of water (Hydration) before every meal to increase satiety.

### [ ] Profile B: BULGE (Muscle Gain / Mass)
*   **Strategy:** Increase Carbohydrates and Fats. 
*   **Deployment:** Add a "Secondary Objective" to every meal (e.g., add SOP-019 Bread or double the Rice portion in SOP-002).
*   **Field Intel:** Utilize SOP-036 (Smoothies) as a calorie-dense "Force Multiplier" between missions.

---

## 3. THE "80/20" RULES OF ENGAGEMENT
*   **80% Compliance:** Whole foods, high-quality proteins, and home-cooked SOPs.
*   **20% Flexibility:** "Social Ops" and Hostage Rescue missions where optimal nutrition is secondary to morale and social cohesion.

## 4. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **The Scale vs. The Mirror:** Weight is a data point, but performance is the objective. If the "Unit" feels sluggish, increase "Hydration" and "Protein" intake.
