# INTELLIGENCE: GROCERY STORE RECON
**Subject:** Area of Operations (AO) Navigation and Target Identification
**Mission Objective:** To extract high-quality "Biological Fuel" while avoiding "Synthetic Fillers" and marketing traps.

---

## 1. THE PERIMETER STRATEGY
The most critical assets (Produce, Meat, Dairy) are located on the **Perimeter** of the AO.
*   **The Kill Zone (Center Aisles):** This is where processed, shelf-stable "Non-Essential" items are stored. Minimize time spent in the center to avoid high-fructose and seed-oil contaminants.

## 2. TARGET IDENTIFICATION (Produce Recon)
Identify asset quality before acquisition:
*   **Avocados:** Target must have a "Slight Give" but no soft spots. If the "button" (stem) pops off easily and is green underneath, the asset is optimal.
*   **Leafy Greens:** Check the "Base of the Bag." If there is liquid or "slush," the asset is compromised.
*   **Root Vegetables:** Target must be firm. Any "sprouting" indicates the asset is past its operational peak.

## 3. DECODING INTELLIGENCE (Label Reading)
Ignore the front of the package (Marketing Propaganda). Flip to the **Ingredients List**.
*   **The Rule of Five:** If a product contains more than 5 ingredients, or ingredients you cannot pronounce, it is likely "Synthetic Rationing."
*   **Red Flag Neutralization:** Avoid any assets containing:
    1.  *High Fructose Corn Syrup* (Metabolic disruptor).
    2.  *Seed Oils* (Soybean, Canola, Cottonseed - Inflammatory).
    3.  *Artificial Colors* (Red 40, Yellow 5).

## 4. FIELD INTEL: THE RE-STOCK CYCLE
Most grocery AOs restock fresh produce and meat on **Tuesdays or Wednesdays**. Schedule your "Recce & Resupply" missions accordingly to secure the freshest assets.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Secondary Objective:** Never enter the AO without a "Manifest" (Shopping List). Shopping without a list leads to "Impulse Acquisitions" and budget failure.
*   **Field Intel:** Use the "Self-Checkout" lane to maintain speed and minimize engagement with non-essential personnel.
