# INTELLIGENCE: KITCHEN SANITATION SOP
**Subject:** Pathogen Neutralization and Area Maintenance
**Mission Objective:** To prevent "Foodborne Casualties" and maintain operational readiness of the Base.

---

## 1. THE "DANGER ZONE" (Temperature Intel)
Pathogens (Bacteria) thrive between **40°F and 140°F**.
*   **The 2-Hour Rule:** Perishable supplies (meat/dairy) must never remain in the Danger Zone for more than 2 hours. If exceeded, the supplies are compromised and must be neutralized (discarded).

---

## 2. HAND HYGIENE (The First Line of Defense)
1.  **The Scrub:** Use warm water and soap. Scrub for 20 seconds (sing the "Sustainment Anthem").
2.  **Critical Intercepts:** Wash hands after touching raw protein, after handling waste, and before "Plating" final rations.

---

## 3. SURFACE NEUTRALIZATION
*   **Two-Board System:** 
    *   **Green Board:** Produce and bread only.
    *   **Red/Wood Board:** Raw proteins only.
*   **Chemical Warfare:** Use a solution of 1 tbsp bleach to 1 gallon of water (or a high-grade kitchen sanitizer) to wipe down counters after every "Heavy Lift" mission.

---

## 4. WASTE MANAGEMENT
1.  **Immediate Extraction:** Clear the "Waste Bowl" as soon as it reaches capacity. 
2.  **Disposal:** Seal high-odor waste (meat trimmings) in a separate bag before placing in the primary bin to prevent "Atmospheric Contamination" (smells).

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Spill Response:** Neutralize spills immediately. A wet floor is a slipping hazard during high-speed engagement.
*   **Sponge Maintenance:** Sponges are "Bacteria Magnets." Replace every 2 weeks or sanitize in the dishwasher on high-heat cycles.
