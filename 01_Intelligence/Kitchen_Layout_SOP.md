# INTELLIGENCE: KITCHEN LAYOUT SOP
**Subject:** Workspace Optimization and Galley Readiness
**Mission Objective:** To organize the "Kitchen AO" for maximum efficiency and reduced "Mission Fatigue."

---

## 1. THE WORK TRIANGLE (Tactical Positioning)
The core of your AO consists of three points. The path between them must be clear of "Obstructions" (clutter):
1.  **The Cold Storage (Fridge)**
2.  **The Heat Source (Stove/Oven)**
3.  **The Sanitation Center (Sink)**

## 2. MISE EN PLACE (Tactical Staging)
"Everything in its place." Before the "Engagement Phase" (Heat) begins, all "Supplies" must be:
*   **Processed:** Chopped, peeled, and measured.
*   **Staged:** Placed in small bowls near the heat source.
*   **Neutralized:** All trash and packaging removed from the AO.

## 3. THE "GOLDEN REEL" (Tool Placement)
*   **Tier 1 (Arms Reach):** Items used in 90% of missions (Primary Blade, Salt Cellar, Oil, Spatula).
*   **Tier 2 (Nearby Cabinet):** Standard Hardware (Cast Iron, Pots, Mixing Bowls).
*   **Tier 3 (Deep Storage):** Specialized Gear (Dutch Oven, Blender, Slow Cooker).

## 4. ATMOSPHERIC CONTROL
*   **Lighting:** Ensure "Task Lighting" is focused on the cutting board to prevent blade accidents.
*   **Audio:** Maintain a "Mission Soundtrack" or "Silence" based on the complexity of the SOP. Avoid distracting media (TV/News) during high-velocity engagement.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **The "Clean as You Go" Doctrine:** Every "Passive Dwell" (waiting for water to boil or meat to sear) should be spent neutralizing dirty hardware or wiping surfaces. The mission is not over until the AO is reset to "Zero State."
