# INTELLIGENCE: TEMPORAL EGG BALLISTICS
**Subject:** Time-on-Target for Optimal Yolk Consistency
**Mission Objective:** To achieve 100% predictable results for boiled eggs based on "Engagement Time."

---

## 1. THE ENGAGEMENT SOP
1.  **Boil Phase:** Bring a pot of water to a rolling boil.
2.  **Deployment:** Gently lower large eggs (straight from the fridge) into the water using a slotted spoon.
3.  **The Timer:** Start the countdown immediately upon immersion.

## 2. THE RANGE CARD (Timing)

| Time (Minutes) | Yolk Status | Mission Profile |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **6 Minutes** | Liquid Gold | "Soft Boiled." For dipping toast soldiers. |
| **7 Minutes** | Jammy/Custard | "The Ramen Egg." Optimal for SOP-002 bowls. |
| **8 Minutes** | Soft-Set | Creamy center, firm white. |
| **10 Minutes** | Hard-Set | Fully cooked but still bright yellow. |
| **12 Minutes** | Field Stable | "Hard Boiled." For SOP-012/Salads/Travel Rations. |

---

## 3. THE "ICE BATH" EXTRACTION (Exfil)
Once the timer hits zero, you must **NEUTRALIZE** the thermal energy immediately.
1.  **Action:** Move eggs to a bowl filled with 50% ice and 50% water.
2.  **Duration:** Let sit for 5 minutes. This prevents the "Green Ring of Death" (overcooked sulfur) and makes peeling 50% easier.
