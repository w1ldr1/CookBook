# INTELLIGENCE: KITCHEN FIRST AID SOP
**Subject:** Damage Control and Injury Neutralization
**Mission Objective:** To manage minor injuries and prevent "Mission Termination" due to accidents.

---

## 1. THERMAL INJURIES (Burns)
*   **Minor Burn (Redness):** Run under **cool** (not cold) water for 10 minutes. Do not apply ice directly; it can cause tissue damage.
*   **Treatment:** Apply a thin layer of aloe vera. Avoid butter or oils on burns (contrary to old myths).
*   **Threshold:** If blisters cover more than 2 inches, seek medical intel.

---

## 2. BLADE INJURIES (Cuts)
*   **Phase 1:** Apply direct pressure with a clean towel to stop the bleed.
*   **Phase 2:** Clean with mild soap and water. 
*   **Phase 3:** Apply antibiotic ointment and a bandage.
*   **Threshold:** If the cut is deep (fat or muscle visible) or won't stop bleeding after 5 minutes of pressure, exfil to an Urgent Care center.

---

## 3. GREASE FIRE NEUTRALIZATION
*   **NEVER USE WATER:** Water will cause a grease fire to explode.
*   **Primary Maneuver:** Cover the pan with a lid to starve the fire of oxygen.
*   **Secondary Maneuver:** Use Baking Soda or a Class B fire extinguisher.

---

## 4. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Inventory:** Maintain a "Kitchen Med Kit" in your pantry including: Band-Aids, Burn Cream, Sterile Gauze, and Medical Tape.
*   **Prevention:** Most injuries happen when the operator is rushing or distracted. Maintain focus during all "Engagement" phases.
