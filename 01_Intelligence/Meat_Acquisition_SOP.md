# INTELLIGENCE: MEAT ACQUISITION & STORAGE
**Subject:** Strategic Sourcing and Preservation of Primary Proteins
**Mission Objective:** To maximize the "Provider" budget while securing high-grade biological fuel for the Unit.

---

## 1. THE GRADING SYSTEM (Intel Verification)
Not all targets are created equal. Understand the USDA grades before engaging.

*   **SELECT:** Lean, lower fat content. Good for "Quick Strike" stir-fry where sauce provides the moisture.
*   **CHOICE:** High quality, moderate marbling. The "Standard Issue" for most missions (SOP-001/003).
*   **PRIME:** Top 2% of beef. Intense marbling. Use for "Hostage Rescue" or high-priority morale-boost missions.

---

## 2. BULK ACQUISITION STRATEGY
Buying individual steaks is a "Retail Trap." Move to wholesale maneuvers to save 30–50% on cost.

### [ ] The "Sub-Primal" Maneuver
Purchase the entire muscle (e.g., a whole Ribeye Loin or Brisket) from a warehouse club. Use your "Primary Blade" to break it down into individual steaks. 
*   **Result:** You control the thickness and save significantly on the "Butcher's Tax."

### [ ] The "Quarter-Cow" Deployment
If you have the storage capacity (Chest Freezer), source directly from a local ranch. 
*   **Tactical Advantage:** Fixed price-per-pound for every cut (from Ground Beef to Filet Mignon). 

---

## 3. COLD STORAGE SOP (Preservation)
Improper storage leads to "Freezer Burn" (Equipment Failure).

1.  **Air is the Enemy:** Oxygen causes oxidation and ice crystals. 
2.  **Vacuum Sealing:** The gold standard for long-term storage. A vacuum-sealed steak remains viable for 12+ months.
3.  **Labeling:** Every package must be marked with **Target Name** and **Date of Acquisition (DOA)**. Use a permanent marker on a freezer bag or tape.

---

## 4. DEFROSTING PROTOCOLS (Infil)
*   **The Preferred Method:** 24-hour slow thaw in the refrigerator. Maintains cell structure and juice retention.
*   **The Emergency Method:** Submerge vacuum-sealed protein in a bowl of **cold** water. Change water every 30 minutes. 
*   **Warning:** Never use a microwave to defrost. It initiates the cooking phase prematurely and unevenly.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Relationship Building:** Get to know your local butcher. They can provide "Off-Menu" intel on when the next shipment of high-grade protein arrives or offer "Manager Specials" on bulk items.
*   **Seasonality:** Watch for "Holiday Surplus." Buy turkeys in November and hams in December when prices are at their lowest and freeze for future ops.
