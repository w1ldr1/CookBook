# INTELLIGENCE: COLD-CHAIN LOGISTICS
**Subject:** Optimizing Refrigeration and Freezer Area Operations
**Mission Objective:** To maximize the shelf-life of supplies and maintain the "First-In, First-Out" (FIFO) inventory system.

---

## 1. THERMAL ZONING (Refrigeration)
Not all areas of the fridge are equal.

*   **Zone: The Door (High Fluctuation):** Store high-stability items only (Condiments, Hot Sauce). Never store eggs or milk here.
*   **Zone: Bottom Shelf (The Coldest):** Store raw proteins here. This prevents "Drip Contamination" onto lower items.
*   **Zone: Crisper Drawers:** High humidity for produce; low humidity for fruits.

---

## 2. THE FREEZER "AMMO CRATE"
1.  **The Deep Freeze:** Store bulk-acquired proteins (SOP-013 Briskets, etc.) at the bottom/back where the temp is most stable.
2.  **The Prep Bag:** Store pre-cut vegetable scraps for "Operation: Bone Broth" in a dedicated freezer bag.
3.  **Labeling SOP:** Every item must have a "Date of Infil" (Date put in) and "Asset Name."

---

## 3. FIFO: THE GOLDEN RULE
**FIRST-IN, FIRST-OUT.**
When you restock, move the older items to the front and the new items to the back. This ensures no "Expired Assets" are lost in the dark corners of the AO.

---

## 4. EMERGENCY POWER-LOSS SOP
In the event of a grid failure:
1.  **Maintain Seal:** Do not open the fridge/freezer unless absolutely necessary.
2.  **Endurance:** A full freezer will maintain safety for 48 hours; a fridge for 4 hours.
3.  **Post-Event AAR:** If the internal temp exceeds 40°F for more than 2 hours, the assets are compromised. Neutralize (discard).
