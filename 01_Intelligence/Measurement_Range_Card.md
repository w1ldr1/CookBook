## 1. VOLUME CONVERSIONS (Liquid Ammo)

*   **1 Tablespoon (tbsp)** = 3 Teaspoons (tsp) = 15ml
*   **1 Fluid Ounce (oz)** = 2 Tablespoons = 30ml
*   **1 Cup** = 8 Fluid Ounces = 236ml (~240ml)
*   **1 Pint** = 2 Cups = 16oz
*   **1 Quart** = 4 Cups = 32oz = 0.95 Liters (~1 Liter)
