# INTELLIGENCE: MANUAL BLADE MANEUVERS
**Subject:** Standardized Cutting Techniques for Safety and Efficiency
**Mission Objective:** To increase prep speed (Infil) and ensure uniform cooking through standardized sizing.

---

## 1. THE "CLAW" GRIP (Safety SOP)
*   **The Problem:** Fingers exposed to the blade path.
*   **The Maneuver:** Tuck fingertips inward like a claw. Use the knuckles to guide the blade. The blade edge should never be higher than your knuckles.

---

## 2. STANDARDIZED CUTS (Terminology)

### [ ] The "Rough Chop" (General Demolition)
*   **Use Case:** Slow-cookers, stews, or items that will be blended.
*   **Spec:** 1-inch to 2-inch irregular chunks. Speed is favored over precision.

### [ ] The "Dice" (Precision Fragmentation)
*   **Use Case:** Onions, peppers, and aromatics for Quick Strike missions.
*   **Spec:** Uniform cubes (1/4 inch). Ensures all pieces soften at the exact same rate.

### [ ] The "Julienne" (Slivering)
*   **Use Case:** Peppers or carrots for stir-fry (SOP-002).
*   **Spec:** Long, thin matchsticks. Maximizes surface area for rapid searing.

---

## 3. THE "ROCKING" MOTION
*   **Technique:** Keep the tip of the knife in contact with the board at all times. Use a circular "pumping" motion.
*   **Advantage:** Minimizes fatigue and increases control during high-volume vegetable processing.

---

## 4. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Waste Management:** Save all "Scraps" (onion ends, carrot peels, celery butts) in a bag in the freezer for "Operation: Bone Broth."
*   **Board Discipline:** Always clear your board into a "Waste Bowl" frequently to keep your Area of Operations (AO) clear.
