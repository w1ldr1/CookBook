# INTELLIGENCE: THERMAL SAFETY BRIEF
**Subject:** Terminal Internal Temperatures and Pathogen Neutralization
**Mission Objective:** To ensure 100% food safety while preventing "Mission Failure" (overcooked, dry rations).

## 2. THE PHYSICS OF "CARRY-OVER COOKING"
The mission does not end when you remove the meat from the heat. Residual thermal energy will continue to raise the internal temperature.

*   **The Pull Rule:** Always remove the target from the heat source **5°F BEFORE** your desired final temperature. 
*   **The Rest Phase:** During the 10-15 minute rest period, the temperature will "coast" up to the final target. 
*   **Warning:** Failure to account for carry-over cooking is the #1 cause of "Dry Rations."

---

## 3. PATHOGEN NEUTRALIZATION (Safety SOP)
*   **Poultry:** Salmonellosis is a high-risk threat. Always verify 165°F (final) in the thickest part of the thigh/breast.
*   **Ground Meats:** Surface bacteria are mixed throughout the product during the grinding process. Ground meat must reach 160°F to ensure total neutralization.
*   **Cross-Contamination:** Hardware (knives/boards) that touch raw "Supplies" must be sanitized before touching "Extraction" (cooked) items.

---

## 4. COLOR IS AN UNRELIABLE INTEL SOURCE
*   **The Myth:** "Cook until the juices run clear" or "Cook until it’s not pink."
*   **The Reality:** Myoglobin (the red liquid) can remain pink even at safe temperatures, and can turn grey before safe temperatures are reached. 
*   **Standard Procedure:** Trust the **Rangefinder** (Digital Thermometer) over visual cues.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Calibration:** Check your thermometer accuracy once a year by placing it in a glass of crushed ice and water. It should read 32°F (0°C).
*   **Placement:** Ensure the probe tip is in the center-mass of the target, not touching bone or the pan surface, which will give a "False Positive" high reading.
