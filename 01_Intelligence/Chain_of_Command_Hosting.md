# INTELLIGENCE: CHAIN OF COMMAND (HOSTING)
**Subject:** Tactical Leadership and Guest Management
**Mission Objective:** To transform a meal into a high-value social experience.

---

## 1. THE "OPERATIONAL" SETTING
Your table is your "Command Center."
*   **Ambient Control:** Lighting should be dimmed; music should be low. The "Atmospheric Integrity" must support conversation, not distract from it.
*   **Water Readiness:** Pitchers of water should be on the table before "Assets" (guests) are seated.

## 2. THE HOSTING SOP (Lead from the Front)
1.  **The First Round:** As the Provider, you serve the guests first. Start with the oldest or most senior guest, then the others. You serve yourself last.
2.  **The Toast:** Briefly "Neutralize the Silence" before eating. Acknowledge the guests and the mission (The Meal).
3.  **Refill Surveillance:** Monitor guest water/beverage levels. Refill before the glass is empty so they never have to ask.

## 3. CONVERSATION DISCIPLINE
*   **Neutral Zone:** Avoid high-friction "Hostile Topics" (Politics/Religion) unless the Unit is in 100% agreement.
*   **Engagement:** Ask "Open-Ended Questions." Your job as the Provider is to facilitate the flow of data among the guests.

## 4. THE CLEAN-UP RETREAT
*   **Post-Action:** Do not begin aggressive "Sanitation Ops" (dishes) while guests are still present. It signals that the "Mission is Over" and they should exfiltrate.
*   **The Pivot:** Move the Unit to a different area (living room/patio) for coffee/dessert to signal a shift in the mission phase.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Feedback Loop:** A successful mission is measured by the "Return Rate" of your guests. If they are asking when the next "SOP Deployment" is, you have achieved Best-Seller status.
