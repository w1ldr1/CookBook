## 2. THE BALANCE EQUATION (S.A.F.E.)
If a dish tastes "off," it is usually missing one of these pillars:
1.  **S - SALT:** Enhances every other flavor.
2.  **A - ACID:** (Lemon/Vinegar) "Cuts" through fat and brightens dull flavors.
3.  **F - FAT:** (Butter/Oil) Carries flavor across the palate.
4.  **E - HEAT:** (Pepper/Spice) Adds depth and complexity.

---

## 3. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **The Taster's Rule:** Taste your work during Phase 2 (Engagement). If it's flat, add Acid. If it's bitter, add Salt.
*   **Standardization:** Stick to one profile per mission. Do not cross-contaminate "Soy Sauce" with "Rosemary" unless you are a Commander-level chef.
