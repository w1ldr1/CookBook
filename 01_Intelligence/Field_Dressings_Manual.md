# INTELLIGENCE: FIELD DRESSINGS MANUAL
**Subject:** Rapid Production of Vinaigrettes and Emulsions
**Mission Objective:** To replace store-bought "Seed-Oil" dressings with high-performance house-made catalysts.

---

## 1. THE GOLDEN RATIO (3:1)
Most dressings follow a standard "Tactical Ratio":
*   **3 Units:** Oil (Extra Virgin Olive Oil or Avocado Oil).
*   **1 Unit:** Acid (Vinegar or Citrus).

## 2. DRESSING SOP: THE "BASIC STRIKE" VINAIGRETTE
1.  **Loading:** Add 1 tbsp Dijon mustard (The Emulsifier) to 1/4 cup Vinegar.
2.  **Engagement:** Whisk in 3/4 cup Oil in a slow stream until the mixture "bonds."
3.  **Seasoning:** Add Salt, Pepper, and 1 tsp Honey to balance.

## 3. DRESSING SOP: THE "BLUE CHEESE CLOUD"
*   **Supplies:** 1/2 cup Sour Cream, 1/4 cup Mayo, 1/2 cup Crumbled Blue Cheese, 1 tbsp Lemon Juice, Black Pepper.
*   **Execution:** Mash components with a fork until desired "Chunky/Smooth" ratio is achieved. Optimal for SOP-038 (Wings).

## 4. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Storage:** Store in glass jars. Vinaigrettes remain stable for 14 days; creamy dressings for 5–7 days.
*   **Field Intel:** Always shake the jar before deployment as natural separation (stratification) will occur.
