# INTELLIGENCE: THE WEEKLY BATTLE RHYTHM
**Subject:** Operational Tempo for Household Sustainment
**Mission Objective:** To eliminate "Decision Fatigue" and ensure the Unit is nourished with maximum efficiency and zero skipped meals.

---

## 1. THE STRATEGIC CYCLE
A successful Provider does not ask "What's for dinner?" at 17:00 HRS. The mission is planned in advance.

*   **SUNDAY (Logistics & Heavy Lift):** Execute a "Heavy Lift" mission (SOP-003) for the family and a "Field Ration" mission (SOP-004) to secure lunches for the work week.
*   **MON–THU (Quick Strike):** Rapid deployment of 15-20 minute meals (SOP-002) using staged pantry inventory.
*   **FRIDAY (Hostage Rescue/Rest):** High-impact entertaining or designated "Exfil" (Eating out/Social engagement).
*   **SATURDAY (Recce & Resupply):** Reconnaissance of the pantry and acquisition of fresh supplies for the following week.

---

## 2. WEEKLY OPERATIONAL GRID

| Day | Mission Type | Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monday** | Quick Strike | High protein, low cleanup after work. |
| **Tuesday** | Quick Strike | Utilize "Field Ration" leftovers for efficiency. |
| **Wednesday** | Quick Strike | Mid-week refuel. |
| **Thursday** | Quick Strike | Clean out the "Perishable" inventory. |
| **Friday** | Hostage Rescue | High-impact meal for guests or spouse. |
| **Saturday** | Logistics/Recce | Acquire fresh protein and resupply pantry. |
| **Sunday** | Heavy Lift | Multi-stage cook + Prep for coming week. |

---

## 3. LOGISTICS SOP (The Shopping Trip)
1.  **Inventory Check:** Refer to `Pantry_Inventory_SOP.md` before leaving Base.
2.  **Perimeter Sweep:** Shop the edges of the store (Meat, Produce, Dairy). Avoid the "Center Aisles" unless retrieving specific dry-good ammunition.
3.  **Bulk Acquisition:** Always buy protein in bulk. If the target (Chicken/Beef) is on sale, buy double and "Freeze for Future Ops."

---

## 4. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **The 10-Minute Rule:** Spend 10 minutes every Sunday night looking at the calendar. Identify "Late Nights" and assign "Quick Strike" SOPs to those days.
*   **Morale Check:** If the Unit (Family) is bored with the rations, rotate in a new SOP from the `03_SOP_Recipes` folder to maintain morale.
