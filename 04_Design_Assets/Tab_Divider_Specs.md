# HARDWARE SPEC: TAB DIVIDERS
**Subject:** Mylar-Reinforced Navigational Dividers
**Mission Objective:** To enable 3-second data retrieval during high-velocity kitchen engagement.

---

## 1. TECHNICAL SPECS
*   **Material:** 110lb White Index Cardstock with Matte Mylar reinforcement on the tabs.
*   **Hole Punch:** Standard 3-hole punch reinforced with clear Mylar to prevent "Ring Tear-out."
*   **Tab Position:** 5-Tab bank (staggered for visibility).

## 2. THE FIVE SECTORS (Labeling)
1.  **INTEL:** (OD Green Tab) - Nutrition, Safety, Recon.
2.  **QUICK:** (Safety Orange Tab) - Under 30m Missions.
3.  **HEAVY:** (Coyote Tan Tab) - Long-duration/Roasts.
4.  **FIELD:** (Charcoal Tab) - Meal Prep and Rations.
5.  **SUPPLY:** (Stencil White Tab) - Hardware and Maintenance.

---

## 3. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Visibility:** Use "Black Ops One" (Stencil) font for the tab text.
*   **Durability:** The tabs are the most handled part of the manual. Ensure the Mylar coating is grease-resistant.
