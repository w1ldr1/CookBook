# DESIGN SPEC: INTERNAL STEP-TAB SYSTEM
**Subject:** Engineering the 5-Bank Internal Index
**Objective:** Protect the navigational tabs within the rigid footprint of the OD Green covers.

## 1. DIMENSIONS
*   **Outer Cover:** 9.0" (W) x 5.5" (H).
*   **Internal Pages:** 8.5" (W) x 5.5" (H).
*   **The "Step" (Die-Cut):** The 0.5" difference between the cover and the page width creates the "Safety Margin" where the tabs reside.

## 2. THE 5-BANK STAGGER
Pages are die-cut on the right-hand side in a "staircase" format. Every 10 SOPs, the cut shifts down by 1 inch.
*   **Tab 1 (INTEL):** Positioned at 0.0" - 1.0" from the top.
*   **Tab 2 (QUICK):** Positioned at 1.1" - 2.0" from the top.
*   **Tab 3 (HEAVY):** Positioned at 2.1" - 3.0" from the top.
*   **Tab 4 (FIELD):** Positioned at 3.1" - 4.0" from the top.
*   **Tab 5 (SUPPLY):** Positioned at 4.1" - 5.0" from the top.

## 3. TYPOGRAPHY ON TABS
*   **Orientation:** Vertical (Top-to-Bottom).
*   **Font:** Stencilia or Roboto Mono Bold.
*   **Treatment:** Black text on Coyote Tan or Safety Orange background for maximum "Scan-ability."
