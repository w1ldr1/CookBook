# CREATIVE BRIEF: PROJECT SUSTAINMENT MANUAL
**Subject:** Visual Identity and Layout Specifications
**Objective:** Create a utilitarian, high-contrast, indestructible field guide.

## 1. THE AESTHETIC: "FUNCTION OVER FORM"
- **Tone:** Authoritative, rugged, industrial, and "Issued."
- **Reference:** 1960s-1980s U.S. Army Field Manuals (FMs).
- **Zero-Tolerance:** No script fonts, no floral patterns, no "food styling" props (napkins, garnish garnishes, or soft lighting).

## 2. COLOR & TYPOGRAPHY
- **Primary Palette:** OD Green (#4B5320), Coyote Tan (#A59C82), Charcoal (#333333).
- **Action Color:** Safety Orange (#FF6600) - use only for warnings or critical data.
- **Headlines:** Stencil-style (e.g., *Black Ops One* or *Stencilia*).
- **Instructional Body:** Monospaced (e.g., *Roboto Mono* or *Courier*). 
- **Data/Macros:** Condensed Sans-Serif (e.g., *DIN* or *Bahnschrift*).

## 3. PAGE ARCHITECTURE (The SOP Layout)
- **Header:** Every page must have the "Mission ID" (e.g., SOP-001) in a high-visibility corner.
- **Grids:** Use 2pt black borders to divide "INTELLIGENCE" from "EXECUTION."
- **Nomenclature:** All headings must use established terms: INFIL, ENGAGEMENT, EXFIL, FIELD INTEL.

## 4. PHYSICAL SPECS
- **The Chassis:** 3-Ring "Break-Back" Easel Binder.
- **The Media:** Matte-finish synthetic paper. Design must account for CMYK printing on non-absorbent surfaces.
