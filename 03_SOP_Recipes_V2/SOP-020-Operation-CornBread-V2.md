# SOP-020: OPERATION CORNBREAD
**Rank:** Recruit | **Time:** 30 Minutes | **Macros:** P: 5g | C: 28g | F: 12g | ~240 Cal
**V2.0 RIFLEMAN ISSUE // VOL 1: FOUNDATIONS**

---
## [ COLUMN 1: INTEL & SUPPLIES ]
### HARDWARE
- [ ] 1x 10" Cast Iron Skillet (The Heavy Armor)
- [ ] 1x Mixing Bowl and Whisk

### SUPPLIES
- **The Dry Payload:** 1 cup Yellow Cornmeal, 1 cup All-Purpose Flour, 1/4 cup Sugar (Secondary Objective), 1 tbsp Baking Powder, 1 tsp Salt.
- **The Liquid Catalyst:** 1 cup Buttermilk, 1 Large Egg.
- **Lubricant:** 1/2 stick (4 tbsp) Unsalted Butter.

## [ COLUMN 3: VISUAL & AAR ]
### FIELD INTEL & OBJECTIVES
*   **Field Intel:** Pre-heating the skillet with butter is non-negotiable for the "Armor" crust.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Fold in 1/2 cup of frozen corn kernels for "Internal Texture."
