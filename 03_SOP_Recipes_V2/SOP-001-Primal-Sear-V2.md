# SOP-001: OPERATION PRIMAL SEAR
**Rank:** Recruit | **Time:** 50 Minutes (Total) / 10 Minutes (Active) | **Macros:** P: 62g | C: 0g | F: 54g | ~740 Cal
**V2.0 RIFLEMAN ISSUE // VOL 1: FOUNDATIONS**

---
## [ COLUMN 1: INTEL & SUPPLIES ]
### HARDWARE
- [ ] 1x 12" Cast Iron Skillet (High thermal mass is non-negotiable)
- [ ] 1x Digital Meat Thermometer (The Rangefinder)
- [ ] 1x Stainless Steel Tongs
- [ ] 1x Tablespoon (For basting maneuvers)

### SUPPLIES
- **The Target:** 1x Ribeye Steak (1.5" thick, Choice or Prime).
- **Friction Reducer:** 2 tbsp Avocado Oil (High smoke point).
- **Seasoning:** Coarse Kosher Salt and Black Pepper.
- **Aromatics:** 2 tbsp Unsalted Butter, 2 cloves Garlic (crushed), 1 sprig Fresh Rosemary.

## [ COLUMN 3: VISUAL & AAR ]
### FIELD INTEL & OBJECTIVES
*   **Field Intel:** If your smoke alarm doesn't trigger, your thermal engagement was too low.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Slice thin against the grain to maximize tenderness.
