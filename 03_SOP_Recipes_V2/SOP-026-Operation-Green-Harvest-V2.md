# SOP-026: OPERATION GREEN HARVEST
**Rank:** Recruit | **Time:** 1 Hour 15 Minutes | **Macros:** DATA PENDING
**V2.0 RIFLEMAN ISSUE // VOL 1: FOUNDATIONS**

---
## [ COLUMN 1: INTEL & SUPPLIES ]
### HARDWARE
- [ ] 1x 9x5" Loaf Pan
- [ ] 1x Box Grater (The "Shredder")
- [ ] 2x Mixing Bowls (Dry/Wet separation)

### SUPPLIES
- **The Payload:** 1.5 cups Zucchini (grated, approx 1 large).
- **The Bulk:** 1.5 cups All-Purpose Flour, 1/2 cup Sugar, 1/2 cup Brown Sugar.
- **The Binding:** 1/2 cup Vegetable Oil, 1 Large Egg, 1 tsp Vanilla.
- **Ordnance:** 1/2 tsp Baking Soda, 1/2 tsp Baking Powder, 1.5 tsp Cinnamon, 1/2 tsp Salt.

## [ COLUMN 3: VISUAL & AAR ]
### FIELD INTEL & OBJECTIVES

*   **Secondary Objective:** Add 1/2 cup dark chocolate chips for "Morale Support."
