# SOP-013: OPERATION TEXAS BLACK-GOLD
**Rank:** Commander | **Time:** 12–16 Hours (Endurance) | **Macros:** P: 42g | C: 0g | F: 28g | ~420 Cal
**V2.0 RIFLEMAN ISSUE // VOL 1: FOUNDATIONS**

## [ COLUMN 1: INTEL & SUPPLIES ]
### HARDWARE
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Offset Smoker or Pellet Grill
- [ ] 1x Heavy Duty Pink Butcher Paper (Secondary Objective: Foil)
- [ ] 1x Dual-Probe Remote Thermometer (The "Sentry")
- [ ] 1x 12-inch Serrated Slicing Blade

### SUPPLIES
*Quantities optimized for 10-12 Personnel:*
- **The Target:** 12–15 lb Whole "Packer" Brisket (Choice or Prime).
- **The Rub:** 1/2 cup Coarse 16-mesh Black Pepper, 1/2 cup Kosher Salt (The "Dalmatian Rub").
- **Fuel:** Post Oak or Hickory wood chunks.
- **Moisture Catalyst:** 1 cup Water/Apple Cider Vinegar (50/50 mix) in a spray bottle.

## [ COLUMN 3: VISUAL & AAR ]
### FIELD INTEL & OBJECTIVES
**Field Intel:**
*   **The Stall:** Do not panic when the internal temp stops rising at 160°F. This is "Evaporative Cooling." Wrap the asset to push through to the finish.
*   **Probe Feel:** The thermometer should feel like it is sliding into "Room Temperature Butter." If there is resistance, the mission is not complete.
**Secondary Objective:**
*   **Tallow Recovery:** Save the fat trimmings from Phase 1, melt them down in a separate pan, and pour the liquid "Gold" over the brisket during the wrap phase.
