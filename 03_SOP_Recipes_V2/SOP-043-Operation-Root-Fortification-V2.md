# SOP-043: OPERATION ROOT FORTIFICATION
**Rank:** Recruit | **Time:** 35 Minutes | **Macros:** P: 2g | C: 22g | F: 7g
**V2.0 RIFLEMAN ISSUE // VOL 1: FOUNDATIONS**

---
## [ COLUMN 1: INTEL & SUPPLIES ]
### HARDWARE
- [ ] 1x Half-Sheet Pan
- [ ] 1x Large Mixing Bowl

### SUPPLIES
*   **The Target:** 1 lb Carrots (peeled and sliced into "diagonal batons") or Baby Potatoes (halved).
*   **Lubricant:** 2 tbsp Olive Oil.
*   **Flavor Ordnance:** 1 tbsp Honey or Maple Syrup, 1 tsp Salt, 1/2 tsp Cumin or Dried Thyme.

## [ COLUMN 3: VISUAL & AAR ]
### FIELD INTEL & OBJECTIVES
*   **Field Intel:** The diagonal cut on the carrots increases "Surface Area," allowing for more caramelization (flavor) in less time.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Top with fresh parsley or cilantro for a "High-Contrast" finish.
