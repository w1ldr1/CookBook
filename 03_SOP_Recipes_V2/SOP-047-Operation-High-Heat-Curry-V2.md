# SOP-047: OPERATION HIGH-HEAT CURRY
**Rank:** Recruit | **Time:** 20 Minutes | **Macros:** P: 32g | C: 12g | F: 24g (excluding rice)
**V2.0 RIFLEMAN ISSUE // VOL 1: FOUNDATIONS**

---
## [ COLUMN 1: INTEL & SUPPLIES ]
### HARDWARE
- [ ] 1x 12" Skillet or Wok
- [ ] 1x Wooden Spoon

### SUPPLIES
*   **The Protein:** 1 lb Chicken Breast or Shrimp.
*   **The Catalyst:** 2 tbsp Red or Green Curry Paste (Thai Kitchen or similar).
*   **Hydration:** 1 can (14oz) Full-fat Coconut Milk.
*   **The Payload:** 1 cup Frozen Bamboo Shoots or Snap Peas, 1 Red Bell Pepper (sliced).
*   **Secondary Objective:** 1 tbsp Fish Sauce, 1 tsp Sugar, Lime wedges.

---

---
## [ COLUMN 3: VISUAL & AAR ]
### FIELD INTEL & OBJECTIVES
*   **Field Intel:** Do not boil the coconut milk aggressively; a gentle simmer prevents the fat from separating.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Garnish with fresh Basil or Cilantro.
