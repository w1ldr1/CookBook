# SOP-035: OPERATION RED SAUCE
**Rank:** Recruit (Basic Maneuver) | **Time:** 20 Minutes | **Macros:** P: 28g | C: 55g | F: 12g
**V2.0 RIFLEMAN ISSUE // VOL 1: FOUNDATIONS**

---
## [ COLUMN 1: INTEL & SUPPLIES ]
### HARDWARE
- [ ] 1x Large Pasta Pot
- [ ] 1x 12" Skillet (The Sauce Bay)
- [ ] 1x Colander

### SUPPLIES
- **The Vehicle:** 1 lb Spaghetti or Penne.
- **The Catalyst:** 1 jar (24oz) High-Quality Marinara (or 1 can crushed tomatoes).
- **Reinforcements:** 1 lb Ground Beef or Italian Sausage.
- **Aromatics:** 1 Onion (diced), 3 cloves Garlic (minced), 1 tsp Dried Oregano.
