# SOP-038: OPERATION AERIAL ASSAULT
**Rank:** Sergeant (Requires heat management) | **Time:** 35 Minutes | **Macros:** P: 45g | C: 0g | F: 32g | ~470 Cal
**V2.0 RIFLEMAN ISSUE // VOL 1: FOUNDATIONS**

---
## [ COLUMN 1: INTEL & SUPPLIES ]
### HARDWARE
- [ ] 1x Baking Sheet with Wire Rack (Essential for 360-degree airflow)
- [ ] 1x Large Stainless Steel Mixing Bowl
- [ ] 1x Pair of Tongs

### SUPPLIES
*   **The Target:** 2 lbs Chicken Wings (Split into flats and drums).
*   **The Armor (Dry Rub):** 1 tsp Kosher Salt, 1 tsp Baking Powder (**CRITICAL: Ensures skin crispness**), 1 tsp Garlic Powder.
*   **The Catalyst (Sauce):** 1/3 cup Frank’s RedHot (or similar), 4 tbsp Unsalted Butter (melted).

## [ COLUMN 3: VISUAL & AAR ]
### FIELD INTEL & OBJECTIVES
*   **Field Intel:** Do not use "Baking Soda" by mistake. It will ruin the taste profile. Baking Powder is the only approved chemical catalyst for this mission.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Serve with celery sticks and blue cheese to neutralize the heat.
