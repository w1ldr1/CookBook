# SOP-031: OPERATION RACK-AND-FILE
**Rank:** Commander | **Time:** 6 Hours | **Macros:** P: 48g | C: 12g | F: 38g | ~580 Cal
**V2.0 RIFLEMAN ISSUE // VOL 1: FOUNDATIONS**

---
## [ COLUMN 1: INTEL & SUPPLIES ]
### HARDWARE
- [ ] 1x Smoker or Grill (Indirect Setup)
- [ ] 1x Heavy Duty Aluminum Foil (The "Shield")
- [ ] 1x Basting Brush

### SUPPLIES
- **The Target:** 2 Racks Baby Back Ribs.
- **The Rub:** 1/4 cup Brown Sugar, 1 tbsp Smoked Paprika, 1 tbsp Black Pepper, 1 tbsp Salt, 1 tsp Garlic Powder, 1 tsp Onion Powder.
- **The Wrap Payload:** 4 tbsp Butter (cubed), 1/4 cup Honey or Brown Sugar, 1/4 cup Apple Juice.
- **The Finish:** 1 cup BBQ Sauce.

## [ COLUMN 3: VISUAL & AAR ]
### FIELD INTEL & OBJECTIVES
*   **Field Intel:** "Fall-off-the-bone" is a preference, but a perfect rib should have a clean "bite-through" without the rest of the meat sliding off.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Use apple cider vinegar spray during the first 3 hours to "Develop the Bark."
