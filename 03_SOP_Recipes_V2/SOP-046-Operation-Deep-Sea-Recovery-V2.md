# SOP-046: OPERATION DEEP-SEA RECOVERY
**Rank:** Sergeant | **Time:** 20 Minutes | **Macros:** P: 28g | C: 52g | F: 12g
**V2.0 RIFLEMAN ISSUE // VOL 1: FOUNDATIONS**

---
## [ COLUMN 1: INTEL & SUPPLIES ]
### HARDWARE
- [ ] 1x Large Pasta Pot
- [ ] 1x 12" Skillet with a TIGHT FITTING lid
- [ ] 1x Tongs

### SUPPLIES
*   **The Target:** 2 lbs Fresh Manila Clams or Littleneck Clams (scrubbed).
*   **The Vehicle:** 1/2 lb Linguine.
*   **The Catalyst:** 1/2 cup Dry White Wine (Sauvignon Blanc), 3 tbsp Olive Oil, 4 cloves Garlic (sliced thin), 1/2 tsp Red Pepper Flakes.
*   **The Finish:** 1/4 cup Fresh Parsley (chopped), 1 tbsp Cold Butter.

## [ COLUMN 3: VISUAL & AAR ]
### FIELD INTEL & OBJECTIVES
*   **Field Intel:** The starch in the pasta water is the "Glue" that binds the wine/oil/butter into a sauce.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Serve with a piece of SOP-019 (No-Knead Bread) to "Mop up" the remaining sauce.
