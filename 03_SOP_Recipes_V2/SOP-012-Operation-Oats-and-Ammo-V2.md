# SOP-012: OPERATION OATS AND AMMO
**Rank:** Recruit | **Time:** 5 Minutes (Active) + 6 Hours (Passive) | **Macros:** P: 30g | C: 45g | F: 12g | ~420 Cal
**V2.0 RIFLEMAN ISSUE // VOL 1: FOUNDATIONS**

---
## [ COLUMN 1: INTEL & SUPPLIES ]
### HARDWARE
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 2-4x Mason Jars (16oz) or airtight glass containers.
- [ ] 1x Mixing Spoon.

### SUPPLIES
*Quantities optimized for 1 Ration (Repeat for batching):*
- **The Base:** 1/2 cup Rolled Oats (not instant).
- **Liquid Ammunition:** 1/2 cup Milk (Dairy or Nut-based).
- **Protein Reinforcement:** 1 scoop Vanilla or Chocolate Protein Powder.
- **The Fuel:** 1 tbsp Chia Seeds (essential for texture) and 1 tbsp Peanut Butter.
- **Finish:** 1/2 cup Frozen Berries.

## [ COLUMN 3: VISUAL & AAR ]
### FIELD INTEL & OBJECTIVES
**Field Intel:**
*   **Chia Hydrodynamics:** Chia seeds are the primary thickening agent. Without them, the rations will remain liquid and fail the "Oat-to-Liquid" ratio.
*   **Engagement Window:** These remain viable for 96 hours. Batch-prep 4 jars on Sunday for work-week sustainment.
**Secondary Objective:**
*   **Macro Boost:** Top with 1 tbsp of chopped walnuts or almonds immediately before consumption for added healthy fats and "Tactical Crunch."
