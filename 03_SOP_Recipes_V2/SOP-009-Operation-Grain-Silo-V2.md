# SOP-009: OPERATION GRAIN SILO
**Rank:** Recruit | **Time:** 25 Minutes | **Macros:** P: 5g | C: 45g | F: 1g | ~210 Cal
**V2.0 RIFLEMAN ISSUE // VOL 1: FOUNDATIONS**

---
## [ COLUMN 1: INTEL & SUPPLIES ]
### HARDWARE
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Heavy-bottomed Pot with a tight-fitting lid
- [ ] 1x Fine Mesh Strainer (The "Filter")
- [ ] 1x Fork (The "Aerator")

### SUPPLIES
*Quantities optimized for 6 Rations:*
- **The Target:** 2 cups Jasmine or Basmati Rice.
- **Liquid Ammunition:** 3 cups Water or Chicken Stock (for enhanced flavor).
- **Catalyst:** 1 tsp Kosher Salt, 1 tsp Butter or Oil (prevents foaming).

## [ COLUMN 3: VISUAL & AAR ]
### FIELD INTEL & OBJECTIVES
**Field Intel:**
*   **Starch Neutralization:** Rinsing the rice until the water runs clear is mandatory to prevent "Sticky Rations."
*   **Vapor Lock:** Do not lift the lid during the dwell. Breaking the vapor seal results in uneven hydration and mission failure.
**Secondary Objective:**
*   **Aromatic Infusion:** Add 1 smashed garlic clove and 1 bay leaf to the water during the boil phase for enhanced fragrance.
