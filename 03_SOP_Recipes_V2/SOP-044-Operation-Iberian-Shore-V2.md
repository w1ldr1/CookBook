# SOP-044: OPERATION IBERIAN SHORE
**Rank:** Commander (Requires advanced thermal management) | **Time:** 60 Minutes | **Macros:** P: 32g | C: 58g | F: 14g | ~510 Cal
**V2.0 RIFLEMAN ISSUE // VOL 1: FOUNDATIONS**

---
## [ COLUMN 1: INTEL & SUPPLIES ]
### HARDWARE
- [ ] 1x 15" Paella Pan or the widest Stainless Steel skillet available (Surface area is critical).
- [ ] 1x Wooden Spoon (The "Distributor").
- [ ] 1x Foil (The "Shield").

### SUPPLIES
*   **The Grain:** 2 cups Bomba or Calasparra Rice (Short-grain is mandatory).
*   **The Protein:** 1 lb Shrimp (peeled), 1/2 lb Spanish Chorizo (sliced), 1/2 lb Chicken Thighs (cubed).
*   **The Liquid Payload:** 4 cups Seafood or Chicken Stock, 1 pinch Saffron threads (The Catalyst).
*   **The Trinity:** 1 Onion (diced), 1 Red Bell Pepper (sliced), 3 cloves Garlic (minced).
*   **The Finish:** 1/2 cup Frozen Peas, 1 Lemon (quartered), Fresh Parsley.

## [ COLUMN 3: VISUAL & AAR ]
### FIELD INTEL & OBJECTIVES
*   **Field Intel:** The "Socarrat" is the highest-value asset in this mission. It should be a thin, crispy, dark brown layer at the base of the pan.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Garnish with lemon quarters and parsley before serving. Serve directly from the pan for "Command Level" presentation.
