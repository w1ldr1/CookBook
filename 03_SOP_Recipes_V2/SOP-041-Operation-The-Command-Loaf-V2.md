# SOP-041: OPERATION THE COMMAND LOAF
**Rank:** Sergeant (Requires textural management) | **Time:** 1 Hour 15 Minutes | **Macros:** P: 38g | C: 18g | F: 22g | ~420 Cal
**V2.0 RIFLEMAN ISSUE // VOL 1: FOUNDATIONS**

---
## [ COLUMN 1: INTEL & SUPPLIES ]
### HARDWARE
- [ ] 1x 9x5" Loaf Pan or Rimmed Baking Sheet
- [ ] 1x Large Mixing Bowl
- [ ] 1x Small Bowl (for the glaze)

### SUPPLIES
*   **The Chassis:** 2 lbs Ground Beef (80/20) or a "Meatloaf Mix" (Beef/Pork/Veal).
*   **Binding Agents:** 2 Eggs, 3/4 cup Breadcrumbs (SOP-019 crumbs preferred), 1/2 cup Milk.
*   **Aromatics:** 1 small Onion (grated), 2 cloves Garlic (minced).
*   **The Glaze (Top Cover):** 1/2 cup Ketchup, 2 tbsp Brown Sugar, 1 tbsp Dijon Mustard.
*   **Ordnance:** 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce, 1 tsp Salt, 1 tsp Black Pepper, 1 tsp Dried Thyme.

## [ COLUMN 3: VISUAL & AAR ]
### FIELD INTEL & OBJECTIVES
*   **Field Intel:** Grating the onion instead of dicing ensures it "disappears" into the loaf, providing moisture without large chunks.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Slice cold leftovers for "Command Sandwiches" on toasted SOP-019 bread.
