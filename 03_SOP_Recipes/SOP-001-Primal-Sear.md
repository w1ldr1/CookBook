# SOP-001: OPERATION PRIMAL SEAR
**Objective:** Execution of a perfect medium-rare Ribeye using high-thermal mass hardware.
...

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 50 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 40 Minutes (includes tempering time)
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 8–10 Minutes
*   **Target Macros:** P: 62g | C: 0g | F: 54g | ~740 Cal (per 12oz serving)
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 2/5 (Requires precise timing)

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 12" Cast Iron Skillet (High thermal mass is non-negotiable)
- [ ] 1x Digital Instant-Read Thermometer (The "Rangefinder")
- [ ] 1x Stainless Steel Tongs (Heavy duty)
- [ ] 1x Tablespoon (For basting maneuvers)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 2 Personnel:*
- **The Target:** 2x Ribeye Steaks (1.5" thick, Choice or Prime grade)
- **Friction Reducer:** 2 tbsp Avocado Oil or Grapeseed Oil (High smoke point)
- **Seasoning:** Coarse Kosher Salt and Cracked Black Pepper
- **Aromatics:** 3 tbsp Unsalted Butter, 3 cloves Garlic (crushed), 2 sprigs Fresh Rosemary

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Preparation)
1.  **Tempering:** Remove steaks from refrigeration 40 minutes prior to engagement. Cold meat causes thermal shock and uneven cooking.
2.  **Surface Prep:** Pat steaks bone-dry with paper towels. Moisture is the enemy of the sear (it creates steam).
3.  **Applying Ordinance:** Liberally coat all surfaces (including the sides) with Kosher salt and pepper. 

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Cook)
1.  **Heat Extraction:** Place cast iron on high heat. Add oil. Wait until the oil reaches "smoke point" (wisps of white smoke appearing).
2.  **The Initial Sear:** Lay steaks into the skillet away from your body to prevent hot oil splash-back. Press down slightly to ensure maximum surface contact.
3.  **The Flip:** Sear for 2–3 minutes per side until a dark, mahogany "crust" (Maillard reaction) is formed.
4.  **Aromatic Infusion:** Reduce heat to medium. Add butter, garlic, and rosemary. 
5.  **Basting Maneuver:** Tilt the skillet slightly so the melting butter pools at the bottom with the aromatics. Use the spoon to continuously pour that hot, flavored butter over the steaks for the final 2 minutes of cooking.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Target Verification:** Insert thermometer into the thickest part of the steak.
    *   *Pull at 125°F (52°C) for Medium-Rare.*
2.  **Resting Period:** Remove steaks to a cutting board. **DO NOT SLICE.** Let the "Target" rest for 10 minutes. This allows muscle fibers to relax and redistribute internal fluids.
3.  **Hardware Maintenance:** While the pan is still warm, rinse with hot water and a chainmail scrubber. Dry immediately and apply a light coat of oil to prevent oxidation (rust).

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)

*   **Field Note 01:** If the smoke alarm doesn't go off, your pan wasn't hot enough. Open windows and deactivate sensors prior to engagement.
*   **Field Note 02:** "Carry-over cooking" will raise the internal temperature by about 5°F while the meat rests. Always pull the meat 5 degrees before your target temperature.
*   **Leftover Utility:** Slice thin against the grain for "SOP-012: Tactical Steak Salads" the following day.
