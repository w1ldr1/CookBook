# SOP-042: OPERATION IRON-SEARED THIGHS
**Objective:** Execution of bone-in chicken thighs with high-decibel crispy skin.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike (High-Efficiency Protein)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 35 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 25–30 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per 2 thighs):** P: 42g | C: 0g | F: 32g | ~460 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-032 (Mashed Potatoes), SOP-034 (Green Perimeter), SOP-043 (Root Fortification), SOP-033 (Pan Sauce).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x 12" Cast Iron Skillet
- [ ] 1x Tongs
- [ ] 1x Digital Meat Thermometer (The Rangefinder)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*   **The Target:** 4–6 Bone-in, Skin-on Chicken Thighs.
*   **Lubricant:** 1 tbsp Avocado Oil.
*   **Seasoning:** Kosher Salt, Black Pepper, Smoked Paprika.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Surface Prep)
1.  **Moisture Extraction:** Pat skin bone-dry. 
2.  **Seasoning:** Season both sides aggressively.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Cold Start Maneuver)
1.  **The Cold Start:** Place thighs skin-side DOWN in a **cold** cast iron skillet. 
2.  **Thermal Engagement:** Turn heat to Medium-High. As the pan heats, the chicken fat renders slowly, creating a superior "Armor" (crust) without burning.
3.  **Hold Position:** Cook for 12–15 minutes without moving. Use a weight (or another skillet) on top for maximum surface contact.
4.  **The Flip:** Once the skin is deep golden and releases easily, flip the thighs.

### Phase 3: Extraction (Thermal Finish)
1.  **Dwell:** Cook for 10 more minutes on the flesh side. 
2.  **Target Verification:** Pull when the thickest part hits **175°F (79°C)**. 
    *   *Field Intel:* Unlike breasts, thighs are better at higher temps (175-185°F) as it breaks down the connective tissue.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Secondary Objective:** Use the rendered fat in the pan to sauté SOP-034 (Green Perimeter) immediately after extraction.
*   **Field Intel:** The "Cold Start" method is the most reliable way to prevent sticking and ensure even rendering.
