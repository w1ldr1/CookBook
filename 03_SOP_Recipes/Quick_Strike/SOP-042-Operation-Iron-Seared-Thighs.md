# SOP-042: OPERATION IRON-SEARED THIGHS
**Objective:** Execution of bone-in chicken thighs with high-decibel crispy skin via cold-start cast iron method.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Difficulty:** 1/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 35 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 5 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 25–30 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per 2 thighs):** P: 42g | C: 0g | F: 32g | ~460 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-032 (Starch Support), SOP-034 (Green Perimeter), SOP-043 (Root Fortification), SOP-033 (Force Multiplier).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 12" Cast Iron Skillet
- [ ] 1x Tongs
- [ ] 1x Digital Meat Thermometer (The Rangefinder)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 2–3 Personnel:*
- **The Target:** 4–6 Bone-in, Skin-on Chicken Thighs
- **Lubricant:** 1 tbsp Avocado Oil
- **Seasoning:** 1 tsp Kosher Salt, 1/2 tsp Black Pepper, 1 tsp Smoked Paprika

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Surface Prep)
1.  **Moisture Extraction:** Pat the skin side completely bone-dry with paper towels. Any moisture on the skin surface will steam instead of sear.
2.  **Seasoning:** Season both sides aggressively with salt, pepper, and smoked paprika.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Cold Start Maneuver)
1.  **The Cold Start:** Place thighs skin-side DOWN in a **cold** cast iron skillet. Add oil around the thighs, not over them.
2.  **Thermal Ramp:** Turn heat to Medium-High. As the pan heats slowly, the chicken fat renders gradually — this produces a superior, even "Armor" without burning.
3.  **Hold Position:** Cook for 12–15 minutes without moving. Apply gentle downward pressure with tongs or a second skillet for maximum skin contact.
4.  **The Flip:** Once the skin is deep golden and releases cleanly from the pan without sticking, flip the thighs.
5.  **Flesh-Side Finish:** Cook for 10 more minutes on the flesh side.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Target Verification:** Pull when internal temp at the thickest part reads **175°F (79°C)**. Unlike breast meat, thigh meat improves at higher temperatures as connective tissue breaks down into gelatin.
2.  **Rest:** Allow to rest 5 minutes before plating.
3.  **Rendered Fat:** Leave the rendered chicken fat in the pan and immediately deploy SOP-034 (Green Perimeter) vegetables into it — zero waste.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   The "Cold Start" method is the most reliable technique for perfect skin. It prevents sticking, ensures even fat rendering, and requires no oil temperature guesswork.
*   175°F is the correct internal target for dark meat — not 165°F. At 165°F, the connective tissue has not yet converted and the texture will be tough and chewy.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   After flipping, add 2 crushed garlic cloves and 3 sprigs of fresh thyme to the pan. Baste the skin side with the rendered fat every 2 minutes during the flesh-side cook.
