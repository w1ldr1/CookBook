# SOP-039: OPERATION MORNING STACK
**Objective:** Deployment of high-morale Buttermilk Pancakes for Unit sustainment.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Difficulty:** 1/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 20 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 5 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 15 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per 3 pancakes):** P: 8g | C: 42g | F: 10g | ~290 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-022 (Whiteout Gravy side-op).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Non-Stick Griddle or 12" Skillet
- [ ] 1x Large Whisk
- [ ] 1x Wide Spatula (The Flipper)
- [ ] 2x Mixing Bowls

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 4 Personnel (~12 pancakes):*
- **The Dry Bulk:** 2 cups All-Purpose Flour, 2 tbsp Sugar, 2 tsp Baking Powder, 1 tsp Baking Soda, 1 tsp Salt
- **The Liquid:** 2 cups Buttermilk, 2 Large Eggs, 4 tbsp Unsalted Butter (melted)

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Mix)
1.  **Dry Build:** Whisk all dry supplies together in Bowl A.
2.  **Wet Build:** Whisk buttermilk, eggs, and melted butter together in Bowl B.
3.  **Merge:** Pour wet into dry. Stir until *just* combined. Lumps are acceptable. Over-mixing develops gluten and produces "Rubber Rations."

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Griddle)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Heat griddle to Medium. Verify readiness with a single water drop — it should dance and evaporate immediately.
2.  **Deployment:** Pour 1/3 cup of batter per pancake onto the surface.
3.  **The Signal:** Wait for bubbles to form across the surface and the edges to look dry and matte.
4.  **The Flip:** Execute one clean flip. Cook for 60–90 seconds on the reverse side until the bottom is golden.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Staging:** Keep finished pancakes warm on a plate in the oven at 200°F while the remaining batter is deployed.
2.  **Deployment:** Stack 3 per person. Apply butter immediately while hot to allow full absorption before the syrup is applied.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   Emergency Buttermilk Substitute: add 2 tbsp of lemon juice to 2 cups of regular milk. Let sit for 5 minutes. This produces the full 2 cups required — the acid curdles the milk and replicates buttermilk's leavening reaction. Using only 1 cup produces batter that is 50% too thick.
*   Never flip twice. Pancakes that are flipped multiple times lose their rise and develop a dense, compressed texture.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Fold 1/2 cup of blueberries or chocolate chips into the batter immediately before griddle deployment for embedded payload pancakes.
