# SOP-039: OPERATION MORNING STACK
**Objective:** Deployment of high-morale Buttermilk Pancakes for Unit sustainment.
**Rank:** Recruit (Basic Maneuver)
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike (Family Ops)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 20 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 5 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 10 Minutes
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-022 (Sausage and Gravy side-op).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x Non-Stick Griddle or 12" Skillet
- [ ] 1x Large Whisk
- [ ] 1x Wide Spatula (The Flipper)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*   **The Bulk:** 2 cups All-Purpose Flour, 2 tbsp Sugar, 2 tsp Baking Powder, 1 tsp Baking Soda, 1 tsp Salt.
*   **The Liquid:** 2 cups Buttermilk, 2 Large Eggs, 4 tbsp Butter (melted).

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Mix)
1.  **Dry/Wet Integration:** Whisk dry supplies in one bowl. Whisk buttermilk, eggs, and butter in another.
2.  **Merge:** Pour wet into dry. Stir until *just* combined. **FIELD WARNING:** Lumps are acceptable. Over-mixing leads to "Rubber Rations."

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Griddle)
1.  **Thermal Prep:** Heat griddle to Medium. Test with a drop of water; it should "dance."
2.  **Deployment:** Pour 1/3 cup of batter per pancake. 
3.  **The Signal:** Wait for bubbles to form on the surface and the edges to look "dry."
4.  **The Flip:** Execute a clean 180-degree flip. Cook for 60–90 seconds on the reverse side.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** If you lack Buttermilk, add 1 tbsp of Lemon Juice to 1 cup of regular milk and let sit for 5 minutes (Emergency Substitution).
*   **Secondary Objective:** Top with real Maple Syrup. High-fructose "table syrup" is considered a failure in the Provider's kitchen.
