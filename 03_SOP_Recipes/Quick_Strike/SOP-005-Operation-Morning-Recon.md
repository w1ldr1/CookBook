# SOP-005: OPERATION MORNING RECON
**Objective:** High-speed, high-protein front-loading of energy to initiate the daily mission.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike (Breakfast Ops)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 10 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 3 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 5 Minutes
*   **Target Macros:** P: 32g | C: 4g | F: 28g | ~400 Cal (Based on 4 Eggs + Spinach)
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5 (Basic Maneuvers)
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-021 (Biscuits).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 10" Non-Stick Skillet or seasoned Carbon Steel
- [ ] 1x Silicone Spatula (High-heat resistant)
- [ ] 1x Small Mixing Bowl

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 1 Personnel:*
- **The Payload:** 3-4 Large Eggs.
- **Reinforcements:** 1/4 cup Shredded Cheese (Cheddar or Monterey Jack).
- **The Fiber:** 1 handful Fresh Baby Spinach.
- **Lubricant:** 1 tbsp Unsalted Butter or Ghee.
- **Seasoning:** Kosher Salt, Black Pepper, Red Pepper Flakes (optional).

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Preparation)
1.  **Ordnance Prep:** Crack eggs into the mixing bowl. Whisk aggressively until no "streaks" remain and the mixture is aerated.
2.  **Staging:** Have your spinach and cheese within arm's reach of the heat source.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Cook)
1.  **Thermal Contact:** Place skillet over medium heat. Add butter. Wait for the "Sizzle Signal" (butter stops foaming).
2.  **Deployment:** Pour egg mixture into the center of the pan. 
3.  **Agitation:** Using the spatula, continuously move the eggs from the edges to the center for the first 60 seconds. This creates "Large Curd" texture and prevents burning.
4.  **Final Loading:** When eggs are 80% set (still slightly wet), kill the heat. Add the spinach and cheese. 
5.  **Fold Maneuver:** Fold the eggs over the fillings. The residual thermal energy of the eggs will wilt the spinach and melt the cheese without overcooking the protein.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Plating:** Slide the scramble directly onto a warmed plate.
2.  **Sanitation:** Wipe the non-stick pan with a paper towel immediately. If using carbon steel, rinse with hot water and dry.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)

**Field Intel:**
*   **Thermal Discipline:** Never cook eggs on High heat. High heat causes "Protein Tightening," resulting in a rubbery, sulfurous texture. If the pan begins to smoke, you have lost thermal control—remove from heat immediately.
*   **Textural Aeration:** Whisking eggs vigorously for 30 seconds prior to deployment incorporates air, resulting in a significantly higher-volume scramble. Adding 1 tsp of water or heavy cream increases the "steam-loft" during the cook.
*   **Residual Heat Maneuver:** Kill the heat when the eggs look 80% finished (slightly wet). The residual thermal energy of the pan will complete the cook during the "Exfil" phase, preventing dry, overcooked rations.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   **The Southwest Maneuver:** After the fold, top with 2 tbsp of salsa and 1/4 sliced avocado. This increases the fat and micronutrient payload, transforming a basic morning refuel into a high-octane "Hostage Rescue" level breakfast.
