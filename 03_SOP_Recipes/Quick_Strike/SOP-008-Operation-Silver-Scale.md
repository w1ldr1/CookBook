# SOP-008: OPERATION SILVER SCALE
**Objective:** Precision execution of skin-on salmon for high-omega fuel.
**Rank:** Sergeant
**Difficulty:** 2/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 15 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 5 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 8–10 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per 6oz):** P: 34g | C: 0g | F: 18g | ~320 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 2/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-034 (Green Perimeter).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Non-Stick Skillet or Stainless Steel Pan
- [ ] 1x Fish Spatula (Thin, flexible metal)
- [ ] 1x Paper Towels (Critical for moisture extraction)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 2 Personnel:*
- **The Target:** 2x Salmon Fillets (6oz each), skin-on
- **Lubricant:** 1 tbsp Avocado Oil (High smoke point)
- **Seasoning:** Kosher Salt, Black Pepper
- **Finishing Catalyst:** 1/2 Lemon, 1 tbsp Unsalted Butter

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Preparation)
1.  **Moisture Extraction:** Pat the skin side of the salmon bone-dry. Any moisture will cause the fish to stick and prevent a "Crispy Armor" finish.
2.  **Seasoning:** Apply salt and pepper to the flesh side only. Season the skin side immediately before hitting the pan.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Sear)
1.  **Thermal Contact:** Heat pan to Medium-High. Add oil.
2.  **Deployment:** Lay fish skin-side down. Press firmly with the spatula for 10 seconds to prevent the skin from curling.
3.  **Hold Position:** Do not move the fish. After 4 minutes, gently test the skin with your spatula — if there is any resistance, wait 60 seconds and test again. The skin will release cleanly once the crust has fully formed (~5–6 minutes total). Do not force it. When the cooked color reaches 75% of the way up the side of the fillet, execute the flip.
4.  **The Flip:** Turn the fish over. Add the butter and a squeeze of lemon.
5.  **Final Burn:** Cook for only 1–2 minutes on the flesh side. Target internal temp: **130°F (54°C)** for Medium (translucent center).

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Extraction:** Remove from pan and rest on a plate skin-side UP to maintain crispness.
2.  **Sanitation:** Wipe pan while warm.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   Test gently after 4 minutes — if the spatula meets resistance, the crust is not set. Wait 60 seconds and test again. Do not pry. Forcing a stuck fillet destroys the skin and the mission. Patience is the technique.
*   Use the "Primary Blade" to scrape the skin side before cooking to remove microscopic moisture — this ensures a high-decibel crunch.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Add 1 tbsp of butter and a sprig of thyme during the final 2 minutes of the flesh-side sear to increase the fat-soluble flavor profile.
