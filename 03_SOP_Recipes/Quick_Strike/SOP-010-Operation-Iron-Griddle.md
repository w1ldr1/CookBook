# SOP-010: OPERATION IRON GRIDDLE
**Objective:** Execution of high-surface-area "Smash Burgers" for maximum Maillard reaction crust.
**Rank:** Sergeant
**Difficulty:** 2/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 15 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 5 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 4 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per 2 patties):** P: 42g | C: 2g | F: 34g | ~480 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 2/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-043 (Root Fortification).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 12" Cast Iron Skillet
- [ ] 1x Heavy-Duty Metal Spatula (No slots preferred)
- [ ] 1x "Smash Tool" (A second spatula or a heavy weighted press)
- [ ] Parchment Paper squares (4x4 inch)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 2 Personnel:*
- **The Target:** 1 lb Ground Beef (80/20 fat ratio is mandatory for crust)
- **Reinforcements:** 4 slices American or Sharp Cheddar cheese
- **The Vehicle:** 2 Brioche or Potato buns
- **Seasoning:** Kosher Salt, Black Pepper
- **Lubricant:** 1 tsp Unsalted Butter (for buns)

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Preparation)
1.  **Ordnance Formation:** Divide meat into 4 loosely packed balls (~4oz each). Do **NOT** form patties yet. Keep them cold until the second they hit the pan.
2.  **Bun Prep:** Butter the cut side of the buns.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Smash)
1.  **Thermal Contact:** Heat skillet to High until it wisps smoke.
2.  **The Toast:** Place buns face down for 30 seconds until golden. Remove and stage on plates.
3.  **The Smash:** Place two meat balls in the skillet. Place a parchment square over a ball and **apply maximum downward force** with the spatula/press until the patty is paper-thin and jagged at the edges. Repeat.
4.  **Seasoning:** Season the "wet" side liberally with salt and pepper.
5.  **The Flip:** Cook for 2 minutes until edges are dark brown/black. Scrape *under* the crust to flip.
6.  **Armor Application:** Immediately add cheese. Stack one patty on top of the other to melt the cheese faster.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Plating:** Slide the double-stack onto the toasted bun.
2.  **Sanitation:** Scrape the skillet immediately while hot to remove carbonized protein.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   80/20 beef is the standard. Anything leaner lacks the lubricant required for a "lacy" edge sear.
*   Keep the meat balls in refrigeration until the exact moment of deployment to ensure maximum fat retention during the smash.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   **The Oklahoma Maneuver:** Place thinly sliced onions directly on top of the raw meat ball before smashing. The onions will steam and char into the patty during the sear.
