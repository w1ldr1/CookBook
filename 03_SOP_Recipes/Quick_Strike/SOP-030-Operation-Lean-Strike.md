# SOP-030: OPERATION LEAN STRIKE
**Objective:** Precision sear and thermal finish of a Pork Tenderloin for lean, high-protein output.
**Rank:** Sergeant
**Difficulty:** 2/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 25 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 5 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 18–20 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per 6oz):** P: 36g | C: 0g | F: 6g | ~210 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 2/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-043 (Root Fortification).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 12" Cast Iron or Oven-Safe Skillet
- [ ] 1x Digital Meat Thermometer (The Rangefinder)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 2–3 Personnel:*
- **The Target:** 1 Pork Tenderloin (approx 1 lb)
- **The Rub:** 1 tsp Kosher Salt, 1 tsp Black Pepper, 1 tsp Garlic Powder, 1 tsp Dried Thyme
- **Lubricant:** 2 tbsp Avocado Oil

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Preparation)
1.  **Standardize:** Remove the "silver skin" — the thin, pearlescent membrane running along the side. Slide a boning knife under it and strip it off. Silver skin does not break down during cooking and will tighten and toughen.
2.  **Coat:** Apply the rub aggressively on all surfaces.
3.  **Thermal Calibration:** Preheat oven to **400°F (204°C)**.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Sear & Roast)
1.  **Heat:** Get skillet screaming hot with oil over High heat.
2.  **Sear:** Brown the tenderloin for 2 minutes per side, rotating through all 4 sides (8 minutes total).
3.  **Finish:** Move the skillet directly into the preheated oven. Roast for 10–12 minutes.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Pull Point:** Remove when internal temperature reads **140°F (60°C)**. Pork is safe at 145°F — pulling at 140°F allows carryover cooking to finish it perfectly.
2.  **Rest:** Let sit for 10 minutes before slicing. Do not skip — cutting too early evacuates all juices.
3.  **Slice:** Cut into 1/2-inch medallions on a slight bias.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   Pull at 140°F. Lean pork dries out instantly past 150°F. A thermometer is not optional — it is mission-critical hardware.
*   Silver skin removal is mandatory. It contracts during cooking, causing the tenderloin to curl and cook unevenly.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Bourbon-Mustard Glaze: during the final 3 minutes of the oven roast, brush with a 50/50 mix of Dijon mustard and maple syrup. After extraction, deglaze the hot skillet with 1oz bourbon and 1 tbsp butter to build a pan sauce from the fond.
