# SOP-030: OPERATION LEAN STRIKE
**Objective:** Precision sear and thermal finish of a Pork Tenderloin.
**Rank:** Sergeant
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike (Lean Protein)


---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 25 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 15–20 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per 6oz):** P: 36g | C: 0g | F: 6g | ~210 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 2/5 (Requires temp precision)
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-043 (Roasted Roots).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x 12" Cast Iron Skillet
- [ ] 1x Digital Meat Thermometer (The Rangefinder)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
- **The Target:** 1 Pork Tenderloin (approx 1 lb).
- **The Rub:** 1 tsp Salt, 1 tsp Black Pepper, 1 tsp Garlic Powder, 1 tsp Dried Thyme.
- **Lubricant:** 2 tbsp Avocado Oil.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Preparation)
1.  **Standardize:** Remove "silver skin" from the pork. Coat in the rub.
2.  **Thermal Prep:** Preheat oven to **400°F (204°C)**.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Sear)
1.  **Heat:** Get skillet screaming hot with oil.
2.  **Sear:** Brown the tenderloin for 2 mins per side (4 sides).

### Phase 3: Extraction
1.  **Dwell:** Move skillet to oven. Bake for 10–12 minutes.
2.  **Pull Point:** Remove when internal temp hits **140°F (60°C)**. 
3.  **Rest:** Let sit for 10 minutes. Temp will rise to 145°F.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** Pull at 140°F. Lean pork dries out instantly past 150°F.
**Secondary Objective:**
*   **The Bourbon-Mustard Glaze:** During the final 3 minutes of the oven "Thermal Dwell," brush the target with a 50/50 mixture of Dijon mustard and Maple syrup (or Honey). Upon extraction, while the meat rests, deglaze the hot skillet with 1oz of Bourbon and 1 tbsp of butter. Stir aggressively to incorporate the "Fond" and pour this high-intensity reduction over the sliced pork.
