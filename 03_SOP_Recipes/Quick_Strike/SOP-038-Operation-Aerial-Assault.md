# SOP-038: OPERATION AERIAL ASSAULT
**Objective:** High-velocity execution of Oven Buffalo Wings with "Tactical Crisp" skin — no fryer required.
**Rank:** Sergeant
**Difficulty:** 2/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 35 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 5 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 30 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per 10 wings):** P: 45g | C: 0g | F: 32g | ~470 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 2/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-034 (Green Perimeter), SOP-015 (Cold Payload).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Baking Sheet with Wire Rack (essential for 360-degree airflow)
- [ ] 1x Large Stainless Steel Mixing Bowl
- [ ] 1x Pair of Tongs

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 2–4 Personnel:*
- **The Target:** 2 lbs Chicken Wings (split into flats and drums)
- **The Armor (Dry Rub):** 1 tsp Kosher Salt, 1 tsp Baking Powder, 1 tsp Garlic Powder
- **The Catalyst (Sauce):** 1/3 cup Frank's RedHot (or equivalent), 4 tbsp Unsalted Butter (melted)

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Surface Prep)
1.  **Moisture Extraction:** Pat wings completely bone-dry with paper towels. Moisture is the enemy of crisp skin.
2.  **Armor Application:** Toss wings in the mixing bowl with the Salt/Baking Powder mixture. **CRITICAL:** Baking Powder (not Baking Soda) alters the skin's pH, enabling a fry-level crunch from an oven without any oil.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Roast)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Preheat oven to **425°F (218°C)**.
2.  **Deployment:** Place wings on the wire rack over the baking sheet. No overlapping — "Airflow Integrity" is mandatory for even crisping.
3.  **Dwell:** Roast for 30 minutes. Flip the wings at the 15-minute mark for uniform browning on both sides.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Sauce Prep:** While wings finish, whisk melted butter and hot sauce together in the large bowl.
2.  **The Toss:** Drop all hot wings into the bowl. Toss aggressively until 100% saturation is achieved.
3.  **Deployment:** Serve immediately with celery sticks and blue cheese or ranch.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   Baking Powder is the approved chemical catalyst — do not substitute Baking Soda. Baking Soda will produce a soapy, metallic off-flavor that ruins the mission.
*   The wire rack is non-negotiable. Wings sitting flat on a pan collect grease underneath and steam instead of roasting.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   For "Honey Garlic" variant, replace hot sauce with 2 tbsp honey, 2 tbsp soy sauce, and 2 cloves minced garlic sautéed in butter.
