# SOP-038: OPERATION AERIAL ASSAULT
**Objective:** High-velocity execution of Buffalo Wings with "Tactical Crisp" skin.
**Rank:** Sergeant (Requires heat management)
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike (Squad/Social Ops)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 35 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 5 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 30 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per 10 wings):** P: 45g | C: 0g | F: 32g | ~470 Cal
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-034 (Green Perimeter) or SOP-015 (Cold Payload).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x Baking Sheet with Wire Rack (Essential for 360-degree airflow)
- [ ] 1x Large Stainless Steel Mixing Bowl
- [ ] 1x Pair of Tongs

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*   **The Target:** 2 lbs Chicken Wings (Split into flats and drums).
*   **The Armor (Dry Rub):** 1 tsp Kosher Salt, 1 tsp Baking Powder (**CRITICAL: Ensures skin crispness**), 1 tsp Garlic Powder.
*   **The Catalyst (Sauce):** 1/3 cup Frank’s RedHot (or similar), 4 tbsp Unsalted Butter (melted).

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Surface Prep)
1.  **Moisture Extraction:** Pat wings bone-dry with paper towels. 
2.  **Armor Application:** Toss wings in the mixing bowl with the Salt/Baking Powder mixture. The baking powder alters the pH of the skin for maximum crunch without frying.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Roast)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Preheat oven to **425°F (218°C)**.
2.  **Deployment:** Place wings on the wire rack. Ensure no overlap (Maintain "Airflow Integrity").
3.  **Dwell:** Roast for 30 minutes. Flip the wings at the 15-minute mark for uniform browning.

### Phase 3: Integration (The Toss)
1.  **Sauce Prep:** Whisk melted butter and hot sauce in the large bowl.
2.  **Merge:** Drop hot wings into the bowl and toss aggressively until 100% saturation is achieved.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** Do not use "Baking Soda" by mistake. It will ruin the taste profile. Baking Powder is the only approved chemical catalyst for this mission.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Serve with celery sticks and blue cheese to neutralize the heat.
