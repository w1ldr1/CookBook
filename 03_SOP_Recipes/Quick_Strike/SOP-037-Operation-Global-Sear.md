# SOP-037: OPERATION GLOBAL SEAR
**Objective:** High-velocity stir-fry engagement for rapid multi-nutrient delivery.
**Rank:** Sergeant
**Difficulty:** 2/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 20 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 12 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 8 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per serving):** P: 45g | C: 15g | F: 18g | ~400 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 2/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-009 (Grain Silo / Rice base).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Wok or 12" Carbon Steel Skillet (must handle very high heat)
- [ ] 1x Spatula or Wok Turner
- [ ] 3x Prep Bowls (for staging all components)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 2 Personnel:*
- **The Protein:** 1 lb Flank Steak or Chicken Breast, sliced thin into bite-size strips
- **The Payload:** 2 cups Broccoli florets, 1 Bell Pepper (sliced), 1 cup Snap Peas
- **The Catalyst (Sauce):** 1/4 cup Soy Sauce, 1 tbsp Fresh Ginger (minced), 2 cloves Garlic (minced), 1 tsp Sesame Oil
- **Lubricant:** 2 tbsp Avocado Oil or Grapeseed Oil (high smoke point)

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Mise en Place)
1.  **Standardization:** Cut all proteins and vegetables to uniform size before heating the pan. In a stir-fry, there is no time for mid-engagement prep — everything must be staged and ready.
2.  **Staging:** Protein in Bowl A. Vegetables in Bowl B. Sauce mixture in Bowl C. This is the entire Infil.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Sear)
1.  **Flash Point:** Heat wok on High until it wisps smoke. Add 1 tbsp oil.
2.  **Protein Deployment:** Add meat in a single layer. Do not move for 60 seconds to set the sear. Toss and cook for 1 more minute. Remove and set aside.
3.  **Vegetable Deployment:** Add remaining oil. Add Bowl B vegetables. Toss constantly for 3–4 minutes until edges are charred but centers are "Crisp-Tender."

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Merge:** Return protein to the wok with the vegetables.
2.  **Catalyst Drop:** Pour Bowl C (sauce) over the mixture. It will bubble and reduce in 30 seconds. Toss to coat all assets completely.
3.  **Deployment:** Serve immediately over SOP-009 rice. Stir-fry degrades fast — deploy within 2 minutes of completion.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   If the pan begins to pool liquid, the heat is too low or the wok is overloaded. Cook protein and vegetables in smaller batches — a crowded wok steams instead of sears.
*   Mise en Place is the mission. A stir-fry cooks in 8 minutes and cannot wait for prep. Everything must be cut, sauced, and staged before the burner is lit.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Garnish with sesame seeds and sliced green onions immediately before deployment for visual contrast and a subtle allium finish.
