# SOP-037: OPERATION GLOBAL SEAR
**Objective:** High-velocity stir-fry engagement for rapid multi-nutrient delivery.
**Rank:** Sergeant (Multi-Tasking Required)
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 20 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 12 Minutes (Crucial Phase)
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 8 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per serving):** P: 45g | C: 15g | F: 18g
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-009 (Grain Silo / Rice base).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x Wok or 12" Carbon Steel Skillet (Must handle high heat)
- [ ] 1x Spatula or Wok Turner
- [ ] 3x Prep Bowls (For staging supplies)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*   **The Protein:** 1 lb Flank Steak or Chicken Breast (sliced thin into "Bite-size Fragments").
*   **The Payload:** 2 cups Broccoli florets, 1 Bell Pepper (sliced), 1 cup Snap Peas.
*   **The Catalyst (Sauce):** 1/4 cup Soy Sauce, 1 tbsp Ginger (minced), 2 cloves Garlic (minced), 1 tsp Sesame Oil.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Prep)
1.  **Standardization:** All proteins and vegetables must be cut to uniform size. In a high-velocity sear, there is no time for mid-cook prep.
2.  **Staging:** Place protein in Bowl A, vegetables in Bowl B, and sauce mixture in Bowl C.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Sear)
1.  **Flash Point:** Heat wok until it wisps smoke. Add 1 tbsp high-smoke-point oil (Avocado/Grapeseed).
2.  **Protein Deployment:** Add meat in a single layer. Do not move for 60 seconds to set the sear. Toss and cook for 1 more minute. Remove and set aside (Exfiltrate).
3.  **Vegetable Deployment:** Add more oil if needed. Add Bowl B. Toss constantly for 3–4 minutes until edges are charred but centers are "Crisp-Tender."

### Phase 3: Integration
1.  **Merge:** Return protein to the wok. 
2.  **Catalyst:** Pour Bowl C over the mixture. The sauce will bubble and thicken instantly. Toss for 30 seconds to coat all assets.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** If the pan begins to "pool" liquid, the heat is too low or you have overloaded the AO. Cook in smaller batches to maintain the sear.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Garnish with sesame seeds and green onions for "Hostage Rescue" level presentation.
