# SOP-022: OPERATION WHITEOUT
**Objective:** Extraction of high-calorie Sausage Gravy for morning sustainment.
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike (Breakfast Ops)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 15 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 10 Minutes
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5 (Basic)

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x 12" Skillet
- [ ] 1x Wooden Spatula

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
- **The Protein:** 1 lb Bulk Breakfast Sausage (Pork).
- **The Thickener:** 1/3 cup All-Purpose Flour.
- **The Liquid:** 3 cups Whole Milk.
- **Ordnance:** 1 tsp Black Pepper (Heavy), 1/2 tsp Salt.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Engagement (The Sear)
1.  **Brown the Meat:** Cook sausage in the skillet over medium-high heat. Break it into small fragments. Do not drain the fat; the fat is the fuel for the roux.

### Phase 2: The Roux Maneuver
1.  **Integration:** Sprinkle flour over the cooked sausage. Stir for 2 minutes to cook out the raw flour taste.

### Phase 3: Hydration
1.  **The Pour:** Gradually pour in the milk while stirring constantly.
2.  **The Simmer:** Reduce heat to medium. Simmer until the mixture thickens into "Whiteout" consistency.
3.  **Seasoning:** Add a massive amount of black pepper.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Note 01:** If the gravy is too thick, add a splash of milk to re-calibrate.
*   **Deployment:** Pour over split biscuits from SOP-021.
