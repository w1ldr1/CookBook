# SOP-022: OPERATION WHITEOUT
**Objective:** Extraction of high-calorie Sausage Gravy for morning sustainment.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Difficulty:** 1/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 15 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 5 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 10 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per serving):** P: 18g | C: 12g | F: 18g | ~280 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-021 (Flaky Biscuit).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 12" Skillet
- [ ] 1x Wooden Spatula

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 4 Personnel:*
- **The Protein:** 1 lb Bulk Breakfast Sausage (Pork)
- **The Thickener:** 1/3 cup All-Purpose Flour
- **The Liquid:** 3 cups Whole Milk
- **Seasoning:** 1 tsp Black Pepper (heavy application), 1/2 tsp Salt

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Sear)
1.  **Brown the Meat:** Cook sausage in the skillet over Medium-High heat. Break it into small fragments with the spatula.
2.  **Critical Constraint:** Do **NOT** drain the fat. The rendered sausage fat is the lubricant for the roux — discarding it forces you to add butter and loses the flavor.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Roux)
1.  **Integration:** Sprinkle flour over the cooked sausage. Stir constantly for 2 minutes to cook out the raw flour taste.
2.  **Hydration:** Gradually pour in the milk while stirring constantly to prevent lumps.
3.  **The Simmer:** Reduce heat to Medium. Simmer, stirring frequently, until the mixture thickens into "Whiteout" consistency (~5 minutes).

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Seasoning:** Apply a heavy dose of black pepper. Taste and adjust salt.
2.  **Deployment:** Ladle over split SOP-021 Biscuits. Serve immediately.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   The rendered sausage fat is the roux lubricant. Do not drain it. The gravy's depth of flavor comes from the fat, not the milk.
*   If the gravy thickens too much, add milk 1/4 cup at a time to thin it back to deployment consistency.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Add 1/2 tsp of crushed red pepper flakes during the roux stage for "Morning Heat" that builds with each bite.
