# SOP-001: OPERATION PRIMAL SEAR
**Objective:** Execution of a perfect medium-rare Ribeye using high-thermal mass hardware.
**Rank:** Corporal
**Difficulty:** 3/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 50 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 40 Minutes (Tempering)
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 8–10 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per 12oz):** P: 62g | C: 0g | F: 54g | ~740 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 3/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-032 (Mashed Potatoes), SOP-033 (Pan Sauce).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 12" Cast Iron Skillet (High thermal mass is non-negotiable)
- [ ] 1x Digital Meat Thermometer (The Rangefinder)
- [ ] 1x Stainless Steel Tongs
- [ ] 1x Tablespoon (For basting maneuvers)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 1 Personnel:*
- **The Target:** 1x Ribeye Steak (1.5" thick, Choice or Prime)
- **Lubricant:** 2 tbsp Avocado Oil (High smoke point)
- **Seasoning:** Coarse Kosher Salt and Black Pepper
- **Aromatics:** 2 tbsp Unsalted Butter, 2 cloves Garlic (crushed), 1 sprig Fresh Rosemary

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Preparation)
1.  **Tempering:** Remove steak from refrigeration 40 minutes prior to engagement. Cold protein on a hot pan causes uneven cooking.
2.  **Surface Prep:** Pat target bone-dry with paper towels. Moisture kills the sear.
3.  **Applying Ordnance:** Liberally coat all surfaces with salt and pepper.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Sear)
1.  **Thermal Saturation:** Heat skillet to High. Add oil. Wait for smoke.
2.  **Initial Sear:** Lay steak away from your body. Sear for 2–3 minutes per side until a mahogany "Armor" (crust) forms.
3.  **Aromatic Infusion:** Reduce heat to medium. Add butter, garlic, and rosemary.
4.  **Basting Maneuver:** Tilt skillet. Spoon hot butter over the steak continuously for the final 2 minutes.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Target Verification:** Pull at **125°F (52°C)**.
2.  **Resting Period:** Rest for 10 minutes. Do not skip — the juices must stabilize.
3.  **Sanitation:** Clean cast iron while warm. Refer to Cast_Iron_Restoration_SOP.md.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   If your smoke alarm doesn't trigger, your thermal engagement was too low.
*   Slice thin against the grain to maximize tenderness.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Place a small pat of compound butter on top of the resting steak to melt into the crust during the rest phase.
