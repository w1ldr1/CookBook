# SOP-025: OPERATION COASTAL EXTRACT
**Objective:** Precision boiling of shrimp for optimal texture.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike (Protein Prep)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 15 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 2–3 Minutes
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5 (Timing critical)
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-015 (Chicken Salad base).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x Large Pot
- [ ] 1x Colander
- [ ] 1x Large Bowl (for Ice Bath)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
- **The Target:** 2 lbs Raw Shrimp (Shell-on for max flavor).
- **The Catalyst:** 1/2 cup Old Bay or Zatarain's Pro-Boil.
- **Acidity:** 1 Lemon (halved).

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Hydration
1.  **Boil:** Fill pot with water, seasoning, and lemon. Bring to a violent boil.

### Phase 2: Engagement
1.  **The Drop:** Add shrimp.
2.  **The Extraction:** Watch closely. As soon as the shrimp turn pink and form a "C" shape, they are done. This usually takes 120–180 seconds.

### Phase 3: Neutralization
1.  **Cold Shock:** Drain and immediately plunge into an ice bath to stop the cooking process. Overcooked shrimp are "Rubber Rations."

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** The "C-Shape" means done; the "O-Shape" means overcooked.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Add halved potatoes and corn to the boil for a "Full Squad" feast.
