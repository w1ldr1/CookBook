# SOP-025: OPERATION COASTAL EXTRACT
**Objective:** Precision boiling of shrimp for optimal pink-firm texture and immediate deployability.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Difficulty:** 1/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 15 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 5 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 2–3 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per 6oz):** P: 28g | C: 2g | F: 2g | ~150 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-024 (Mud-Bug), SOP-015 (Cold Payload base).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Large Pot
- [ ] 1x Colander
- [ ] 1x Large Bowl (for Ice Bath)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 4 Personnel:*
- **The Target:** 2 lbs Raw Shrimp (shell-on for maximum flavor extraction during boil)
- **The Catalyst:** 1/2 cup Old Bay or Zatarain's Pro-Boil
- **Acidity:** 1 Lemon (halved)

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Prep the Bath)
1.  **Boil:** Fill pot with water. Add seasoning and halved lemon. Bring to a violent, rolling boil.
2.  **Ice Bath:** Fill the large bowl with ice and cold water. Stage it next to the stove before dropping the shrimp.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Drop)
1.  **Deployment:** Add shrimp to the boiling water.
2.  **Extraction Window:** Watch closely. The moment the shrimp turn pink and curl into a "C" shape, they are done — typically 120–180 seconds. Do not walk away.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Cold Shock:** Drain immediately through the colander and plunge into the ice bath. Hold for 2 minutes to halt all residual cooking. Overcooked shrimp are "Rubber Rations" — rubbery, tasteless, and irreversible.
2.  **Staging:** Drain from ice bath and serve cold with cocktail sauce, or deploy into any recipe requiring pre-cooked shrimp.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   "C" shape = done. "O" shape = overcooked. Memorize these silhouettes. The difference is 30 seconds.
*   Shell-on shrimp absorb significantly more seasoning from the boil than peeled shrimp. Peel after cooking, not before.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Add halved red potatoes and corn cobs to the boil 15 minutes before the shrimp for a "Full Squad" Low-Country boil feast.
