# SOP-027: OPERATION OVERRIPE RECOVERY
**Objective:** Salvaging expired Banana assets into high-value Banana Bread.
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike (Resource Recovery)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 1 Hour 10 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 10 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 55–60 Minutes
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5 (Simple)

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x 9x5" Loaf Pan
- [ ] 1x Large Fork (The "Masher")
- [ ] 1x Mixing Bowl

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
- **The Asset:** 3 Overripe Bananas (The blacker the skin, the better the intel).
- **The Base:** 1.5 cups All-Purpose Flour, 3/4 cup Sugar.
- **The Catalyst:** 1/3 cup Melted Butter, 1 Egg, 1 tsp Vanilla, 1 tsp Baking Soda, 1 pinch Salt.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Demolition)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Preheat oven to **350°F (177°C)**.
2.  **Neutralize Assets:** Peel bananas and mash into a pulp with the fork until no large chunks remain.

### Phase 2: Integration
1.  **Direct Mix:** Stir melted butter into the mashed bananas. Add baking soda, salt, sugar, egg, and vanilla. 
2.  **Deployment:** Fold in the flour last. Mix until just incorporated.

### Phase 3: Engagement
1.  **Deployment:** Pour into a greased loaf pan.
2.  **Dwell:** Bake for 50–60 minutes. 

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Note 01:** Add 1/2 cup Walnuts or Chocolate Chips during Phase 2 for a "Morale Force Multiplier."
*   **Strategy:** Never throw away black bananas. If you can't bake today, peel them and store in a freezer bag for future ops.
