# SOP-027: OPERATION OVERRIPE RECOVERY
**Objective:** Salvaging expired Banana assets into high-value Banana Bread.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Difficulty:** 1/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 1 Hour 10 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 10 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 55–60 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per slice):** P: 3g | C: 40g | F: 8g | ~250 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-036 (Liquid Fuel).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 9x5" Loaf Pan
- [ ] 1x Large Fork (The "Masher")
- [ ] 1x Mixing Bowl

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 1 Loaf (8 slices):*
- **The Asset:** 3 Overripe Bananas (the blacker the skin, the higher the sugar content)
- **The Base:** 1.5 cups All-Purpose Flour, 3/4 cup Sugar
- **The Catalyst:** 1/3 cup Melted Butter, 1 Egg, 1 tsp Vanilla Extract, 1 tsp Baking Soda, 1 pinch Salt

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Preparation)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Preheat oven to **350°F (177°C)**.
2.  **Neutralize Assets:** Peel bananas and mash into a smooth pulp with the fork. No large chunks — full demolition required.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Mix & Bake)
1.  **Direct Mix:** Stir melted butter into the mashed banana. Add baking soda, salt, sugar, egg, and vanilla. Combine.
2.  **Bulk Load:** Fold in flour last. Stir until just incorporated — do not over-mix.
3.  **Deployment:** Pour into a greased loaf pan.
4.  **Dwell:** Bake for 55–60 minutes until a toothpick exits the center clean.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Extraction:** Cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack.
2.  **The Wait:** Allow to cool fully before slicing — cutting hot banana bread produces a gummy, dense crumb.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   Black bananas are the highest-value asset for this mission. The darker the skin, the more the starch has converted to sugar. Yellow bananas will produce a flat, bland loaf.
*   One-bowl method: no mixer, no creaming, no separation of wet and dry required. The banana provides enough moisture to bind everything.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Fold in 1/2 cup of chopped walnuts before baking for "Structural Integrity" and a complementary bitter-fat contrast to the banana sweetness.
