# SOP-047: OPERATION HIGH-HEAT CURRY
**Objective:** Rapid deployment of a coconut-based Thai Red Curry for international versatility.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Difficulty:** 2/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 20 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 3 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 15 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per serving):** P: 32g | C: 12g | F: 24g | ~390 Cal (excluding rice)
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 2/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-009 (Grain Silo), SOP-019 (Daily Bread for dipping).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 12" Skillet or Wok
- [ ] 1x Wooden Spoon

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 2–3 Personnel:*
- **The Protein:** 1 lb Chicken Breast (sliced thin) or Shrimp (peeled)
- **The Catalyst:** 2 tbsp Red or Green Curry Paste (Thai Kitchen or similar)
- **Hydration:** 1 can (14oz) Full-Fat Coconut Milk
- **The Payload:** 1 cup Frozen Bamboo Shoots or Snap Peas, 1 Red Bell Pepper (sliced)
- **The Polish:** 1 tbsp Fish Sauce, 1 tsp Sugar, Lime wedges, Fresh Basil or Cilantro

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Bloom)
1.  **Thermal Contact:** Heat skillet over Medium. Add 1 tbsp oil.
2.  **Paste Bloom:** Add the curry paste. Fry for 2 minutes, stirring constantly, until intensely fragrant. The paste must toast in the oil — this is the "Bloom" that unlocks the fat-soluble flavor compounds.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Cook)
1.  **Hydration:** Pour in 1/4 of the coconut milk. Whisk aggressively into the paste to form a smooth slurry. Add the remaining coconut milk. Bring to a gentle simmer.
2.  **Protein Deployment:** Add raw protein. **Do not add vegetables yet.** Simmer for 4–5 minutes for chicken (until exterior shows no pink), or 60 seconds for shrimp (until just beginning to curl).
3.  **Vegetable Deployment:** Add all vegetables. Stir to combine.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Final Simmer:** Cook for 6–8 more minutes. Doneness — chicken reads 165°F internally or cuts clean with no pink. Shrimp curl into a "C" (done); "O" shape means overcooked.
2.  **The Polish:** Stir in fish sauce, sugar, and a squeeze of lime. Taste and recalibrate salt and heat.
3.  **Deployment:** Serve over SOP-009 rice. Garnish with fresh basil or cilantro.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   Do not boil the coconut milk aggressively. Hard boiling causes the fat to separate and produce a greasy, broken sauce. Maintain a gentle simmer throughout.
*   The paste bloom in the oil is the most important step. Skipping it (adding paste directly to liquid) produces a flat, one-dimensional flavor.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Add 1 tbsp of peanut butter to the paste bloom for a "Thai Peanut" variant that adds richness and body to the sauce.
