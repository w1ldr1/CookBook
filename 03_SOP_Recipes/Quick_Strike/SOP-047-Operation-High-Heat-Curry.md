# SOP-047: OPERATION HIGH-HEAT CURRY
**Objective:** Rapid deployment of a coconut-based "Red Curry" for international versatility.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 20 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per serving):** P: 32g | C: 12g | F: 24g (excluding rice)
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-009 (Rice) or SOP-019 (Bread for dipping).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x 12" Skillet or Wok
- [ ] 1x Wooden Spoon

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*   **The Protein:** 1 lb Chicken Breast or Shrimp.
*   **The Catalyst:** 2 tbsp Red or Green Curry Paste (Thai Kitchen or similar).
*   **Hydration:** 1 can (14oz) Full-fat Coconut Milk.
*   **The Payload:** 1 cup Frozen Bamboo Shoots or Snap Peas, 1 Red Bell Pepper (sliced).
*   **Secondary Objective:** 1 tbsp Fish Sauce, 1 tsp Sugar, Lime wedges.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Bloom)
1.  **Thermal Engagement:** Heat skillet over medium. Add 1 tbsp oil. 
2.  **Searing the Paste:** Add the curry paste. Fry for 2 minutes until fragrant (This "blooms" the spices).

### Phase 2: Integration
1.  **Hydration:** Pour in 1/4 of the coconut milk. Whisk into the paste to form a smooth "Slurry." Add the rest of the milk.
2.  **Deployment:** Add protein and vegetables. 

### Phase 3: Extraction
1.  **Dwell:** Simmer for 8–10 minutes until protein is cooked through.
2.  **The Polish:** Stir in fish sauce, sugar, and a squeeze of lime.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** Do not boil the coconut milk aggressively; a gentle simmer prevents the fat from separating.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Garnish with fresh Basil or Cilantro.
