# SOP-015: OPERATION COLD PAYLOAD
**Objective:** High-speed conversion of leftover "High-Value Bird" into nutrient-dense chilled rations.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Difficulty:** 1/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 10 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 10 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 0 Minutes (Assembly only)
*   **Target Macros (per 1 cup):** P: 32g | C: 6g | F: 14g | ~280 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-007 (High-Value Bird), SOP-019 (Bread).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Large Mixing Bowl
- [ ] 1x Chef's Knife (The Primary Blade)
- [ ] 1x Silicone Spatula

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 2 Personnel:*
- **The Target:** 2 cups cooked Chicken (from SOP-007), cubed or shredded
- **Binding Catalyst:** 1/4 cup Greek Yogurt (high protein) or Avocado Mayo
- **Crunch Components:** 1/2 cup Celery (diced), 1/4 cup Red Onion (minced)
- **Acid/Seasoning:** 1 tbsp Dijon Mustard, 1 tsp Kosher Salt, 1/2 tsp Black Pepper, 1/2 tsp Dried Dill

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Preparation)
1.  **Standardize the Protein:** Ensure chicken is chilled and cut into uniform 1/2-inch cubes.
2.  **Vegetable Processing:** Fine-dice celery and onion to ensure even distribution in every bite.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Mix)
1.  **The Sauce:** Combine binding catalyst, mustard, and seasonings in the mixing bowl. Stir until homogenous.
2.  **Deployment:** Add chicken and vegetables. Fold until all protein is fully coated.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Deployment Options:** Serve over a bed of greens, inside a whole-wheat wrap, or directly from the bowl.
2.  **Storage:** Store in an airtight container for up to 3 days. Flavors improve after a 4-hour cold soak.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   Greek Yogurt delivers a higher protein-to-fat ratio than mayo. It also adds a subtle tang that improves the flavor profile.
*   Ensure chicken is fully chilled before mixing — warm chicken will break the sauce.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Add 1/2 cup diced Granny Smith apple for textural contrast and natural sweetness that cuts the richness of the binder.
