# SOP-015: OPERATION COLD PAYLOAD
**Objective:** High-speed conversion of leftover "High-Value Bird" into nutrient-dense chilled rations.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike (Zero-Heat Mission)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 10 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 10 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 0 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per 1 cup):** P: 32g | C: 6g | F: 14g | ~280 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5 (Assembly only)
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-019 (Bread).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Large Mixing Bowl
- [ ] 1x Chef's Knife (The Primary Blade)
- [ ] 1x Silicone Spatula

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 2 Personnel:*
- **The Target:** 2 cups cooked Chicken (from SOP-007), cubed or shredded.
- **Binding Catalyst:** 1/4 cup Greek Yogurt (High protein) or Avocado Mayo.
- **Crunch Components:** 1/2 cup Celery (diced), 1/4 cup Red Onion (minced).
- **Acid/Seasoning:** 1 tbsp Dijon Mustard, 1 tsp Kosher Salt, 1/2 tsp Black Pepper, 1/2 tsp Dried Dill.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Preparation)
1.  **Standardize the Protein:** Ensure the chicken is chilled and cut into uniform 1/2-inch cubes.
2.  **Vegetable Processing:** Fine-dice the celery and onion to ensure "Even Distribution" in every bite.

### Phase 2: Integration
1.  **The Mix:** Combine binding catalyst, mustard, and seasonings in the mixing bowl. Stir until a homogenous "sauce" is formed.
2.  **Deployment:** Add the chicken and vegetables. Fold the mixture until all protein is fully coated.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Deployment Options:** Serve over a bed of greens, inside a whole-wheat wrap, or directly from the bowl.
2.  **Storage:** Store in an airtight container for up to 3 days.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** Use Greek Yogurt for a higher protein-to-fat ratio.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Add diced Granny Smith apples for "Textural Intel."
