# SOP-024: OPERATION MUD-BUG
**Objective:** Rapid engagement of Shrimp Étouffée.
**Rank:** Commander
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike (High-Flavor Seizure)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 40 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 25 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per serving):** P: 35g | C: 15g | F: 18g
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 3/5 (Roux management)
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-009 (Rice).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x Large Heavy Skillet
- [ ] 1x Whisk

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
- **The Protein:** 1 lb Large Shrimp (peeled/deveined).
- **The Roux:** 1/2 cup Butter, 1/2 cup Flour.
- **The Trinity:** 1 Onion, 1 Bell Pepper, 2 stalks Celery (diced).
- **Liquid:** 2 cups Seafood or Chicken Stock.
- **Ordnance:** 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning, 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: The "Blonde to Peanut" Roux
1.  **Melt:** Butter in skillet over medium heat.
2.  **Whisk:** Add flour. Stir constantly for 10–15 minutes until it reaches the color of "Peanut Butter." Do not burn.

### Phase 2: Engagement
1.  **Trinity:** Add veggies to the roux. They will cook fast.
2.  **Hydration:** Slowly whisk in stock and seasonings. Simmer for 10 minutes until thick.

### Phase 3: Extraction
1.  **The Shrimp:** Add shrimp. Cook for 3–5 minutes until pink. **DO NOT OVERCOOK.**

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** A "Peanut Butter" color roux is the target; any darker is "Killed in Action."
*   **Secondary Objective:** Use crawfish tail meat for "Bayou Authentic" ops.
