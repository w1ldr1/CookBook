# SOP-024: OPERATION MUD-BUG
**Objective:** Rapid engagement of Shrimp Étouffée over white rice.
**Rank:** Commander
**Difficulty:** 3/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 40 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 15 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 25 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per serving):** P: 35g | C: 15g | F: 18g | ~360 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 3/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-009 (Grain Silo).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Large Heavy-bottomed Skillet
- [ ] 1x Whisk
- [ ] 1x Wooden Spatula

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 4 Personnel:*
- **The Protein:** 1 lb Large Shrimp (peeled and deveined)
- **The Roux:** 1/2 cup Unsalted Butter, 1/2 cup All-Purpose Flour
- **The Holy Trinity:** 1 Onion, 1 Bell Pepper, 2 stalks Celery (all diced)
- **Liquid:** 2 cups Seafood Stock or Chicken Stock
- **Seasoning:** 1 tbsp Cajun Seasoning, 1 tsp Worcestershire Sauce, Salt and Pepper

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Roux)
1.  **Melt:** Add butter to the skillet over Medium heat until foaming subsides.
2.  **The Build:** Whisk in flour. Stir constantly for 10–15 minutes until the roux reaches the color of "Peanut Butter." This is the Blonde-to-Peanut transition — do not rush it, do not burn it.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Build)
1.  **Trinity Deployment:** Add diced onion, bell pepper, and celery directly to the hot roux. Stir to combine — the vegetables will sizzle and release moisture.
2.  **Hydration:** Slowly whisk in the stock. Add Cajun seasoning and Worcestershire. Simmer for 10 minutes until the sauce thickens and the raw flour taste is gone.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **The Shrimp:** Add shrimp in a single layer. Cook for 3–5 minutes until they turn pink and curl into a "C" shape. **DO NOT OVERCOOK** — shrimp continue cooking from residual heat after the pan is off.
2.  **Deployment:** Serve immediately over white rice (SOP-009).

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   A "Peanut Butter" color roux is the mission objective. Lighter = raw flour taste. Darker = bitter. Once it burns, the mission is over — start the roux again.
*   Stir the roux constantly. It can go from perfectly golden to scorched in under 30 seconds over uneven heat.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Substitute crawfish tail meat (frozen, thawed) for shrimp for "Bayou Authentic" ops. The flavor is sweeter and more complex.
