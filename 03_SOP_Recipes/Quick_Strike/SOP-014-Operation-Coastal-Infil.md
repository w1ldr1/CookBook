# SOP-014: OPERATION COASTAL INFIL
**Objective:** Rapid deployment of Street Tacos using high-heat protein sear.
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike (High-Speed/Social Mission)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 20 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 10 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 10 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per 3 tacos):** P: 38g | C: 24g | F: 18g | ~410 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 2/5 (Requires multi-tasking)

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 12" Cast Iron Skillet or Carbon Steel Comal
- [ ] 1x Tongs
- [ ] 1x Small Blender or Mortar/Pestle (for salsa)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 2 Personnel:*
- **The Protein:** 1 lb Flank Steak or Skirt Steak (Thin-cut).
- **The Vehicle:** 6-8 Street-size Corn Tortillas.
- **The Toppings:** 1/2 White Onion (diced), 1 bunch Cilantro, 2 Limes (quartered).
- **The Catalyst:** 1 tsp Cumin, 1 tsp Chili Powder, 1 tsp Salt.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Preparation)
1.  **Protein Prep:** Slice steak into 1/4" thin strips *against the grain* before cooking. 
2.  **Standardization:** Dice onion and chop cilantro. Combine them into a "Garnish Unit."

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Cook)
1.  **High-Thermal Sear:** Heat skillet to High. Add oil. 
2.  **Flash Engagement:** Add steak strips. Season in the pan. Cook for 2-3 mins per side until charred edges appear. Remove and set aside.
3.  **Tortilla Toast:** Wipe pan. Place tortillas in the pan for 30 seconds per side until they bubble and soften. Store in a clean towel (The "Warmer").

### Phase 3: Extraction & Plating
1.  **Assembly:** Double-stack tortillas (for structural integrity). Add meat, Garnish Unit, and a heavy squeeze of lime.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Note 01:** Corn tortillas must be toasted; raw corn tortillas are "unstable" and will crumble.
*   **Field Note 02:** Use the leftover brisket from SOP-013 for a "Special Ops" version of this mission.
