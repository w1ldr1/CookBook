# SOP-014: OPERATION COASTAL INFIL
**Objective:** Rapid deployment of Street Tacos using high-heat protein sear.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Difficulty:** 2/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 20 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 10 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 10 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per 3 tacos):** P: 38g | C: 24g | F: 18g | ~410 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 2/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-013 (Texas Black-Gold leftovers).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 12" Cast Iron Skillet or Carbon Steel Comal
- [ ] 1x Tongs
- [ ] 1x Chef's Knife

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 2 Personnel:*
- **The Protein:** 1 lb Flank Steak or Skirt Steak (thin-cut)
- **The Vehicle:** 6–8 Street-size Corn Tortillas
- **The Toppings:** 1/2 White Onion (diced), 1 bunch Cilantro, 2 Limes (quartered)
- **Seasoning:** 1 tsp Cumin, 1 tsp Chili Powder, 1 tsp Kosher Salt
- **Lubricant:** 1 tbsp Avocado Oil or neutral high-smoke-point oil

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Preparation)
1.  **Protein Prep:** Slice steak into 1/4" thin strips *against the grain* before cooking.
2.  **Standardization:** Dice onion and chop cilantro. Combine into a single "Garnish Unit."

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Cook)
1.  **High-Thermal Contact:** Heat skillet to High. Add oil.
2.  **Flash Sear:** Add steak strips. Season in the pan. Cook for 2–3 minutes per side until charred edges appear. Remove and rest.
3.  **Tortilla Toast:** Wipe pan. Place tortillas in the dry pan for 30 seconds per side until they bubble and char. Store in a clean towel (The "Warmer") to keep pliable.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Assembly:** Double-stack tortillas for structural integrity. Add meat, Garnish Unit, and a heavy squeeze of lime.
2.  **Serve Immediately:** Tacos degrade within 3 minutes. Deploy straight to the target.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   Tortillas must be charred — raw corn tortillas are structurally unstable and will split under load.
*   Skirt steak is the preferred protein due to its fat content and char potential. Do not overcook past medium.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Garnish with pickled red onions for acid balance and visual contrast.
