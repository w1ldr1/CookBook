# SOP-006: OPERATION GREEN COVER
**Objective:** Rapid deployment of fiber and micronutrients to support primary protein targets.
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike (Vegetable Side-Op)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 20 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 5 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 15 Minutes
*   **Target Macros:** P: 4g | C: 10g | F: 7g | ~120 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5 (Basic Maneuvers)

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Half-Sheet Pan (Industrial Grade Aluminum)
- [ ] 1x Large Mixing Bowl
- [ ] Parchment Paper (Secondary Objective: For zero-friction cleanup)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 2-4 Personnel:*
- **The Target:** 1.5 lbs Fresh Broccoli florets or Brussels Sprouts (halved).
- **Lubricant:** 2 tbsp Extra Virgin Olive Oil.
- **Seasoning:** 1 tsp Kosher Salt, 1/2 tsp Black Pepper, 1 tsp Garlic Powder.
- **Acidity/Finish:** 1/2 Fresh Lemon (for zest and juice), 2 tbsp Shredded Parmesan (Secondary Objective).

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Preparation)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Preheat oven to **425°F (218°C)**. High heat is required to achieve "Charred Ordnance" texture.
2.  **Standardizing the Target:** Ensure all florets/sprouts are roughly the same size to ensure uniform thermal distribution.
3.  **Coating:** Toss the vegetables in the mixing bowl with oil and dry seasonings until every surface is "wet."

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Cook)
1.  **Deployment:** Spread the target across the sheet pan in a single layer. **CRITICAL:** Do not crowd the pan. If the veggies touch too much, they will steam instead of roast.
2.  **Thermal Exposure:** Roast for 12–15 minutes. 
3.  **The Flip:** At the 10-minute mark, use a spatula to agitate the pan for even browning.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Final Polish
1.  **Extraction:** Remove when edges are dark brown/charred and stalks are fork-tender.
2.  **Final Finish:** While hot, squeeze the fresh lemon juice over the pan and sprinkle with Parmesan. The residual heat will bond the cheese to the target.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)

*   **Field Note 01:** If using frozen vegetables, do not thaw. Increase cook time by 5 minutes and ensure the oven is at full temp before deployment.
*   **Field Note 02:** "Char equals Flavor." Do not be afraid of the black edges; that is where the Maillard reaction creates the best results for vegetables.
*   **Logistics:** These store well and can be integrated into "Field Rations" for mid-week lunches.
