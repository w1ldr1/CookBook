# SOP-026: OPERATION GREEN HARVEST
**Objective:** Tactical extraction of nutrients from Zucchini into a high-density quick bread.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Difficulty:** 1/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 1 Hour 15 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 15 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 55–60 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per slice):** P: 3g | C: 30g | F: 10g | ~220 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-036 (Liquid Fuel).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 9x5" Loaf Pan
- [ ] 1x Box Grater (The "Shredder")
- [ ] 2x Mixing Bowls (Dry/Wet separation)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 1 Loaf (8 slices):*
- **The Payload:** 1.5 cups Zucchini (grated, approx 1 large — do not peel)
- **The Bulk:** 1.5 cups All-Purpose Flour, 1/2 cup Granulated Sugar, 1/2 cup Brown Sugar
- **The Binding:** 1/2 cup Vegetable Oil, 1 Large Egg, 1 tsp Vanilla Extract
- **Ordnance:** 1/2 tsp Baking Soda, 1/2 tsp Baking Powder, 1.5 tsp Cinnamon, 1/2 tsp Salt

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Prep)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Preheat oven to **350°F (177°C)**.
2.  **Shredding:** Grate zucchini directly into a paper towel. Squeeze firmly to extract excess moisture — a wet zucchini will sink the loaf.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Mix & Bake)
1.  **Wet Mix:** Whisk oil, both sugars, egg, and vanilla in Bowl A. Stir in the squeezed zucchini.
2.  **Dry Mix:** Combine flour, baking soda, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt in Bowl B.
3.  **Merge:** Fold Dry into Wet until just combined. Over-mixing develops gluten and produces "Tough Rations."
4.  **Deployment:** Grease the loaf pan. Pour in batter.
5.  **Dwell:** Bake for 55–60 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the center exits clean.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Extraction:** Cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack.
2.  **The Wait:** Allow to cool completely before slicing — cutting hot quick bread produces crumbling and a wet crumb.
3.  **Storage:** Wraps well at room temperature for 3 days or refrigerate for 1 week.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   Squeezing the zucchini is mandatory. Skipping this step introduces excess moisture that prevents the loaf from setting properly.
*   Do not peel the zucchini — the skin adds color, nutrients, and virtually disappears into the finished bread.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Fold in 1/2 cup dark chocolate chips before baking for "Morale Support" that converts even vegetable-skeptical personnel.
