# SOP-026: OPERATION GREEN HARVEST
**Objective:** Tactical extraction of nutrients from Zucchini into a high-density quick bread.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike (Baking Ops)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 1 Hour 15 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 15 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 55–60 Minutes
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5 (Basic)
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-036 (Coffee).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x 9x5" Loaf Pan
- [ ] 1x Box Grater (The "Shredder")
- [ ] 2x Mixing Bowls (Dry/Wet separation)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
- **The Payload:** 1.5 cups Zucchini (grated, approx 1 large).
- **The Bulk:** 1.5 cups All-Purpose Flour, 1/2 cup Sugar, 1/2 cup Brown Sugar.
- **The Binding:** 1/2 cup Vegetable Oil, 1 Large Egg, 1 tsp Vanilla.
- **Ordnance:** 1/2 tsp Baking Soda, 1/2 tsp Baking Powder, 1.5 tsp Cinnamon, 1/2 tsp Salt.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Shred)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Preheat oven to **350°F (177°C)**.
2.  **Shredding:** Grate zucchini. **CRITICAL:** Do not peel. Use a paper towel to squeeze out excess moisture if the zucchini is exceptionally watery.

### Phase 2: Integration
1.  **Wet Mix:** Whisk oil, sugars, egg, and vanilla in Bowl A. Stir in shredded zucchini.
2.  **Dry Mix:** Combine flour, baking soda, baking powder, and spices in Bowl B.
3.  **Merge:** Fold Dry into Wet until just combined. Over-mixing results in "Tough Rations."

### Phase 3: Engagement
1.  **Deployment:** Grease loaf pan. Pour in batter.
2.  **Dwell:** Bake for 55–60 minutes. Target success: Toothpick inserted in center comes out clean.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** This is the ultimate "Stealth Health" mission. The Unit won't taste the vegetables.
*   **Logistics:** Slices can be frozen and toasted for "Emergency Morning Rations."
