# SOP-021: OPERATION FLAKY BISCUIT
**Objective:** Rapid deployment of "Cathead" style Buttermilk Biscuits — tall, rugged, and butter-laced.
**Rank:** Sergeant
**Difficulty:** 2/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 25 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 10 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 12–15 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per biscuit):** P: 4g | C: 25g | F: 11g | ~210 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 2/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-022 (Whiteout Gravy).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Baking Sheet or Cast Iron Skillet
- [ ] 1x Large Mixing Bowl
- [ ] 1x Box Grater (The "Shredder")

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 8 Biscuits:*
- **Bulk:** 2 cups Self-Rising Flour
- **Fat:** 1/2 cup (1 stick) Unsalted Butter, **FROZEN**
- **Hydration:** 3/4 cup Cold Buttermilk

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Grate)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Preheat oven to **450°F (232°C)**.
2.  **The Grate:** Use a box grater to grate the frozen butter directly into the flour. Toss to coat every piece. The frozen butter creates "micro-pockets" of steam that generate the flaky layers during baking.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Mix & Bake)
1.  **Hydration:** Pour in cold buttermilk. Stir gently with a fork until just moistened. **DO NOT OVERWORK** — overworking develops gluten and produces dense biscuits, not flaky ones.
2.  **Drop Maneuver:** Scoop 1/4-cup mounds of dough onto the baking sheet. They should look rugged and irregular, like "Catheads."
3.  **Dwell:** Bake for 12–15 minutes until tops are deep golden brown.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Extraction:** Remove from oven and transfer to a wire rack.
2.  **Immediate Finish:** Brush tops with melted honey-butter within 60 seconds of extraction for maximum absorption.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   Keeping the butter frozen until the moment it hits the grater is the single most critical success factor. Warm butter melts into the flour instead of staying in distinct pockets, and the layers collapse.
*   Self-Rising Flour already contains baking powder and salt — do not add more or the biscuits will be salty and over-leavened.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Add 1/2 cup of shredded sharp cheddar and 1 tsp of garlic powder to the flour for "Tactical Cheddar-Garlic" biscuits.
