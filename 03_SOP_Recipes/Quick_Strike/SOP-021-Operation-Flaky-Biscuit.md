# SOP-021: OPERATION FLAKY BISCUIT
**Objective:** Rapid deployment of "Cathead" style Buttermilk Biscuits.
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike (Side-Op)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 25 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 10 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 12–15 Minutes
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 2/5 (Requires cold-fat management)

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x Baking Sheet or Cast Iron Skillet
- [ ] 1x Large Mixing Bowl
- [ ] 1x Pastry Cutter or two forks

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
- **Bulk:** 2 cups Self-Rising Flour (standardized for speed).
- **Fat:** 1/2 cup (1 stick) Unsalted Butter, **FROZEN**.
- **Hydration:** 3/4 cup Cold Buttermilk.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Grate)
1.  **Thermal Prep:** Preheat oven to **450°F (232°C)**.
2.  **The Grate:** Use a cheese grater to grate the frozen butter directly into the flour. Toss to coat. This ensures "micro-pockets" of steam.

### Phase 2: Integration
1.  **Hydration:** Add buttermilk. Stir gently with a fork until just moistened. **DO NOT OVERWORK.** Overworking neutralizes the fluffiness.

### Phase 3: Deployment
1.  **Drop Maneuver:** Scoop 1/4 cup mounds of dough onto the pan. They should look rugged, like "Catheads."
2.  **Dwell:** Bake for 12–15 minutes until tops are golden brown.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Note 01:** Keeping the butter frozen is the critical success factor. 
*   **Usage:** Primary vehicle for SOP-022 (Sausage & Gravy).
