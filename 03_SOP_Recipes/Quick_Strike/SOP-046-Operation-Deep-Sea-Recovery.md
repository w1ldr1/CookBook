# SOP-046: OPERATION DEEP-SEA RECOVERY
**Objective:** Rapid deployment of Linguine with Clams (Linguine alle Vongole).
**Rank:** Sergeant
**Difficulty:** 2/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 20 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 10 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 10 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per serving):** P: 28g | C: 52g | F: 12g | ~420 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 2/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-019 (Daily Bread — for sauce recovery), SOP-034 (Green Perimeter), SOP-048 (Hostage Rescue Beverages).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Large Pasta Pot
- [ ] 1x 12" Skillet with Tight-Fitting Lid
- [ ] 1x Tongs

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 2 Personnel:*
- **The Target:** 2 lbs Fresh Manila Clams or Littleneck Clams (scrubbed under cold water)
- **The Vehicle:** 1/2 lb Linguine
- **The Catalyst:** 1/2 cup Dry White Wine (Sauvignon Blanc), 3 tbsp Olive Oil, 4 cloves Garlic (sliced thin), 1/2 tsp Red Pepper Flakes
- **The Finish:** 1/4 cup Fresh Flat-Leaf Parsley (chopped), 1 tbsp Cold Unsalted Butter

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Pasta Prep)
1.  **Boil:** Heat heavily salted water in the pasta pot. Add linguine when boiling.
2.  **Al Dente Target:** Cook for 2 minutes *less* than the box instructions — the pasta will finish in the clam sauce.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Clam Steam)
1.  **Aromatic Build:** Heat olive oil in the skillet over Medium. Add garlic and red pepper flakes. Cook for 60 seconds until fragrant — do not brown.
2.  **Deployment:** Increase heat to High. Add clams and wine. Close the lid immediately.
3.  **Steam Dwell:** Cook for 5–7 minutes, shaking the pan occasionally.
4.  **CRITICAL CHECK:** Open the lid. Discard any clams that did not open — they are compromised assets and a biological hazard.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Merge:** Add the undercooked pasta directly to the skillet with the clams and liquid.
2.  **Emulsify:** Add cold butter and 1/4 cup of reserved pasta water. Toss vigorously for 2 minutes over Medium heat until a silky, cohesive sauce forms. Stir in parsley.
3.  **Deployment:** Serve immediately in warmed bowls. Place a piece of SOP-019 bread alongside for sauce recovery operations.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   The pasta water starch is the binding agent that turns the wine, oil, and butter into a unified sauce. Without it, the sauce separates into an oily puddle.
*   Discard unopened clams without exception. An unopened clam did not cook properly and cannot be consumed safely.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Use a piece of SOP-019 No-Knead Bread to mop up the remaining sauce in the bowl — this is the highest-value use of the remaining liquid.
