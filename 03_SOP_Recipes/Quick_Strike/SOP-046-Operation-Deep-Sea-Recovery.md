# SOP-046: OPERATION DEEP-SEA RECOVERY
**Objective:** Rapid deployment of Linguine with Clams (Linguine alle Vongole).
**Rank:** Sergeant
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike (High-Performance Seafood)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 20 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 10 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per serving):** P: 28g | C: 52g | F: 12g
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 2/5 (Timing is critical)
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-019 (Daily Bread - for sauce extraction), SOP-034 (Green Perimeter), SOP-048 (Hostage Rescue Beverages).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x Large Pasta Pot
- [ ] 1x 12" Skillet with a TIGHT FITTING lid
- [ ] 1x Tongs

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*   **The Target:** 2 lbs Fresh Manila Clams or Littleneck Clams (scrubbed).
*   **The Vehicle:** 1/2 lb Linguine.
*   **The Catalyst:** 1/2 cup Dry White Wine (Sauvignon Blanc), 3 tbsp Olive Oil, 4 cloves Garlic (sliced thin), 1/2 tsp Red Pepper Flakes.
*   **The Finish:** 1/4 cup Fresh Parsley (chopped), 1 tbsp Cold Butter.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Pasta Prep)
1.  **Boil:** Heat salted water. Add linguine. Cook for 2 minutes *less* than the box instructions.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Clam Steam)
1.  **Sauté:** Heat oil in the skillet over medium heat. Add garlic and red pepper flakes. Cook for 60 seconds (do not brown).
2.  **Deployment:** Increase heat to High. Add clams and wine. **CLOSE THE LID.**
3.  **Steam Dwell:** Cook for 5–7 minutes. Shake the pan occasionally. 
4.  **Extraction:** Open the lid. **CRITICAL:** Discard any clams that did not open; they are "Hostile Assets" (Biological hazard).

### Phase 3: Integration
1.  **Merge:** Add the undercooked pasta directly to the skillet.
2.  **Emulsify:** Add cold butter and 1/4 cup of the pasta water. Toss vigorously for 2 minutes until a silky sauce forms. Stir in parsley.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** The starch in the pasta water is the "Glue" that binds the wine/oil/butter into a sauce.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Serve with a piece of SOP-019 (No-Knead Bread) to "Mop up" the remaining sauce.
