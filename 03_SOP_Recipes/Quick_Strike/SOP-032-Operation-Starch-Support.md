# SOP-032: OPERATION STARCH SUPPORT
**Objective:** Deployment of smooth, high-calorie Mashed Potatoes — the primary Side-Op.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Difficulty:** 1/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 30 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 10 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 15–20 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per 1 cup):** P: 4g | C: 35g | F: 14g | ~280 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-041 (Command Loaf), SOP-001 (Primal Sear).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Large Pot
- [ ] 1x Potato Masher or Ricer
- [ ] 1x Colander

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 4 Personnel:*
- **The Target:** 3 lbs Yukon Gold Potatoes (peeled and cubed — Yukon Gold preferred for buttery flavor)
- **Binding Catalyst:** 1/2 cup Whole Milk or Heavy Cream, 1/2 stick (4 tbsp) Unsalted Butter
- **Seasoning:** 2 tsp Kosher Salt, 1 tsp Black Pepper, 1/2 tsp Garlic Powder

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Cold Start)
1.  **Submerge:** Place cubed potatoes in the pot and cover with **cold** water — not boiling. Starting in cold water ensures uniform cooking from outside to center.
2.  **Boil:** Add 1 tsp salt. Bring to a boil over High heat, then reduce to a steady simmer.
3.  **Check:** Cook for 15–20 minutes until "Fork Tender" — a fork slides in with zero resistance.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Mash)
1.  **Drain:** Drain through the colander. Return potatoes to the hot pot and let them sit for 2 minutes to "Steam Dry" — residual moisture is the enemy of smooth mash.
2.  **The Mash:** Use the masher to break down the potatoes while they are hot. Hot potatoes mash smoothly; cool potatoes turn gluey.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **The Finish:** Add butter first and let it melt in. Then add warm milk gradually, stirring until smooth and the desired consistency is reached.
2.  **Season:** Add salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Taste and recalibrate.
3.  **Deployment:** Serve immediately — mashed potatoes lose texture as they cool.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   Always start in cold water. Dropping potatoes into boiling water cooks the outside before the center is done, producing an uneven texture.
*   Warm your milk and butter before adding. Cold dairy dropped into hot potatoes lowers the temperature and causes the mash to seize.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   "Guerilla Style" — leave the skins on for a rustic, nutrient-dense version. Increase salt slightly to compensate for the bitterness of the skins.
