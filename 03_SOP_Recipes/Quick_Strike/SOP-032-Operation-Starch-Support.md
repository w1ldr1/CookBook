# SOP-032: OPERATION STARCH SUPPORT
**Objective:** Deployment of smooth, high-calorie Mashed Potatoes (The Primary Side-Op).
**Rank:** Recruit
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike (Side-Op)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 30 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 10 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 15–20 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per 1 cup):** P: 4g | C: 35g | F: 14g | ~280 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5 (Basic)
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-041 (Meatloaf), SOP-001 (Steak).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x Large Pot
- [ ] 1x Potato Masher or Ricer
- [ ] 1x Colander

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
- **The Target:** 3 lbs Yukon Gold or Russet Potatoes (peeled and cubed).
- **Binding Catalyst:** 1/2 cup Whole Milk or Heavy Cream, 1/2 stick (4 tbsp) Unsalted Butter.
- **Seasoning:** 2 tsp Kosher Salt, 1 tsp Black Pepper, 1/2 tsp Garlic Powder.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Hydration)
1.  **Submerge:** Place cubed potatoes in the pot and cover with **cold** water.
2.  **Boil:** Add a pinch of salt. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer.
3.  **Check:** Cook for 15–20 mins until "Fork Tender" (zero resistance).

### Phase 2: Engagement (Extraction)
1.  **Drain:** Use the colander to remove all water. Let the potatoes sit in the hot pot for 2 minutes to "Steam Dry."
2.  **The Mash:** Use the masher to break down the potatoes while they are hot.

### Phase 3: Integration
1.  **Deployment:** Add butter and milk gradually. Whisk until smooth.
2.  **Season:** Add salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Taste and re-calibrate if necessary.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** Start in cold water for uniform thermal penetration.
*   **Secondary Objective:** For "Guerilla Style" (Rustic), leave the skins on.
