# SOP-002: OPERATION GROUND COMBAT
**Objective:** Rapid deployment of a high-protein, nutrient-dense fuel bowl.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Difficulty:** 2/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 15 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 2 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 10 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per serving):** P: 55g | C: 45g | F: 22g | ~600 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 2/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-009 (Grain Silo / Rice), SOP-018 (Yogurt Armor Cake).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 12" Skillet or Wok
- [ ] 1x Meat Masher or Wooden Spatula
- [ ] 1x Measuring Cup

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 1 Personnel:*
- **The Protein:** 0.5 lbs Lean Ground Beef (90/10) or Ground Turkey
- **The Base:** 1 cup Pre-cooked Jasmine Rice (Microwavable pouch)
- **The Payload:** 1 cup Frozen Stir-fry vegetable mix
- **The Catalyst:** 2 tbsp Soy Sauce, 1 tsp Sriracha, 1 tsp Garlic Powder
- **Top Cover (Secondary Objective):** 1 Egg

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Preparation)
1.  **Clear the Deck:** Ensure the skillet is clean and all supplies are staged within reach of the heat source.
2.  **Rice Prep:** Massage the microwave rice pouch to break up grains while still sealed. Set aside.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Cook)
1.  **High Heat Contact:** Set skillet to medium-high heat. Add the ground protein immediately.
2.  **Neutralize the Protein:** Use the masher to break the meat into small, uniform crumbles. Cook until no pink remains (approx. 5–6 minutes).
3.  **Deploy Payload:** Add the frozen vegetables. The ice crystals will steam-cook the veggies rapidly in the hot pan.
4.  **Integration:** Add the pre-cooked rice and the Catalyst (Soy sauce, Sriracha, Garlic powder). Stir for 2 minutes until heated through.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Secondary Objective:** Push the mixture to one side of the pan. Crack the egg into the empty space and fry for 2 minutes until the white is set.
2.  **Plating:** Transfer the mixture directly into a heavy-duty bowl. Top with the egg.
3.  **Immediate Sanitation:** Run the pan under hot water while still warm to prevent the sauce from baking onto the hardware.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   Keep microwave rice pouches in the pantry at all times for maximum velocity.
*   This mission scales directly — double all supplies for 2 personnel without changing cook time.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Add 1 tsp of Red Pepper Flakes during the sear for a "High-Heat" engagement profile.
