# SOP-035: OPERATION RED SAUCE
**Objective:** Rapid deployment of a high-calorie Meat Sauce Pasta.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Difficulty:** 1/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 20 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 5 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 15 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per 1.5 cups):** P: 28g | C: 55g | F: 12g | ~450 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-021 (Flaky Biscuit / Garlic Bread variation), SOP-016 (Iron-Clad Chili leftovers).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Large Pasta Pot
- [ ] 1x 12" Skillet (The Sauce Bay)
- [ ] 1x Colander

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 4 Personnel:*
- **The Vehicle:** 1 lb Spaghetti or Penne
- **The Catalyst:** 1 jar (24oz) High-Quality Marinara (or 1 can 28oz Crushed Tomatoes)
- **The Protein:** 1 lb Ground Beef (80/20) or Italian Sausage
- **Aromatics:** 1 Onion (diced), 3 cloves Garlic (minced), 1 tsp Dried Oregano

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Preparation)
1.  **Boil:** Fill the pasta pot with water. Add a fistful of salt — the water should taste like the sea.
2.  **Pasta Deployment:** Once boiling, add pasta. Cook for 1 minute *less* than the package instructions. It will finish in the sauce (Al Dente target).

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Meat Sauce)
1.  **Sear:** Brown the protein in the skillet over High heat. Drain excess fat.
2.  **Aromatic Deployment:** Add onion and garlic. Sauté for 3 minutes until softened.
3.  **The Simmer:** Pour in the marinara and oregano. Reduce heat to LOW. Simmer while the pasta finishes.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Reserve Water:** Before draining the pasta, capture 1/2 cup of pasta water (Liquid Starch). Do not skip this — it is the binding agent.
2.  **The Merge:** Add drained pasta and the pasta water to the skillet with the sauce. Toss over medium heat for 60 seconds until the sauce coats every strand.
3.  **Deployment:** Portion immediately and top with shredded Parmesan.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   Pasta water is the "Starch Glue" — the dissolved starch in the water emulsifies the sauce and locks it to the noodle. This is the single technique that separates restaurant pasta from home pasta.
*   Never rinse cooked pasta. Rinsing strips the surface starch and prevents the sauce from bonding.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Add 1 tsp of sugar to the sauce to neutralize high-acid canned tomatoes. Taste first — quality marinara may not need it.
