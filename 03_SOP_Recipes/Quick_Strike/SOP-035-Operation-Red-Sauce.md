# SOP-035: OPERATION RED SAUCE
**Objective:** Rapid deployment of a high-calorie Marinara/Pasta mission.
**Rank:** Recruit (Basic Maneuver)
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 20 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 15 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per 1.5 cups):** P: 28g | C: 55g | F: 12g
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-021 (Garlic Bread Variation), SOP-016 (Chili leftovers integration).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x Large Pasta Pot
- [ ] 1x 12" Skillet (The Sauce Bay)
- [ ] 1x Colander

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
- **The Vehicle:** 1 lb Spaghetti or Penne.
- **The Catalyst:** 1 jar (24oz) High-Quality Marinara (or 1 can crushed tomatoes).
- **Reinforcements:** 1 lb Ground Beef or Italian Sausage.
- **Aromatics:** 1 Onion (diced), 3 cloves Garlic (minced), 1 tsp Dried Oregano.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Hydration)
1.  **Boil:** Fill pot with water. Add a "Handful" of salt (Water should taste like the ocean). 
2.  **Deployment:** Once boiling, add pasta. Set timer for 1 minute *less* than the box instructions (Al Dente).

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Meat Sauce)
1.  **Sear:** Brown the meat in the skillet over high heat. Drain excess fat if necessary.
2.  **Sauté:** Add onions and garlic. Sauté for 3 minutes.
3.  **The Simmer:** Pour in the sauce and oregano. Drop heat to LOW. 

### Phase 3: The Integration Maneuver
1.  **The Extraction:** Before draining pasta, save 1/2 cup of the "Pasta Water" (Liquid Starch).
2.  **Merge:** Add drained pasta and the saved water to the skillet. Toss for 60 seconds. The starch binds the sauce to the noodle.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** Never rinse your pasta. Rinsing removes the "Adhesion Coating" (starch) that holds the sauce.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Top with a "Rain of Parmesan" for maximum morale.
