# SOP-040: OPERATION TUBER LOAD
**Objective:** High-calorie execution of a "Fully Loaded" Baked Potato.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike (Side-Op)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 60 Minutes (Passive) / 10 Minutes (Active)
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 45–60 Minutes
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-001 (Steak) or SOP-013 (Brisket leftovers).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x Baking Sheet
- [ ] 1x Fork (The Piercer)
- [ ] 1x Aluminum Foil (Secondary Objective: For "Soft Armor" finish)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*   **The Target:** Large Russet Potatoes (1 per personnel).
*   **Lubricant:** Olive Oil.
*   **The Payload:** Butter, Sour Cream, Shredded Cheddar, Chopped Chives, Cooked Bacon Bits.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Skin Prep)
1.  **Piercing:** Use the fork to poke 6–8 holes in the potato (Prevents "Pressure Explosion").
2.  **Armor Coating:** Rub skin with olive oil and a heavy coating of Kosher salt. This creates the "Elite Crust."

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Roast)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Bake at **400°F (204°C)** directly on the oven rack for maximum airflow.
2.  **Dwell:** Cook for 45–60 minutes. Target success: Potato yields easily when squeezed (Use an oven mitt).

### Phase 3: Extraction (The Loadout)
1.  **The Cut:** Slice down the center. Push the ends together to "bloom" the interior.
2.  **Loading:** Aggressively apply butter, cheese, and bacon while the interior is at maximum thermal capacity.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** Never wrap in foil if you want crispy skin. Foil steams the skin, resulting in "Cafeteria-style" soggy texture.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Use leftover SOP-016 (Chili) as a primary topping for a "High-Calorie Meal-in-One."
