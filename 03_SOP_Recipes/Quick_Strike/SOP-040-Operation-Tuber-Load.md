# SOP-040: OPERATION TUBER LOAD
**Objective:** High-calorie execution of a "Fully Loaded" Baked Potato.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Difficulty:** 1/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 60 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 5 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 45–55 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per fully loaded potato):** P: 12g | C: 40g | F: 18g | ~360 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-001 (Primal Sear), SOP-013 (Texas Black-Gold leftovers), SOP-016 (Iron-Clad Chili).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Baking Sheet (or place directly on oven rack)
- [ ] 1x Fork (The Piercer)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 1 Personnel (repeat per person):*
- **The Target:** 1 Large Russet Potato
- **Skin Lubricant:** 1 tsp Olive Oil, 1 tsp Kosher Salt (for the exterior)
- **The Payload:** 2 tbsp Butter, 2 tbsp Sour Cream, 1/4 cup Shredded Cheddar, 1 tbsp Chopped Chives, 2 strips Cooked Bacon (crumbled)

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Skin Prep)
1.  **Piercing:** Use the fork to poke 8–10 holes all over the potato. This vents steam and prevents a pressure build-up that can cause the potato to rupture.
2.  **Armor Coating:** Rub the skin with olive oil and apply a heavy coating of kosher salt. This creates the "Elite Crust" — the salty, crackling skin that is half the reason to eat a baked potato.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Roast)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Preheat oven to **400°F (204°C)**.
2.  **Deployment:** Place potato directly on the oven rack (not wrapped) for maximum airflow.
3.  **Dwell:** Bake for 45–55 minutes until the potato yields easily when squeezed with a gloved hand.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **The Cut:** Slice lengthwise down the center. Push both ends toward each other to "bloom" the interior — this creates maximum surface area for the payload.
2.  **Loading:** Deploy butter first (direct contact with the hot interior is maximum melt). Follow with cheese, then sour cream, then bacon and chives.
3.  **Serve Immediately:** Loaded potatoes lose heat fast. Deploy to the table within 2 minutes.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   Never wrap the potato in foil if crispy skin is the objective. Foil traps steam and converts the skin to a soft, cafeteria-style failure.
*   The "squeeze test" is the only reliable doneness check. A fork inserted through the skin can fool you — the center may still be hard while the outer 1/2 inch is done.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Load with leftover SOP-016 (Iron-Clad Chili), shredded cheddar, and a dollop of sour cream for a "High-Calorie Meal-in-One" that requires zero additional preparation.
