# SOP-018: OPERATION YOGURT ARMOR
**Objective:** Deployment of a high-utility sponge cake (Bizcocho) using the 3-2-1 field ratio — no measuring cups required.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Difficulty:** 1/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 45 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 5 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 35 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per slice):** P: 5g | C: 32g | F: 12g | ~240 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-036 (Liquid Fuel).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 9-inch Round Cake Pan or Loaf Pan
- [ ] 1x Mixing Bowl and Whisk
- [ ] **The Measure:** The empty 125g/4oz yogurt container

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*The "3-2-1" Field Ratio — all units measured with the empty yogurt container:*
- **[1] Unit:** Plain or Lemon Yogurt (the container itself is now the standard unit)
- **[1] Unit:** Vegetable Oil
- **[2] Units:** Granulated Sugar
- **[3] Units:** All-Purpose Flour
- **Reinforcements:** 3 Eggs, 1 tbsp Baking Powder, Lemon Zest (optional)

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Mix)
1.  **Calibrate:** Dump the yogurt into the mixing bowl. Clean the container — this is now the "Standard Measure" for all remaining ingredients.
2.  **Combine:** Add 3 eggs and 2 measures of sugar. Whisk until frothy and pale.
3.  **Lubrication:** Whisk in 1 measure of vegetable oil until fully incorporated.
4.  **Bulk Loading:** Whisk in 3 measures of flour and the baking powder until just combined. Do not over-mix.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Bake)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Preheat oven to **350°F (177°C)**.
2.  **Deployment:** Grease the pan and pour in the batter.
3.  **Dwell:** Bake for 30–35 minutes. Verify mission success with the "Toothpick Test" — insert into the center; it must exit clean.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Cool Down:** Remove from oven. Let the cake rest in the pan for 10 minutes before turning out onto a wire rack.
2.  **Deployment:** Serve at room temperature. Stores well wrapped at room temperature for 3 days.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   The empty yogurt container is the only tool required for measurement. Any 125g container produces the correct ratio.
*   Do not over-mix once the flour is added — gluten development will make the cake dense instead of tender.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Add lemon zest and a squeeze of fresh lemon juice to the batter for "Citrus Reinforcement." The acid activates the baking powder for a higher rise.
