# SOP-018: OPERATION YOGURT ARMOR
**Objective:** Deployment of a high-utility sponge cake (Bizcocho) using the 3-2-1 Ratio.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike (Zero-Scale Mission)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 45 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 5 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 35 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per slice):** P: 5g | C: 32g | F: 12g | ~240 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5 (No measuring cups required)
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-036 (Coffee).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 9-inch Round Cake Pan or Loaf Pan
- [ ] 1x Mixing Bowl and Whisk
- [ ] **The Measure:** The empty 125g/4oz yogurt container.

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*The "3-2-1" Field Ratio:*
- **[1] Unit:** Plain or Lemon Yogurt (use the container to measure the rest).
- **[1] Unit:** Vegetable Oil.
- **[2] Units:** Granulated Sugar.
- **[3] Units:** All-Purpose Flour.
- **Reinforcements:** 3 Eggs, 1 tbsp Baking Powder, Lemon Zest (optional).

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Mix)
1.  **Empty the Container:** Dump the yogurt into the bowl. Clean the container—this is now your "Standard Measure."
2.  **Combine:** Add 3 eggs and 2 measures of sugar. Whisk until frothy.
3.  **Lubrication:** Whisk in 1 measure of oil.
4.  **Bulk Loading:** Whisk in 3 measures of flour and the baking powder.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Cook)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Preheat oven to **350°F (177°C)**.
2.  **Deployment:** Grease the pan and pour in the batter.
3.  **Dwell:** Bake for 30–35 minutes. Verify success with the "Toothpick Test" (must come out clean).

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** This is the most efficient cake in the manual. No scale or measuring cups makes it ideal for "Remote Ops."
*   **Tactical Variation:** Fold in 1 measure of chocolate chips or blueberries for a morale boost.
