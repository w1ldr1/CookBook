# SOP-029: OPERATION SOUTHERN COMFORT
**Objective:** Layered deployment of No-Bake Banana Pudding for high-morale sustainment.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Difficulty:** 1/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 4 Hours 20 Minutes (includes cold soak)
*   **Infil (Prep):** 20 Minutes (Active)
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 0 Minutes (Cold-Set Mission)
*   **Target Macros (per serving):** P: 5g | C: 58g | F: 14g | ~380 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-023 (Delta Slow / Southern theme).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Large Glass Bowl or Trifle Dish
- [ ] 1x Mixing Bowl and Whisk

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 8–10 Personnel:*
- **The Base:** 1 box (5oz) Instant Vanilla Pudding mix, 2 cups Cold Milk
- **The Amplifiers:** 1 can (14oz) Sweetened Condensed Milk, 1 tub (12oz) Whipped Topping (thawed)
- **The Payload:** 4 Bananas (sliced), 1 box Nilla Wafers

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Cream)
1.  **Pudding Build:** Combine pudding mix and cold milk. Whisk for 2 minutes until thickened. Stir in sweetened condensed milk.
2.  **The Fold:** Gently fold in half of the whipped topping until just combined. Do not deflate it.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Layers)
1.  **Layer 1:** Line the bottom of the bowl with Nilla Wafers.
2.  **Layer 2:** Add a layer of sliced bananas.
3.  **Layer 3:** Pour 1/3 of the pudding mixture over the top.
4.  **Repeat:** Continue layering wafers, bananas, and pudding until all supplies are exhausted.
5.  **Cap:** Spread the remaining whipped topping over the final layer.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Cold Soak:** Cover and refrigerate for a minimum of 4 hours. This allows the wafers to absorb moisture and transition from crisp to "cake-like."
2.  **Deployment:** Crush a few extra wafers and scatter over the top immediately before serving for "Tactical Texture."

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   The 4-hour cold soak is the mission. The wafers must fully soften into a layered pudding cake — serving too early produces a crunchy, disconnected texture.
*   Sweetened condensed milk added to the pudding base is the key to the ultra-rich "Southern" flavor profile. Do not substitute.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Double the banana quantity for maximum potassium loading and a more pronounced fruit presence in every bite.
