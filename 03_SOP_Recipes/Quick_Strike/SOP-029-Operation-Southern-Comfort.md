# SOP-029: OPERATION SOUTHERN COMFORT
**Objective:** Layered deployment of Banana Pudding (No-Bake).
**Rank:** Recruit
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike (Assembly Ops)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 20 Minutes (Active) + 4 Hours (Cold Soak)
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5 (No heat required)
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-023 (Red Beans/Southern theme).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x Large Glass Bowl or Trifle Dish
- [ ] 1x Hand Mixer or Whisk

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
- **The Base:** 1 box (5oz) Instant Vanilla Pudding mix, 2 cups Cold Milk.
- **The Reinforcements:** 1 can (14oz) Sweetened Condensed Milk, 1 tub (12oz) Whipped Topping (thawed).
- **The Payload:** 4 Bananas (sliced), 1 box Nilla Wafers.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Cream)
1.  **Mix:** Combine pudding mix and milk. Whisk for 2 mins. Fold in condensed milk.
2.  **The Fold:** Gently fold in half of the whipped topping.

### Phase 2: Assembly (Layering)
1.  **Layer 1:** Line bottom of bowl with wafers.
2.  **Layer 2:** Add a layer of sliced bananas.
3.  **Layer 3:** Pour 1/3 of the pudding mixture over the top.
4.  **Repeat:** Continue layers until supplies are exhausted. Finish with remaining whipped topping.

### Phase 3: Extraction
1.  **Cold Soak:** Refrigerate for 4+ hours. This allows the wafers to soften into a "cake-like" state.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** Crushing a few wafers on top before serving provides "Tactical Texture."
*   **Secondary Objective:** Double the bananas for "Potassium Loading."
