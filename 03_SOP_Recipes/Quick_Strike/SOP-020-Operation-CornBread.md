# SOP-020: OPERATION CORNBREAD
**Objective:** Deployment of high-density Southern-style skillet cornbread.
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike (Side-Op)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 30 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 5 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 20–25 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per slice):** P: 5g | C: 28g | F: 12g | ~240 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5 (Basic Maneuvers)

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x 10" Cast Iron Skillet (The Heavy Armor)
- [ ] 1x Mixing Bowl and Whisk

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
- **The Dry Payload:** 1 cup Yellow Cornmeal, 1 cup All-Purpose Flour, 1/4 cup Sugar (Optional), 1 tbsp Baking Powder, 1 tsp Salt.
- **The Liquid Catalyst:** 1 cup Buttermilk, 1 Large Egg.
- **Lubricant:** 1/2 stick (4 tbsp) Unsalted Butter.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Thermal Calibration
1.  **Preheat:** Set oven to **400°F (204°C)**.
2.  **Pan Prep:** Place the butter in the cast iron skillet and slide it into the oven while it preheats.

### Phase 2: Integration
1.  **Mix:** Whisk dry supplies in the bowl. Add buttermilk and egg. Stir until just combined.
2.  **The Sizzle:** Remove the hot skillet from the oven. Swirl the melted butter to coat the sides. 
3.  **Deployment:** Pour the excess melted butter into the batter, stir quickly, and pour the batter back into the screaming hot skillet.

### Phase 3: Engagement
1.  **Dwell:** Bake for 20–25 minutes until the edges are dark brown and a toothpick comes out clean.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Note 01:** Pouring batter into a hot, buttered skillet creates the "Armor" (crispy crust) that separates professional ops from amateur work.
*   **Variation:** Add diced jalapeños or corn kernels for "Advanced Recon" flavor.
