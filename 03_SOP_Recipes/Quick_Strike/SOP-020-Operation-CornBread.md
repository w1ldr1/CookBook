# SOP-020: OPERATION CORNBREAD
**Objective:** Deployment of high-density Southern-style skillet cornbread.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Difficulty:** 1/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 30 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 5 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 20–25 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per slice):** P: 5g | C: 28g | F: 12g | ~240 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-016 (Iron-Clad Chili), SOP-023 (Delta Slow).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 10" Cast Iron Skillet (The Heavy Armor)
- [ ] 1x Mixing Bowl and Whisk

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 8 Personnel (slices):*
- **The Dry Payload:** 1 cup Yellow Cornmeal, 1 cup All-Purpose Flour, 1/4 cup Sugar, 1 tbsp Baking Powder, 1 tsp Salt
- **The Liquid Catalyst:** 1 cup Buttermilk, 1 Large Egg
- **Lubricant:** 1/2 stick (4 tbsp) Unsalted Butter

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Preparation)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Set oven to **400°F (204°C)**.
2.  **Pan Prep:** Place the butter in the cast iron skillet and slide it into the oven while it preheats. The butter will melt and the pan will become scorching hot — this is the objective.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Bake)
1.  **Mix:** Whisk all dry supplies in the bowl. Add buttermilk and egg. Stir until just combined — do not overwork.
2.  **The Sizzle:** Remove the hot skillet from the oven. Swirl the melted butter to coat the sides.
3.  **Deployment:** Pour the excess melted butter into the batter, stir quickly, and pour the batter back into the screaming hot skillet. You should hear a sizzle on contact.
4.  **Dwell:** Bake for 20–25 minutes until edges are dark brown and a toothpick exits clean.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Extraction:** Remove from oven and let rest in the skillet for 5 minutes before slicing.
2.  **Serve:** Slice into wedges directly from the skillet. Deploy alongside SOP-016 or SOP-023.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   Pre-heating the skillet with butter is non-negotiable — it creates the "Armor" bottom crust and prevents sticking simultaneously.
*   Buttermilk creates a tender crumb via acid-leavening reaction with baking powder. Whole milk is an inferior substitute.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Fold in 1/2 cup of frozen corn kernels and 1/4 cup of diced jalapeño for "Internal Texture" and heat.
