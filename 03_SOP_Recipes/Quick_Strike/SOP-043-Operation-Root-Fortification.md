# SOP-043: OPERATION ROOT FORTIFICATION
**Objective:** Deployment of high-heat roasted root vegetables (Carrots/Potatoes).
**Rank:** Recruit
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike (Side-Op)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 35 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 10 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 20–25 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per serving):** P: 2g | C: 22g | F: 7g
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-030 (Pork Tenderloin) or SOP-007 (High-Value Bird).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x Half-Sheet Pan
- [ ] 1x Large Mixing Bowl

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*   **The Target:** 1 lb Carrots (peeled and sliced into "diagonal batons") or Baby Potatoes (halved).
*   **Lubricant:** 2 tbsp Olive Oil.
*   **Flavor Ordnance:** 1 tbsp Honey or Maple Syrup, 1 tsp Salt, 1/2 tsp Cumin or Dried Thyme.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Preparation)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Preheat oven to **425°F (218°C)**.
2.  **Standardization:** Ensure all vegetable pieces are of similar thickness to ensure "Synchronized Doneness."
3.  **The Toss:** Combine all supplies in the mixing bowl. Ensure 100% oil/syrup coverage.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Roast)
1.  **Deployment:** Spread in a single layer on the sheet pan.
2.  **Dwell:** Roast for 20–25 minutes. 
3.  **Agitation:** Halfway through, shake the pan to ensure caramelization on all sides.

### Phase 3: Extraction
1.  **Target ID:** Look for "Blistered" edges and a fork-tender interior. 

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** The diagonal cut on the carrots increases "Surface Area," allowing for more caramelization (flavor) in less time.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Top with fresh parsley or cilantro for a "High-Contrast" finish.
