# SOP-043: OPERATION ROOT FORTIFICATION
**Objective:** Deployment of high-heat roasted root vegetables with caramelized honey glaze.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Difficulty:** 1/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 35 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 10 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 20–25 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per serving):** P: 2g | C: 22g | F: 7g | ~160 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-030 (Lean Strike), SOP-007 (High-Value Bird).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Half-Sheet Pan
- [ ] 1x Large Mixing Bowl

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 2–4 Personnel:*
- **The Target:** 1 lb Carrots (peeled and cut into diagonal batons) or Baby Potatoes (halved)
- **Lubricant:** 2 tbsp Olive Oil
- **Flavor Ordnance:** 1 tbsp Honey or Maple Syrup, 1 tsp Kosher Salt, 1/2 tsp Cumin or Dried Thyme

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Preparation)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Preheat oven to **425°F (218°C)**.
2.  **Standardization:** Ensure all vegetable pieces are of uniform thickness. Inconsistent sizing produces "Synchronized Doneness Failure" — some pieces burnt, some raw.
3.  **The Toss:** Combine all supplies in the mixing bowl. Toss until every surface has full oil and honey coverage.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Roast)
1.  **Deployment:** Spread in a single layer on the sheet pan — no overlapping. Crowding causes steaming, not caramelizing.
2.  **Dwell:** Roast for 20–25 minutes.
3.  **Agitation:** At the 12-minute mark, shake or flip the vegetables for even browning on all surfaces.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Target Verification:** Look for "Blistered" charred edges and a fork-tender interior with no resistance. The caramelized honey edges should be dark, not black.
2.  **Deployment:** Serve immediately as a side-op alongside any protein mission.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   The diagonal cut on carrots significantly increases surface area compared to a straight cut. More surface area = more Maillard reaction = more flavor per piece.
*   Honey burns faster than other sugars. Watch the pan closely after the 20-minute mark — the line between caramelized and scorched is narrow.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Top with fresh flat-leaf parsley or cilantro after extraction for "High-Contrast" visual finish and a fresh herbal note.
