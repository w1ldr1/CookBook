# SOP-034: OPERATION GREEN PERIMETER
**Objective:** High-speed execution of pan-seared Asparagus or Green Beans.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Difficulty:** 1/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 10 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 3 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 5–7 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per serving):** P: 3g | C: 8g | F: 5g | ~90 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-008 (Silver Scale), SOP-042 (Iron-Seared Thighs).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 12" Skillet (stainless or carbon steel for blistering)
- [ ] 1x Tongs

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 2 Personnel:*
- **The Target:** 1 bunch Asparagus (trimmed) or 1 lb Fresh Green Beans (stems removed)
- **Lubricant:** 1 tbsp Olive Oil, 1 tsp Unsalted Butter
- **Seasoning:** Kosher Salt, Black Pepper, 1/2 tsp Garlic Powder
- **Finish:** 1 tsp Fresh Lemon Juice or Balsamic Glaze

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Preparation)
1.  **Standardize:** For asparagus — snap the woody end off each spear where it naturally breaks. For green beans — trim the stem end with a knife.
2.  **Dry:** Pat vegetables dry. Moisture on the surface steams instead of sears.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Sear)
1.  **Thermal Contact:** Set skillet to Medium-High. Add oil and butter.
2.  **Deployment:** Lay vegetables in a single layer. Do not crowd — crowding causes steaming.
3.  **Dwell:** Cook for 5–7 minutes, tossing occasionally with tongs. The target texture is "Blistered" skin with a "Crisp-Tender" interior — not limp, not raw.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **The Polish:** Squeeze fresh lemon juice over the top immediately after plating. Acid brightens the green color and cuts the richness of the oil.
2.  **Deployment:** Serve immediately as a side-op alongside any protein mission.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   High heat and a short dwell time are the two non-negotiables. Low-heat vegetable cooking produces soggy, dull rations with no texture.
*   Do not cover the pan — trapped steam is the enemy of the blistered exterior.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Top with toasted slivered almonds and a drizzle of balsamic glaze for "Visual Intel" and textural contrast.
