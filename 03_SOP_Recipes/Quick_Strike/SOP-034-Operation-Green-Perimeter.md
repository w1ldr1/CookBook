# SOP-034: OPERATION GREEN PERIMETER
**Objective:** High-speed execution of pan-seared Asparagus or Green Beans.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike (Vegetable Side-Op)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 10 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 5–7 Minutes
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-008 (Salmon), SOP-042 (Thighs).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x 12" Skillet

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
- **The Target:** 1 bunch Asparagus (trimmed) or 1 lb Fresh Green Beans.
- **Lubricant:** 1 tbsp Olive Oil, 1 tsp Butter.
- **Seasoning:** Salt, Pepper, Garlic Powder.
- **Finish:** 1 tsp Lemon Juice or Balsamic Glaze.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Trim)
1.  **Standardize:** Snap the woody ends off the asparagus or trim the stems from the beans.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Sear)
1.  **Heat:** Set skillet to medium-high with oil and butter.
2.  **Deployment:** Lay veggies in a single layer.
3.  **Dwell:** Cook for 5–7 minutes, tossing occasionally. The goal is "Blistered" skin and a "Crisp-Tender" interior.

### Phase 3: Exfil
1.  **The Polish:** Squeeze lemon juice over the top. The acid will "brighten" the green color.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** High heat and a short dwell keep the "Green Integrity" (crunch).
*   **Secondary Objective:** Top with toasted slivered almonds for "Visual Intel."
