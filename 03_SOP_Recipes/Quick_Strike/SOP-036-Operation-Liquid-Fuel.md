# SOP-036: OPERATION LIQUID FUEL
**Objective:** Rapid deployment of high-performance stimulants and recovery fluids.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Difficulty:** 1/5
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 5–10 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 2 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 4 Minutes (Coffee) / 1 Minute (Smoothie)
*   **Target Macros (Smoothie):** P: 40g | C: 25g | F: 12g | ~380 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-012 (Oats and Ammo).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x French Press or Pour-Over Kit (The Extractor)
- [ ] 1x High-Speed Blender (The Centrifuge)
- [ ] 1x Insulated Flask (The Reservoir)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 1 Personnel:*
- **Mission A (Coffee):** 2 tbsp Fresh Ground Beans, 12oz Filtered Water (200°F / just off-boil)
- **Mission B (Smoothie):** 1.5 scoops Whey Protein, 1 cup Frozen Berries, 1 tbsp Almond Butter, 8oz Water or Milk

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Preparation)
1.  **Mission Selection:** Choose Mission A (Coffee) or Mission B (Smoothie) based on operational requirements.
2.  **Coffee:** Measure grounds. Heat water to 200°F (remove from boil for 30 seconds if using a kettle).
3.  **Smoothie:** Stage all smoothie ingredients within arm's reach of the blender. Add liquid first to prevent powder from sticking to the blade base.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Extraction)
1.  **Mission A — Coffee:** Add grounds to French Press. Pour 1/4 of the water over the grounds and wait 30 seconds (The Bloom — degasses the grounds for a cleaner extraction). Pour remaining water. Dwell for 4 minutes. Press slowly and transfer to flask immediately.
2.  **Mission B — Smoothie:** Blend on High for 45 seconds until fully pulverized. No protein chunks, no ice shards.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Coffee:** Transfer to insulated flask. Consume within 30 minutes for peak extraction quality.
2.  **Smoothie:** Pour and consume immediately. Rinse the blender within 60 seconds — dried protein is a sanitation hazard.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   The "Bloom" step in French Press is not optional — it degasses the CO2 from fresh grounds, resulting in a cleaner, less bitter extraction.
*   Water temperature matters: boiling water (212°F) over-extracts and turns coffee bitter. 200°F is the approved thermal window.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Add 1 tbsp of MCT Oil or unsalted butter to finished coffee and blend for 15 seconds (Bulletproof Protocol) for sustained cognitive engagement and appetite suppression.
