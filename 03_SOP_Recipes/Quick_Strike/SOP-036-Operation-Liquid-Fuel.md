# SOP-036: OPERATION LIQUID FUEL
**Objective:** Rapid deployment of high-performance stimulants and recovery fluids.
**Rank:** Recruit (Basic Maneuver)
**Mission Type:** Quick Strike (Fueling Ops)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 5–10 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 2 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 4 Minutes (Coffee) / 1 Minute (Smoothie)
*   **Target Macros (Smoothie):** P: 40g | C: 25g | F: 12g | ~380 Cal
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-012 (Oats and Ammo integration).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x French Press or Pour-Over Kit (The Extractor)
- [ ] 1x High-Speed Blender (The Centrifuge)
- [ ] 1x Insulated Flask (The Reservoir)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*   **Mission A (Coffee):** 2 tbsp Fresh Ground Beans, 12oz Filtered Water (200°F).
*   **Mission B (Smoothie):** 1.5 scoops Whey Protein, 1 cup Frozen Berries, 1 tbsp Almond Butter, 8oz Water or Milk.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Mission A (High-Performance Coffee)
1.  **Thermal Extraction:** Heat water to 200°F (just off-boil). 
2.  **Loading:** Add coffee grounds to the French Press. 
3.  **The Bloom:** Pour 1/4 of the water over grounds. Wait 30 seconds for "degassing."
4.  **Deployment:** Pour remaining water. Let dwell for 4 minutes.
5.  **Extraction:** Press slowly. Transfer to flask immediately to prevent over-extraction.

### Phase 2: Mission B (Rapid Refuel Smoothie)
1.  **Loading:** Add liquid first (prevents powder sticking to the base). Add protein, berries, and fat source.
2.  **Engagement:** Blend on High for 45 seconds until pulverized.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** Rinse the blender immediately. Dried protein is a "Biological Hazard." 
*   **Secondary Objective:** (Tactical Coffee): Add 1 tbsp of MCT Oil to coffee for "Cognitive Engagement."
