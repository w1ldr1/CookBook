# SOP-009: OPERATION GRAIN SILO
**Objective:** Batch production of high-stability carbohydrates for weekly sustainment.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Difficulty:** 1/5
**Mission Type:** Field Rations

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 25 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 2 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 18 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per 1 cup):** P: 5g | C: 45g | F: 1g | ~210 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-002 (Ground Combat Bowl), SOP-035 (Red Sauce), SOP-047 (High-Heat Curry).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Heavy-bottomed Pot with a tight-fitting lid
- [ ] 1x Fine Mesh Strainer (The "Filter")
- [ ] 1x Fork (The "Aerator")

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 6 Rations:*
- **The Target:** 2 cups Jasmine or Basmati Rice
- **Liquid Ammunition:** 3 cups Water or Chicken Stock (for enhanced flavor)
- **Catalyst:** 1 tsp Kosher Salt, 1 tsp Butter or Oil (prevents foaming)

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Rinse)
1.  **Filtration:** Place rice in the strainer. Rinse under cold water until the water runs clear. This removes excess starch and prevents "Sticky Rations."

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Cook)
1.  **Combination:** Add rice, liquid, salt, and fat to the pot.
2.  **Boil Phase:** Bring to a rolling boil over high heat, uncovered.
3.  **Low-Thermal Dwell:** Once boiling, turn heat to **LOW** and cover with the lid.
4.  **No-Intel Period:** **DO NOT LIFT THE LID.** Lifting releases steam and ruins the hydration cycle. Set timer for 15 minutes.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Resting:** Kill the heat. Let the pot sit, covered, for 5 minutes.
2.  **Aeration:** Remove lid and fluff with a fork to release residual steam.
3.  **Storage:** Spread rice on a sheet pan to cool quickly before placing in airtight containers for the week.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   Rinsing the rice until the water runs clear is mandatory to prevent "Sticky Rations."
*   Do not lift the lid during the dwell. Breaking the vapor seal results in uneven hydration and mission failure.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Add 1 smashed garlic clove and 1 bay leaf to the water during the boil phase for enhanced fragrance and flavor.
