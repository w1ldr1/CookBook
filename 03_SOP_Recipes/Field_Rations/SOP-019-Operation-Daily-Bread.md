# SOP-019: OPERATION DAILY BREAD
**Objective:** Deployment of high-quality crusty bread with zero manual labor (No-Knead method).
**Rank:** Sergeant
**Difficulty:** 1/5
**Mission Type:** Field Rations

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 18 Hours (Passive)
*   **Infil (Prep):** 5 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 45 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per slice):** P: 4g | C: 22g | F: 0.5g | ~110 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-028 (Salvage Rations), SOP-033 (Pan Sauce), SOP-001 (Primal Sear).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 6qt Dutch Oven (Enameled or Raw Cast Iron)
- [ ] 1x Large Mixing Bowl
- [ ] Parchment Paper

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 1 Loaf (8 slices):*
- **The Core:** 3 cups All-Purpose or Bread Flour
- **The Catalyst:** 1/4 tsp Active Dry Yeast
- **The Mineral:** 1.5 tsp Kosher Salt
- **Hydration:** 1.5 cups Warm Water

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The 18-Hour Ferment)
1.  **Integration:** Combine flour, salt, and yeast in the bowl. Pour in the warm water.
2.  **Agitation:** Stir until a "shaggy" ball forms. **DO NOT KNEAD.**
3.  **Covert Ops:** Cover the bowl tightly with plastic wrap. Leave at room temperature for 12–18 hours. The dough will double in size and become bubbly with visible fermentation activity.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Bake)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Place the empty Dutch oven (with lid ON) in the oven. Preheat to **450°F (232°C)**.
2.  **Staging:** Scrape the dough onto a piece of parchment paper. It will be sticky — use well-floured hands. Shape loosely into a ball.
3.  **Loading:** Carefully remove the scorching Dutch oven. Lower the dough (on parchment) into the pot.
4.  **Covered Dwell:** Bake for 30 minutes with the lid ON. The trapped steam creates the "Armor" crust.
5.  **Final Exposure:** Remove the lid and bake for another 15 minutes to achieve a deep golden-brown crust.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Extraction:** Remove loaf from pot and place on a wire rack.
2.  **The Hardest Part:** Wait 30 minutes before slicing. Cutting too early releases the internal steam and produces a gummy crumb.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   The 18-hour ferment develops gluten without any manual labor. Time is the only ingredient that cannot be skipped.
*   Preheating the Dutch oven is mandatory — the explosion of steam from the cold dough hitting a scorching hot surface is what creates the signature blistered crust.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Add 1 tbsp of dried rosemary and 1 tbsp of flaky sea salt to the dry mix for an elevated "Aromatic Ops" profile.
