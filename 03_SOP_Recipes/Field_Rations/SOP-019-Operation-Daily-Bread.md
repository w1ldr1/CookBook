# SOP-019: OPERATION DAILY BREAD
**Objective:** Deployment of high-quality crusty bread with zero manual labor (No-Knead).
**Mission Type:** Field Rations (High-Endurance/Passive)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 18 Hours (Passive)
*   **Infil (Prep):** 5 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 45 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per slice):** P: 4g | C: 22g | F: 0.5g | ~110 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5 (Requires patience, not skill)

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x 6qt Dutch Oven (Enameled or Raw Cast Iron)
- [ ] 1x Large Mixing Bowl
- [ ] Parchment Paper

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
- **The Core:** 3 cups All-Purpose or Bread Flour.
- **The Catalyst:** 1/4 tsp Active Dry Yeast.
- **The Mineral:** 1.5 tsp Kosher Salt.
- **Hydration:** 1.5 cups Warm Water.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The 18-Hour Ferment)
1.  **Integration:** Combine flour, salt, and yeast in the bowl. Pour in the water.
2.  **Agitation:** Stir until a "shaggy" ball forms. **DO NOT KNEAD.**
3.  **Covert Ops:** Cover the bowl tightly with plastic wrap. Leave at room temperature for 12–18 hours. The dough will double in size and become bubbly.

### Phase 2: Engagement (Thermal Prep)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Place the empty Dutch oven (with lid) into the oven and preheat to **450°F (232°C)**.
2.  **Staging:** Scrape the dough onto a piece of parchment paper. It will be sticky; use extra flour on your hands.

### Phase 3: Deployment
1.  **Loading:** Carefully remove the hot Dutch oven. Drop the dough (on the parchment) into the pot. 
2.  **Covered Dwell:** Bake for 30 minutes with the lid ON. This traps steam and creates the "Armor" (crust).
3.  **Final Exposure:** Remove the lid and bake for another 15 minutes to brown the crust.

### Phase 4: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Extraction:** Remove and place on a wire rack.
2.  **The Hardest Part:** Wait 30 minutes before slicing. Cutting too early releases the steam and makes the bread gummy.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Note 01:** For "Operation: Garlic Bread," add 1 tbsp garlic powder and dried rosemary during Phase 1.
*   **Logistics:** This bread has no preservatives. If not consumed within 48 hours, slice and freeze.
