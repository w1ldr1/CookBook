# SOP-012: OPERATION OATS AND AMMO
**Objective:** Pre-staged, high-fiber breakfast rations for zero-prep morning deployment.
**Rank:** Recruit 
**Mission Type:** Field Rations (Overnight Prep)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 5 Minutes (Active) + 6 Hours (Passive)
*   **Infil (Prep):** 5 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 0 Minutes (Cold Hydration)
*   **Target Macros (per jar):** P: 30g | C: 45g | F: 12g | ~420 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5 (No-heat mission)
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-036 (Liquid Fuel).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 2-4x Mason Jars (16oz) or airtight glass containers.
- [ ] 1x Mixing Spoon.

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 1 Ration (Repeat for batching):*
- **The Base:** 1/2 cup Rolled Oats (not instant).
- **Liquid Ammunition:** 1/2 cup Milk (Dairy or Nut-based).
- **Protein Reinforcement:** 1 scoop Vanilla or Chocolate Protein Powder.
- **The Fuel:** 1 tbsp Chia Seeds (essential for texture) and 1 tbsp Peanut Butter.
- **Finish:** 1/2 cup Frozen Berries.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Loading)
1.  **Dry Loading:** Add oats, protein powder, and chia seeds to the jar. Shake to mix.
2.  **Hydration:** Pour in the milk. Stir aggressively to ensure no protein powder "clumps" remain at the bottom.

### Phase 2: Integration
1.  **The Payload:** Drop in the peanut butter and frozen berries. Do not stir them in completely; leave them as "flavor pockets."
2.  **Sealing:** Close the lid tightly.

### Phase 3: Deployment (Exfil)
1.  **Cold Storage:** Place in refrigeration for a minimum of 6 hours.
2.  **The Morning Grab:** Remove and consume cold. No heating required.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   **Chia Hydrodynamics:** Chia seeds are the primary thickening agent. Without them, the rations will remain liquid and fail the "Oat-to-Liquid" ratio.
*   **Engagement Window:** These remain viable for 96 hours. Batch-prep 4 jars on Sunday for work-week sustainment.
**Secondary Objective:**
*   **Macro Boost:** Top with 1 tbsp of chopped walnuts or almonds immediately before consumption for added healthy fats and "Tactical Crunch."
