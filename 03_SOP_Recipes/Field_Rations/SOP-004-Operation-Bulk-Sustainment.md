# SOP-004: OPERATION BULK SUSTAINMENT
**Objective:** Batch production of high-yield pulled protein for 5+ days of rations.
**Rank:** Sergeant
**Mission Type:** Field Rations
---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 8 Hours (Passive)
*   **Target Macros (per 6oz):** P: 42g | C: 0g | F: 24g | ~380 Cal
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-014 (Coastal Infil / Tacos), SOP-020 (Cornbread).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Slow Cooker (Crock-Pot) or 6qt Dutch Oven
- [ ] 2x Heavy Duty Forks (The "Shredders")
- [ ] 1x Large Mixing Bowl

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 8-10 Rations:*
- **The Target:** 4–5 lbs Pork Butt (Shoulder), bone-in preferred.
- **The Rub:** 2 tbsp Salt, 1 tbsp Black Pepper, 1 tbsp Smoked Paprika, 1 tbsp Onion Powder.
- **Liquid Payload:** 1/2 cup Apple Cider Vinegar, 1/4 cup Water or Apple Juice.
- **Aromatics:** 1 large Onion (quartered), 4 cloves Garlic (smashed).

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Preparation)
1.  **Drying the Target:** Remove pork from packaging and pat dry. Excess moisture prevents rub adhesion.
2.  **Applying Ordinance:** Combine all "Rub" ingredients and coat the entire surface of the pork. Press firmly to ensure contact.
3.  **Staging:** Place the quartered onions and smashed garlic at the bottom of the hardware (Slow Cooker).

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Cook)
1.  **Deployment:** Place the pork butt on top of the aromatics.
2.  **Adding Liquid:** Pour the "Liquid Payload" around the sides—do not pour directly over the rub.
3.  **Thermal Dwell:** Cover and set to **LOW for 8 hours**. The target is "Fork Tender" (internal temp of 205°F).

### Phase 3: Extraction & Shredding (Exfil)
1.  **Extraction:** Carefully remove the roast from the cooker and place it into a large mixing bowl. Remove the bone (it should slide out with zero resistance).
2.  **The Shred:** Use two forks to pull the meat apart into uniform shreds.
3.  **Re-Hydration:** Add 1/2 cup of the leftover cooking juices (the "jus") back into the shredded meat to maintain moisture during storage.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** The "bone-in" shoulder provides superior thermal stability and collagen extraction.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Save the "Pork Fat" (Lard) in a jar to use as a lubricant for future "Quick Strike" egg missions.
