# SOP: HOSTAGE RESCUE BEVERAGES
**Objective:** Tactical production of high-value cocktails to facilitate social cohesion.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Mission Type:** Hostage Rescue (Social Ops)
**Compatible Systems:** SOP-003 (Tri-Tip), SOP-044 (Paella), SOP-045 (Roasted Lamb), SOP-049 (Prime Directive).

---

## 1. MISSION A: THE "NEGOTIATOR" (Old Fashioned)
*   **Supplies:** 2oz Bourbon/Rye, 2 dashes Angostura Bitters, 1 Sugar Cube (or 1/2 tsp syrup), Orange Peel.
*   **Execution:** Muddle sugar and bitters. Add ice and whiskey. Stir for 30 seconds.
*   **Field Intel:** The "Orange Peel" must be expressed (squeezed) over the glass to release oils before dropping it in.

## 2. MISSION B: THE "RECON" (Gin & Tonic)
*   **Supplies:** 2oz Gin, 4oz Premium Tonic Water, 2 Lime Wedges.
*   **Execution:** Fill a high-ball glass with ice. Add gin. Top with tonic. Squeeze lime and drop it in.
*   **Field Intel:** Use high-quality tonic (e.g., Fever-Tree). Poor tonic is a "Supply Failure."

---

## 3. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)

**Field Intel:**
*   **Thermal Mass (Ice):** Use the largest ice cubes available for "Mission A." Large ice has less surface area, which slows the "Dilution Rate," ensuring the stimulant remains high-potency for the duration of the mission.
*   **Oil Extraction:** When expressing the orange peel, "snap" it skin-side down over the glass. The microscopic mist of citrus oils provides the initial "Aromatic Engagement" before the first sip.
*   **Carbonation Integrity:** For "Mission B," never stir aggressively after adding tonic water. Agitation neutralizes the carbonation, resulting in "Flat Rations." A single, gentle lift with a spoon is the only approved maneuver.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   **The "Sustainment" Mocktail:** For non-combatant assets or designated drivers, replace the spirit in "Mission B" with 2oz of strong-brewed chilled Hibiscus tea or a splash of Apple Cider Vinegar. It maintains the "Acid/Bitter" profile required for a sophisticated social lubricant without the alcohol payload.
