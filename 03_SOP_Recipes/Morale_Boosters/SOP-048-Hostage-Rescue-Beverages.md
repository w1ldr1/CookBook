# SOP-048: OPERATION HOSTAGE RESCUE BEVERAGES
**Objective:** Tactical production of high-value cocktails to facilitate social cohesion and Unit morale.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Difficulty:** 1/5
**Mission Type:** Morale Booster

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 5 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 2 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 0 Minutes (Assembly only)
*   **Target Macros (per cocktail):** P: 0g | C: 4g | F: 0g | ~160 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-044 (Iberian Shore), SOP-045 (Mediterranean Strike), SOP-049 (Prime Directive).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Mixing Glass or Rocks Glass (for Mission A)
- [ ] 1x High-Ball Glass (for Mission B)
- [ ] 1x Bar Spoon
- [ ] 1x Muddler or Spoon-handle (for Mission A)
- [ ] Large Ice (the largest cubes available — critical for Mission A)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 1 Personnel per mission:*
- **Mission A — The "Negotiator" (Old Fashioned):** 2oz Bourbon or Rye, 2 dashes Angostura Bitters, 1 sugar cube (or 1/2 tsp simple syrup), Orange peel
- **Mission B — The "Recon" (Gin & Tonic):** 2oz Gin, 4oz Premium Tonic Water (Fever-Tree or equivalent), 2 Lime wedges

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Preparation)
1.  **Mission Selection:** Choose Mission A or Mission B based on operational context.
2.  **Ice Staging:** For Mission A, source the largest ice cube available. For Mission B, fill the high-ball glass with ice to the rim.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Build)

**Mission A — The "Negotiator" (Old Fashioned):**
1.  **Muddling:** In the rocks glass, combine sugar cube and bitters. Muddle until sugar dissolves.
2.  **The Pour:** Add one large ice cube. Pour 2oz bourbon or rye directly over the ice.
3.  **The Stir:** Stir gently for 20 seconds to chill and integrate — do not dilute aggressively.

**Mission B — The "Recon" (Gin & Tonic):**
1.  **The Pour:** Add gin over the ice-filled high-ball glass.
2.  **Top:** Pour tonic water slowly down the side of the glass to preserve carbonation.
3.  **Garnish:** Squeeze one lime wedge over the top and drop in. Do not stir.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Mission A Finish:** Express the orange peel over the glass — hold it skin-side down and snap it firmly over the drink. The microscopic citrus oil mist is the aromatic engagement layer. Drop the peel into the glass.
2.  **Deployment:** Deliver within 60 seconds of completion. No holding. Dilution and carbonation loss begin immediately.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   Large ice for Mission A: one large cube has less surface area than multiple small cubes, which slows dilution and maintains high-potency stimulant for the duration of the mission.
*   For Mission B, never stir aggressively after adding tonic. A single, gentle lift with a bar spoon is the only approved maneuver — agitation eliminates carbonation and produces "Flat Rations."

**Secondary Objective:**
*   "Sustainment Mocktail" (Mission B): replace gin with 2oz of chilled strong-brewed hibiscus tea. Maintains the acid/bitter profile for non-combatant assets or designated drivers.
