# SOP: HOSTAGE RESCUE BEVERAGES
**Objective:** Tactical production of high-value cocktails to facilitate social cohesion.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Mission Type:** Hostage Rescue (Social Ops)

---

## 1. MISSION A: THE "NEGOTIATOR" (Old Fashioned)
*   **Supplies:** 2oz Bourbon/Rye, 2 dashes Angostura Bitters, 1 Sugar Cube (or 1/2 tsp syrup), Orange Peel.
*   **Execution:** Muddle sugar and bitters. Add ice and whiskey. Stir for 30 seconds.
*   **Field Intel:** The "Orange Peel" must be expressed (squeezed) over the glass to release oils before dropping it in.

## 2. MISSION B: THE "RECON" (Gin & Tonic)
*   **Supplies:** 2oz Gin, 4oz Premium Tonic Water, 2 Lime Wedges.
*   **Execution:** Fill a high-ball glass with ice. Add gin. Top with tonic. Squeeze lime and drop it in.
*   **Field Intel:** Use high-quality tonic (e.g., Fever-Tree). Poor tonic is a "Supply Failure."

---

## 3. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Discipline:** As the Provider, you are responsible for the safety of the Unit. Monitor "Asset Intake" (Guest drinking) to ensure safe exfiltration (driving) after the mission.
