# SOP-007: OPERATION HIGH-VALUE BIRD
**Objective:** Execution of a whole-bird roast using the "Spatchcock" maneuver for maximum surface area and uniform thermal distribution.
**Rank:** Sergeant
**Mission Type:** Heavy Lift (Foundational Skill)


---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 1 Hour 15 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 15 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 45–55 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per 6oz):** P: 40g | C: 0g | F: 12g | ~280 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 3/5 (Requires manual dexterity/scissors)
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-011 (Overwatch Broth), SOP-043 (Root Fortification), SOP-015 (Cold Payload). 

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Heavy Duty Kitchen Shears (The "Breachers")
- [ ] 1x Half-Sheet Pan with Wire Rack
- [ ] 1x Digital Instant-Read Thermometer
- [ ] 1x Small Bowl (for dry rub)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 4 Personnel:*
- **The Target:** 1x Whole Roasting Chicken (4–5 lbs).
- **Lubricant:** 2 tbsp Olive Oil or melted Butter.
- **The Rub:** 1 tbsp Kosher Salt, 1 tsp Black Pepper, 1 tsp Smoked Paprika, 1 tsp Dried Thyme, 1 tsp Onion Powder.
- **Aromatics:** 1 Lemon (halved), 4 cloves Garlic (smashed).

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Breaching Maneuver)
1.  **Clear the Runway:** Remove giblets and pat the bird bone-dry with paper towels. 
2.  **The Spatchcock:** Place the bird breast-side down. Use shears to cut along both sides of the backbone. Remove the backbone entirely (store for "Stock Ops").
3.  **The Snap:** Flip the bird over. Place your palms on the breastbone and press down firmly until you hear a "crack." The bird should now lay completely flat.
4.  **Coating:** Rub the skin with oil/butter and apply the "Rub" aggressively to all surfaces, including under the skin of the breast.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Cook)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Preheat oven to **425°F (218°C)**.
2.  **Deployment:** Place the bird on the wire rack over the sheet pan. Stash the lemon halves and garlic underneath the rack.
3.  **Extraction:** Roast until the skin is golden-brown and crispy. 

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Target Verification:** Insert thermometer into the thickest part of the thigh (avoiding the bone).
    *   *Target: 165°F (74°C).* 
2.  **Rest Phase:** Remove from heat. Rest for 15 minutes to allow juices to stabilize.
3.  **Sectioning:** Use your "Primary Blade" to separate wings, drumsticks, thighs, and breasts.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   **Anatomical Strategy:** Spatchcocking (removing the spine) ensures the dark meat reaches safety temps (175°F) at the same time the breast reaches optimal juiciness (165°F).
*   **Skin Desiccation:** For elite-level skin, salt the bird and leave uncovered in the fridge for 24 hours prior to engagement.
**Secondary Objective:**
*   **Resource Recovery:** Save the extracted backbone in a freezer bag for future use in SOP-011 (Overwatch Broth).
