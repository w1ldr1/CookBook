# SOP-003: OPERATION HOSTAGE RESCUE
**Objective:** High-impact "Show-Stopper" roast to secure the satisfaction of guest assets.
**Rank:** Sergeant
**Mission Type:** Heavy Lift (Entertaining / High-Stakes)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 2 Hours
*   **Infil (Prep):** 15 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 90 Minutes (Indirect) + 6 Minutes (Direct Sear)
*   **Target Macros (per 8oz):** P: 48g | C: 0g | F: 22g | ~410 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 3/5 (Requires thermal discipline)
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-032 (Mashed Potatoes), SOP-006 (Green Cover), SOP-033 (Pan Sauce).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Wire cooling rack set inside a baking sheet (Crucial for airflow)
- [ ] 1x Digital Instant-Read Thermometer (The "Rangefinder")
- [ ] 1x 12" Cast Iron Skillet (For the Final Assault)
- [ ] 1x Sharp Slicing Knife (The "Extractor")

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 4-6 Personnel:*
- **The Target:** 1x Tri-Tip Roast (2.5 – 3.5 lbs). 
- **The Rub:** 2 tbsp Kosher Salt, 1 tbsp Coarse Black Pepper, 1 tbsp Garlic Powder, 1 tsp Dried Rosemary.
- **The Finisher:** 4 tbsp Unsalted Butter, 3 cloves Garlic (smashed), 2 sprigs Fresh Thyme.
- **Lubricant:** 2 tbsp Grapeseed or Avocado Oil.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Preparation)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Preheat oven (or smoker) to **225°F (107°C)**. 
2.  **Surface Treatment:** Pat the roast bone-dry. Trim any "silver skin" but leave the fat cap intact for flavor.
3.  **Applying the Rub:** Coat the roast aggressively on all sides with the salt/pepper/garlic mixture.

### Phase 2: The Slow Crawl (Indirect Cook)
1.  **Deployment:** Place the roast on the wire rack. Insert your thermometer probe into the thickest part.
2.  **Surveillance:** Cook until the internal temperature reaches **115°F (46°C)**. This is the "Safe Zone" before the final sear. 
3.  **Extraction:** Remove from heat. TENT LIGHTLY WITH FOIL and let it rest for 10 minutes while you prep the skillet.

### Phase 3: The Final Assault (The Sear)
1.  **Maximum Heat:** Crank the cast iron skillet to high heat with oil until it wisps smoke.
2.  **Neutralize:** Drop the roast into the pan. Sear for 2 minutes without moving it to build a heavy crust.
3.  **Butter Maneuver:** Flip the roast. Add butter, garlic, and thyme to the pan. Baste the hot butter over the meat for another 2 minutes.
4.  **Target ID:** Pull the roast when it hits an internal temp of **130°F (54°C)** for a perfect Medium-Rare.

### Phase 4: Extraction & Slicing
1.  **Mandatory Rest:** Let the roast sit for 15 minutes. **DO NOT SKIP.** The juices must be secured inside the muscle fibers.
2.  **The Extraction (Slicing):** Tri-Tip grain changes direction halfway through. Identify the grain and **slice against it** into thin strips. If you slice with the grain, the mission fails (meat will be chewy).

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)

**Field Intel:**
*   **Thermal Physics:** The "Reverse Sear" ensures the meat is edge-to-edge pink. By cooking low and slow first, you prevent the "Grey Ring" of overcooked protein typical of high-heat-only methods.
*   **Pull Point:** Carry-over cooking will raise the internal temperature by approximately 5°F during the rest. Always pull the target at 125°F for a 130°F final result.
*   **Grain Navigation:** Tri-Tip grain changes direction halfway through the roast. Identify the "seam" where the fibers rotate and adjust your slicing angle 90 degrees to ensure every slice is against the grain.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   **The Board Sauce Maneuver:** While the meat rests, finely chop 1 tbsp parsley and 1 clove garlic directly on your cutting board. Mix with 1 tbsp Olive Oil. Slice the rested meat directly onto this mixture. The escaping juices will emulsify with the herbs to create an instant, high-velocity "Board Sauce."
