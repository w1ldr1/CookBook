# SOP-049: OPERATION PRIME DIRECTIVE
**Objective:** Precision execution of a Standing Rib Roast (Prime Rib) using the "Thermal Equilibrium" (Reverse Sear) maneuver.
**Rank:** Commander (High-Value Asset Management)
**Mission Type:** Heavy Lift

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 5 Hours
*   **Infil (Prep):** 24-Hour "Dry Brine" (Recommended)
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 3–4 Hours
*   **Target Macros (per 8oz):** P: 52g | C: 0g | F: 44g | ~620 Cal
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-032 (Mashed Potatoes), SOP-033 (Pan Sauce), SOP-034 (Green Perimeter).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x Roasting Pan with V-Rack
- [ ] 1x Digital Dual-Probe Thermometer (The Sentry)
- [ ] 1x Chef's Knife (Primary Blade)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*   **The Target:** 3-bone or 4-bone Standing Rib Roast (approx. 7–9 lbs).
*   **The Armor (Rub):** 3 tbsp Kosher Salt, 2 tbsp Coarse Black Pepper, 1 tbsp Garlic Powder.
*   **The Finisher:** 4 tbsp Softened Butter, 1 tbsp Fresh Horseradish.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The 24-Hour Salt-Lock)
1.  **Preparation:** Season the roast heavily with salt 24 hours prior to engagement. 
2.  **Cold Soak:** Leave uncovered in the fridge on a rack. This dries the exterior for a superior sear and allows salt to penetrate the "Deep Tissue."

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Low-Thermal Dwell)
1.  **Calibration:** Preheat oven to **225°F (107°C)**. 
2.  **Deployment:** Place roast on the V-rack. Insert "Sentry" probe into the geometric center.
3.  **Dwell:** Cook until internal temp hits **120°F (49°C)**. This will take 3–4 hours depending on mass.
4.  **Extraction:** Remove from oven. Tent with foil. Let rest for 45 minutes (The "Stabilization Phase").

### Phase 3: The Final Assault (High-Heat Finish)
1.  **Flash Point:** Increase oven to **500°F (260°C)**.
2.  **Deployment:** Return the rested roast to the oven for 6–10 minutes until the exterior "Armor" is dark brown and bubbling.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** The long rest between the low-cook and the high-sear is what ensures edge-to-edge pink meat without a "Grey Ring" of failure.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Serve with a side of "Tactical Horseradish" (Fresh horseradish mixed with sour cream).
