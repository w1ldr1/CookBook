# SOP-049: OPERATION PRIME DIRECTIVE
**Objective:** Precision execution of a Standing Rib Roast (Prime Rib) using the Reverse Sear maneuver.
**Rank:** Commander
**Difficulty:** 5/5
**Mission Type:** Heavy Lift

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 5 Hours (including 24-hour dry brine)
*   **Infil (Prep):** 24 Hours (Dry Brine) + 15 Minutes day-of
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 3–4 Hours (Low) + 10 Minutes (High-Heat Finish)
*   **Target Macros (per 8oz):** P: 52g | C: 0g | F: 44g | ~620 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 5/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-032 (Starch Support), SOP-033 (Force Multiplier), SOP-034 (Green Perimeter).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Roasting Pan with V-Rack
- [ ] 1x Digital Dual-Probe Thermometer (The Sentry)
- [ ] 1x Chef's Knife (Primary Blade)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 6–8 Personnel:*
- **The Target:** 3-bone or 4-bone Standing Rib Roast (approx. 7–9 lbs, Prime or Choice grade)
- **The Armor (Rub):** 3 tbsp Kosher Salt, 2 tbsp Coarse Black Pepper, 1 tbsp Garlic Powder
- **The Finisher:** 4 tbsp Softened Butter, 1 tbsp Fresh Horseradish

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The 24-Hour Salt-Lock)
1.  **Dry Brine:** Season the roast heavily with salt on all surfaces 24 hours before engagement.
2.  **Cold Soak:** Leave uncovered on a rack in the refrigerator. This dries the exterior for a superior sear and allows salt to penetrate deep tissue for uniform seasoning throughout.
3.  **Day-Of Prep:** Remove from refrigerator 1 hour before cooking to allow thermal equilibration.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Reverse Sear)
1.  **Calibration:** Preheat oven to **225°F (107°C)**.
2.  **Deployment:** Place roast bone-side down on the V-rack. Insert the Sentry probe into the geometric center of the roast.
3.  **Low Thermal Dwell:** Cook until internal temp reads **120°F (49°C)** — this will take 3–4 hours depending on mass.
4.  **Stabilization Phase:** Remove from oven. Tent with foil. Rest for 45 minutes. During this time, brush the exterior with the softened butter mixture.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Flash Point:** Increase oven to **500°F (260°C)**. Allow it to fully preheat.
2.  **High-Heat Finish:** Return the rested roast to the oven for 6–10 minutes until the exterior "Armor" is deep brown and bubbling.
3.  **Carve:** Slice between the bones for individual bone-in portions, or remove bones and slice into 3/4-inch medallions. Serve with Tactical Horseradish (Secondary Objective) and SOP-033 Pan Sauce from the drippings.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   The 45-minute rest between the low cook and the high-heat finish is the technique that ensures edge-to-edge even doneness with no grey ring. The internal temp continues to rise and stabilize during the rest, then the blast of 500°F only browns the exterior without pushing the center further.
*   Buy by weight: a 3-bone roast feeds 6, a 4-bone roast feeds 8. Budget 1 lb per person before trimming.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Tactical Horseradish: mix 2 tbsp fresh grated horseradish with 1/4 cup sour cream, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, and a pinch of salt. The acid and heat cut through the fat of the Prime Rib.
