# SOP-013: OPERATION TEXAS BLACK-GOLD
**Objective:** Precision execution of a central Texas-style Beef Brisket.
**Rank:** Commander
**Mission Type:** Heavy Lift (High-Endurance/Overnight Mission)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 12–16 Hours (Endurance)
*   **Infil (Prep):** 30 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 8–12 Hours (Indirect) + 2–4 Hour Rest
*   **Target Macros (per 6oz):** P: 42g | C: 0g | F: 28g | ~420 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 5/5 (The "Black Belt" of BBQ)
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-014 (Coastal Infil), SOP-020 (Cornbread), SOP-032 (Mashed Potatoes).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Offset Smoker or Pellet Grill
- [ ] 1x Heavy Duty Pink Butcher Paper (Secondary Objective: Foil)
- [ ] 1x Dual-Probe Remote Thermometer (The "Sentry")
- [ ] 1x 12-inch Serrated Slicing Blade

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 10-12 Personnel:*
- **The Target:** 12–15 lb Whole "Packer" Brisket (Choice or Prime).
- **The Rub:** 1/2 cup Coarse 16-mesh Black Pepper, 1/2 cup Kosher Salt (The "Dalmatian Rub").
- **Fuel:** Post Oak or Hickory wood chunks.
- **Moisture Catalyst:** 1 cup Water/Apple Cider Vinegar (50/50 mix) in a spray bottle.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Trim)
1.  **Cold Start:** Trim brisket while cold. Remove "hard fat" (the deckle) and leave exactly 1/4" fat cap.
2.  **Aerodynamics:** Round off any "thin" edges that will burn during the long dwell.
3.  **Rub Application:** Apply Salt/Pepper liberally. It should look like a gravel road.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Smoke)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Stabilize your pit at **225°F - 250°F**.
2.  **Deployment:** Place brisket fat-cap UP (or toward heat source). Insert "Sentry" probe into the flat.
3.  **The Stall:** Around 160°F, the temp will stop rising as moisture evaporates. Spray with the "Moisture Catalyst" every 45 mins once the bark is set.
4.  **The Wrap:** Once the bark is "Dark Obsidian" and temp is 170°F, wrap tightly in Butcher Paper. 

### Phase 3: Extraction & The Critical Rest
1.  **Final Target:** Pull when the thermometer slides into the meat like "Room Temp Butter." 
    *   *Target: 203°F (95°C).*
2.  **The Long Rest:** Place the wrapped brisket in an empty cooler (The "Holding Bay") for 2-4 hours. This is where the magic happens.

### Phase 4: Slicing (Exfil)
1.  **Sectioning:** Cut against the grain. The "Flat" and the "Point" have grains running in different directions. Rotate 90 degrees when you hit the Point.

---


## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   **The Stall:** Do not panic when the internal temp stops rising at 160°F. This is "Evaporative Cooling." Wrap the asset to push through to the finish.
*   **Probe Feel:** The thermometer should feel like it is sliding into "Room Temperature Butter." If there is resistance, the mission is not complete.
**Secondary Objective:**
*   **Tallow Recovery:** Save the fat trimmings from Phase 1, melt them down in a separate pan, and pour the liquid "Gold" over the brisket during the wrap phase.
