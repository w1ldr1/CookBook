# SOP-023: OPERATION DELTA SLOW
**Objective:** Long-duration deployment of Louisiana Red Beans and Rice.
**Rank:** Sergeant
**Difficulty:** 2/5
**Mission Type:** Heavy Lift

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 4–6 Hours
*   **Infil (Prep):** 15 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 3.5–5 Hours
*   **Target Macros (per 1.5 cups):** P: 22g | C: 42g | F: 10g | ~350 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 2/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-009 (Grain Silo), SOP-020 (Cornbread).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 6qt Dutch Oven
- [ ] 1x Wooden Spoon

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 6–8 Personnel:*
- **The Core:** 1 lb Dried Red Kidney Beans (rinsed, soaked overnight if possible)
- **The Meat:** 1 lb Andouille Sausage (sliced), 1 Smoked Ham Hock
- **The Holy Trinity:** 1 Onion, 1 Bell Pepper, 3 stalks Celery (all diced)
- **Aromatics:** 3 cloves Garlic, 2 Bay Leaves, 1 tbsp Cajun Seasoning

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Sauté)
1.  **Sausage Sear:** Brown the Andouille in the Dutch oven over Medium-High heat. Remove and set aside — leave the rendered fat.
2.  **Trinity Deployment:** Add diced onion, bell pepper, and celery to the sausage fat. Sauté for 5–7 minutes until softened and translucent.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Long Dwell)
1.  **Full Load:** Add beans, ham hock, garlic, and bay leaves.
2.  **Hydration:** Cover with 6 cups of water or chicken stock.
3.  **Dwell:** Bring to a boil, then reduce to a low simmer. Cover and cook for 3 hours, stirring occasionally.
4.  **The Creaming Maneuver:** Use the back of a spoon to mash 10% of the beans against the side of the pot. This releases starch and creates the signature "creamy" texture.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Finish:** Add the reserved sausage back in. Simmer uncovered for 30 more minutes to tighten the sauce.
2.  **Deployment:** Serve over hot Jasmine Rice (SOP-009). Remove ham hock, shred meat, and return to pot before serving.
3.  **Storage:** Improves significantly after refrigerating overnight. Reheat with a splash of water or stock.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   Mashing 10% of the beans against the pot wall is the technique behind the "creamy base." Do not skip this step.
*   Monday is the traditional deployment day in New Orleans — red beans were put on to simmer while laundry was done. This is a "set and forget" mission.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Substitute smoked turkey wings for ham hocks for a leaner fat profile that retains the smoky depth.
