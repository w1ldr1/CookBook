# SOP-023: OPERATION DELTA SLOW
**Objective:** Long-duration deployment of Red Beans and Rice.
**Rank:** Sergeant
**Mission Type:** Heavy Lift (Endurance)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 4–6 Hours
*   **Infil (Prep):** 15 Minutes
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 2/5 (Patience required)
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-009 (Grain Silo), SOP-020 (Cornbread).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x 6qt Dutch Oven
- [ ] 1x Wooden Spoon

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
- **The Core:** 1 lb Dried Red Kidney Beans (Rinsed).
- **The Meat:** 1 lb Andouille Sausage (sliced), 1 Smoked Ham Hock.
- **The Trinity:** 1 Onion, 1 Bell Pepper, 3 stalks Celery (The Holy Trinity of Creole cooking).
- **Aromatics:** 3 cloves Garlic, 2 Bay Leaves, 1 tbsp Cajun Seasoning.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Sauté)
1.  **Sausage Sear:** Brown the Andouille in the Dutch oven. Remove and set aside.
2.  **Trinity Deployment:** Add the diced onion, pepper, and celery to the sausage fat. Sauté until soft.

### Phase 2: Full Deployment
1.  **Load:** Add beans, ham hock, garlic, and bay leaves.
2.  **Hydrate:** Cover with 6 cups of water or chicken stock.
3.  **Dwell:** Bring to a boil, then reduce to a low simmer. Cover and cook for 3 hours.

### Phase 3: The Creaming Maneuver
1.  **Texture Op:** Use the back of a spoon to mash 10% of the beans against the side of the pot. This creates the signature "creamy" texture.
2.  **Finish:** Add the sausage back in. Simmer uncovered for 30 more minutes.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** Serve over SOP-009 (Rice).
*   **Logistics:** Like chili, this is better the next day.
