# SOP-031: OPERATION RACK-AND-FILE
**Objective:** Slow-burn execution of Baby Back Ribs using the "3-2-1" deployment method.
**Rank:** Commander
**Mission Type:** Heavy Lift (Outdoor Ops / Smoker Mission)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 6 Hours
*   **Infil (Prep):** 15 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 6 Hours
*   **Target Macros (per 1/2 rack):** P: 48g | C: 12g | F: 38g | ~580 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 3/5 (Patience-intensive)
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-020 (Cornbread), SOP-032 (Mashed Potatoes).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x Smoker or Grill (Indirect Setup)
- [ ] 1x Heavy Duty Aluminum Foil (The "Shield")
- [ ] 1x Basting Brush

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
- **The Target:** 2 Racks Baby Back Ribs.
- **The Rub:** 1/4 cup Brown Sugar, 1 tbsp Smoked Paprika, 1 tbsp Black Pepper, 1 tbsp Salt, 1 tsp Garlic Powder, 1 tsp Onion Powder.
- **The Wrap Payload:** 4 tbsp Butter (cubed), 1/4 cup Honey or Brown Sugar, 1/4 cup Apple Juice.
- **The Finish:** 1 cup BBQ Sauce.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Membrane Maneuver)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Stabilize smoker at **225°F (107°C)**.
2.  **Membrane Extraction:** Use a paper towel to grip the thin white membrane on the bone side of the ribs and rip it off. Failure to do this results in "Tough Rations."
3.  **Rub Deployment:** Coat ribs aggressively on all sides.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The 3-2-1 Method)
1.  **Stage 1 (The Smoke):** Place ribs on the smoker, bone-side down. Cook for **3 hours** to set the bark and smoke profile.
2.  **Stage 2 (The Wrap):** Remove ribs. Place on foil. Add the "Wrap Payload" (butter, honey, juice). Seal tightly and return to heat for **2 hours**. This tenderizes the protein.
3.  **Stage 3 (The Glaze):** Remove from foil. Place back on grates. Brush with BBQ sauce. Cook for **1 final hour** to set the glaze.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **The Bend Test:** Pick up the rack with tongs. If it bends and the meat starts to "crack" open, the mission is a success.
2.  **Rest:** Let sit for 10 minutes before slicing between the bones.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** "Fall-off-the-bone" is a preference, but a perfect rib should have a clean "bite-through" without the rest of the meat sliding off.
*   **Logistics:** Save the bones for "SOP-011: Overwatch Broth."
