# SOP-031: OPERATION RACK-AND-FILE
**Objective:** Slow-burn execution of Baby Back Ribs using the 3-2-1 deployment method.
**Rank:** Commander
**Difficulty:** 3/5
**Mission Type:** Heavy Lift

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 6 Hours
*   **Infil (Prep):** 15 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 6 Hours
*   **Target Macros (per 1/2 rack):** P: 48g | C: 12g | F: 38g | ~580 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 3/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-020 (Cornbread), SOP-032 (Starch Support).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Smoker or Grill (Indirect heat setup)
- [ ] 1x Heavy Duty Aluminum Foil (The "Shield")
- [ ] 1x Basting Brush
- [ ] 1x Instant-Read Thermometer

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 4–6 Personnel:*
- **The Target:** 2 Racks Baby Back Ribs
- **The Rub:** 1/4 cup Brown Sugar, 1 tbsp Smoked Paprika, 1 tbsp Black Pepper, 1 tbsp Salt, 1 tsp Garlic Powder, 1 tsp Onion Powder
- **The Wrap Payload:** 4 tbsp Butter (cubed), 1/4 cup Honey or Brown Sugar, 1/4 cup Apple Juice
- **The Finish:** 1 cup BBQ Sauce

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Membrane Maneuver)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Stabilize smoker at **225°F (107°C)**.
2.  **Membrane Extraction:** Flip the rack bone-side up. Use a paper towel to grip the thin white membrane and rip it off in one motion. Failure to remove this membrane results in "Tough Rations."
3.  **Rub Deployment:** Coat ribs aggressively on all surfaces and both sides.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The 3-2-1 Method)
1.  **Stage 1 — The Smoke (3 Hours):** Place ribs bone-side down on the smoker. Cook for 3 hours to set the bark and smoke profile.
2.  **Stage 2 — The Wrap (2 Hours):** Remove ribs. Place on heavy foil. Add Wrap Payload (butter, honey, apple juice) and seal tightly. Return to smoker for 2 hours. This tenderizes the collagen.
3.  **Stage 3 — The Glaze (1 Hour):** Remove from foil. Place back on grates. Brush with BBQ sauce. Cook for 1 final hour to set the glaze.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **The Bend Test:** Pick up the rack with tongs at one end. If it bends 90 degrees and the meat starts to crack, the mission is successful.
2.  **Rest:** Let sit for 10 minutes before slicing between the bones.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   "Fall-off-the-bone" is over-cooked. A properly executed rib has a clean "bite-through" — the meat detaches from the bone with one pull but retains its structure.
*   Removing the membrane is not optional. It creates a moisture barrier that prevents smoke penetration and toughens the back of the rack.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Spray the ribs with 50/50 apple cider vinegar and apple juice every 45 minutes during Stage 1 to develop the bark and maintain surface moisture.
