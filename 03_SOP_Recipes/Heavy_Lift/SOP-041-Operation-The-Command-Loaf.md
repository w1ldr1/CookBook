# SOP-041: OPERATION THE COMMAND LOAF
**Objective:** Deployment of a high-density, multi-ration Meatloaf for family sustainment.
**Rank:** Sergeant
**Difficulty:** 2/5
**Mission Type:** Heavy Lift

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 1 Hour 15 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 15 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 55–60 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per serving):** P: 38g | C: 18g | F: 22g | ~420 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 2/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-032 (Starch Support), SOP-006 (Green Cover).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 9x5" Loaf Pan or Rimmed Baking Sheet
- [ ] 1x Large Mixing Bowl
- [ ] 1x Small Bowl (for the glaze)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 6 Personnel:*
- **The Chassis:** 2 lbs Ground Beef (80/20) or "Meatloaf Mix" (Beef/Pork/Veal)
- **Binding Agents:** 2 Eggs, 3/4 cup Breadcrumbs, 1/2 cup Whole Milk
- **Aromatics:** 1 small Onion (grated), 2 cloves Garlic (minced)
- **Ordnance:** 1 tbsp Worcestershire Sauce, 1 tsp Salt, 1 tsp Black Pepper, 1 tsp Dried Thyme
- **The Glaze:** 1/2 cup Ketchup, 2 tbsp Brown Sugar, 1 tbsp Dijon Mustard

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Mix)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Preheat oven to **350°F (177°C)**.
2.  **Glaze Prep:** Whisk ketchup, brown sugar, and Dijon mustard together in the small bowl. Set aside — it deploys in Phase 3.
3.  **The Panade:** Soak breadcrumbs in milk for 5 minutes. This hydrated bread-paste keeps the loaf moist throughout the long dwell.
4.  **Integration:** Combine meat, eggs, aromatics, panade, and all seasonings in the large bowl. Mix with your hands until *just* combined. Over-working produces a dense, rubbery loaf.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Bake)
1.  **Loading:** Press mixture into the greased loaf pan or form a free-standing loaf on a baking sheet.
2.  **Initial Dwell:** Bake for 40 minutes.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Glaze Deployment:** Remove loaf from oven. Spread the prepared glaze liberally across the entire top surface.
2.  **Final Dwell:** Return to oven for 15–20 minutes until the glaze is set and internal temp reads **160°F (71°C)**.
3.  **Rest:** Let rest 10 minutes before slicing. This allows the internal structure to set and juices to redistribute.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   Grating the onion (not dicing) ensures it melts completely into the loaf, providing moisture and flavor without any large chunks that affect the texture.
*   Free-form loaf on a baking sheet allows all four sides to develop a crust. Loaf pan is faster but produces softer sides.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Slice cold leftover meatloaf and serve between two slices of toasted SOP-019 bread with mayonnaise for "Command Sandwiches" the next day.
