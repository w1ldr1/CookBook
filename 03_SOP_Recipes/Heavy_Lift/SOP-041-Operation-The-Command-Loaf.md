# SOP-041: OPERATION THE COMMAND LOAF
**Objective:** Deployment of a high-density, multi-ration Meatloaf for family sustainment.
**Rank:** Sergeant (Requires textural management)
**Mission Type:** Heavy Lift (High-Yield Rations)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 1 Hour 15 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 15 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 55–60 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per serving):** P: 38g | C: 18g | F: 22g | ~420 Cal
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-032 (Mashed Potatoes) and SOP-006 (Green Cover).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x 9x5" Loaf Pan or Rimmed Baking Sheet
- [ ] 1x Large Mixing Bowl
- [ ] 1x Small Bowl (for the glaze)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*   **The Chassis:** 2 lbs Ground Beef (80/20) or a "Meatloaf Mix" (Beef/Pork/Veal).
*   **Binding Agents:** 2 Eggs, 3/4 cup Breadcrumbs (SOP-019 crumbs preferred), 1/2 cup Milk.
*   **Aromatics:** 1 small Onion (grated), 2 cloves Garlic (minced).
*   **The Glaze (Top Cover):** 1/2 cup Ketchup, 2 tbsp Brown Sugar, 1 tbsp Dijon Mustard.
*   **Ordnance:** 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce, 1 tsp Salt, 1 tsp Black Pepper, 1 tsp Dried Thyme.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Mix)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Preheat oven to **350°F (177°C)**.
2.  **Hydration:** Soak breadcrumbs in milk for 5 minutes (The Panade maneuver—ensures the loaf stays moist).
3.  **Integration:** Combine meat, eggs, aromatics, panade, and seasonings in the large bowl. 
4.  **Field Warning:** Mix with your hands until *just* combined. Over-working the meat results in a "Dense/Rubbery" unit.

### Phase 2: Deployment (The Forming)
1.  **Loading:** Press mixture into a greased loaf pan OR form into a free-standing loaf on a baking sheet. 
2.  **Initial Dwell:** Bake for 40 minutes.

### Phase 3: Extraction (The Glaze)
1.  **Top Cover:** Whisk glaze supplies. Remove loaf from oven and spread glaze liberally over the top.
2.  **Final Dwell:** Return to oven for 15–20 minutes. Target internal temp is **160°F (71°C)**.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** Grating the onion instead of dicing ensures it "disappears" into the loaf, providing moisture without large chunks.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Slice cold leftovers for "Command Sandwiches" on toasted SOP-019 bread.
