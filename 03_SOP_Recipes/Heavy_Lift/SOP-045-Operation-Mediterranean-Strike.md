# SOP-045: OPERATION MEDITERRANEAN STRIKE
**Objective:** Slow-roasting a Bone-In Leg of Lamb for high-yield Hostage Rescue missions.
**Rank:** Commander (High-value target)
**Mission Type:** Heavy Lift (Entertaining)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 2.5 Hours
*   **Infil (Prep):** 20 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 1.5–2 Hours
*   **Target Macros (per 6oz):** P: 42g | C: 0g | F: 28g | ~430 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 3/5

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x Large Roasting Pan with Rack
- [ ] 1x Small Bowl (for the paste)
- [ ] 1x Digital Meat Thermometer (The Rangefinder)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*   **The Target:** 1 Bone-In Leg of Lamb (5–7 lbs).
*   **The Paste:** 1/4 cup Olive Oil, 6 cloves Garlic (minced), 2 tbsp Fresh Rosemary (chopped), 1 tbsp Fresh Thyme, 2 tsp Salt, 1 tsp Black Pepper.
*   **Foundation:** 2 Onions (thickly sliced), 1 head of Garlic (halved).

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Infusion)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Preheat oven to **325°F (163°C)**.
2.  **Surgical Prep:** Use a small knife to make 10–12 small incisions (1-inch deep) across the surface of the lamb. 
3.  **The Paste:** Mix oil, garlic, and herbs. Rub the paste over the entire lamb, pushing it into the incisions.

### Phase 2: Deployment
1.  **Staging:** Place sliced onions and the halved garlic head at the bottom of the roasting pan. Place the lamb on the rack above them.
2.  **Dwell:** Roast until the internal temp reaches your desired profile.
    *   *Medium-Rare Pull Point:* 130°F (Final: 135°F).
    *   *Medium Pull Point:* 140°F (Final: 145°F).

### Phase 3: Extraction & Resting
1.  **Extraction:** Remove from oven. Tent with foil.
2.  **Critical Rest:** Let sit for 20 minutes. This is a "High-Mass" protein; slicing too early will result in "Juice Loss Failure."

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** Use the drippings at the bottom of the pan to create a "Secondary Objective" Pan Sauce (SOP-033).
*   **Secondary Objective:** Serve with "SOP-032 Mashed Potatoes" and "SOP-043 Roasted Roots."
