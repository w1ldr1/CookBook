# SOP-045: OPERATION MEDITERRANEAN STRIKE
**Objective:** Slow-roasting a Bone-In Leg of Lamb for high-yield Hostage Rescue missions.
**Rank:** Commander
**Difficulty:** 3/5
**Mission Type:** Heavy Lift

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 2.5 Hours
*   **Infil (Prep):** 20 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 1.5–2 Hours
*   **Target Macros (per 6oz):** P: 42g | C: 0g | F: 28g | ~430 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 3/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-043 (Root Fortification), SOP-032 (Starch Support), SOP-033 (Force Multiplier).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x Large Roasting Pan with Rack
- [ ] 1x Small Bowl (for the paste)
- [ ] 1x Digital Meat Thermometer (The Rangefinder)
- [ ] 1x Paring Knife (for infusion incisions)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 6–8 Personnel:*
- **The Target:** 1 Bone-In Leg of Lamb (5–7 lbs)
- **The Paste:** 1/4 cup Olive Oil, 6 cloves Garlic (minced), 2 tbsp Fresh Rosemary (chopped), 1 tbsp Fresh Thyme, 2 tsp Salt, 1 tsp Black Pepper
- **Foundation:** 2 Onions (thickly sliced), 1 head Garlic (halved crosswise)

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Infusion)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Preheat oven to **325°F (163°C)**.
2.  **Surgical Prep:** Use the paring knife to make 10–12 small incisions (1-inch deep) across the entire surface of the lamb.
3.  **The Paste:** Combine oil, garlic, rosemary, and thyme. Rub the paste over the entire surface, pushing it into every incision with your finger.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Roast)
1.  **Foundation:** Place thickly sliced onions and the halved garlic head at the bottom of the roasting pan. Set the rack over them.
2.  **Deployment:** Place the lamb on the rack.
3.  **Dwell:** Roast until internal temp reaches target:
    *   Medium-Rare Pull: **130°F (54°C)** — Final temp after rest: 135°F
    *   Medium Pull: **140°F (60°C)** — Final temp after rest: 145°F

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Extraction:** Remove from oven. Tent loosely with foil.
2.  **Critical Rest:** Let the lamb rest for 20 minutes minimum. This is a high-mass protein — cutting too early produces catastrophic juice loss.
3.  **Pan Sauce:** Deglaze the drippings in the roasting pan with 1/2 cup red wine or stock for a SOP-033 Pan Sauce variant.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   The incisions are the key technique — they carry the herb paste deep into the muscle, flavoring every slice, not just the exterior surface.
*   Lamb fat has a lower melting point than beef. The 325°F oven renders the fat slowly and keeps the meat juicy without seizing the muscle fibers.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Deploy with SOP-032 Mashed Potatoes and SOP-043 Roasted Roots for a complete "Hostage Rescue" spread — a meal that converts non-believers.
