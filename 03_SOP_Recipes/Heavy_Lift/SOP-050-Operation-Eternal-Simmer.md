# SOP-050: OPERATION ETERNAL SIMMER
**Objective:** Long-duration extraction of flavor into a "Sunday Sauce" (Meat Ragù).
**Rank:** Commander
**Difficulty:** 4/5
**Mission Type:** Heavy Lift

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 6–8 Hours
*   **Infil (Prep):** 30 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 6 Hours
*   **Target Macros (per 1.5 cups):** P: 45g | C: 12g | F: 32g | ~520 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 4/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-035 (Red Sauce), SOP-019 (Daily Bread), SOP-048 (Hostage Rescue Beverages).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 7qt+ Dutch Oven (The Force Multiplier)
- [ ] 1x Wooden Spatula (The Scraper)
- [ ] 1x Tongs

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 8–10 Personnel:*
- **The Multi-Meat Payload:** 1 lb Italian Sausage (links), 1 lb Pork Neck Bones or Short Ribs, 1 lb Meatballs (optional)
- **The Base:** 2 cans (28oz) San Marzano Whole Peeled Tomatoes (crushed by hand — do not use a blender)
- **The Trinity:** 1 Large Onion (minced), 4 cloves Garlic (smashed), 1/4 cup Tomato Paste
- **The Catalyst:** 1/2 cup Dry Red Wine, 1 Parmesan Rind

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Browning)
1.  **Protein Engagement:** Brown all meats (sausage, ribs, bones) in the Dutch oven over Medium-High heat in batches. Do not crowd — sear, do not steam.
2.  **Tactical Residue:** Remove meat and set aside. Leave the Fond (brown bits) and rendered fat in the pot.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Foundation)
1.  **Aromatic Deployment:** Add onion and garlic to the pot fat. Sauté until translucent, about 5 minutes.
2.  **Paste Integration:** Add tomato paste. Stir constantly for 3 minutes until it transitions from red to a deep "Brick Red" — this removes bitterness and concentrates the flavor.
3.  **Deglaze:** Add red wine. Scrape the bottom of the pot aggressively to release every bit of fond into the liquid.
4.  **Full Deployment:** Add crushed tomatoes, all browned meats, and the Parmesan rind.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **The Long Dwell:** Reduce to the lowest possible heat — the sauce should produce a single "blip" every 30–45 seconds, not a simmer. Cook for 6 hours, stirring every 45 minutes to prevent bottom-burn.
2.  **Meat Recovery:** Remove ribs and neck bones. Shred the meat and return it to the sauce. Discard bones and the spent Parmesan rind.
3.  **Deployment:** Serve over fresh pasta or rigatoni. This mission produces a surplus — freeze half in airtight containers for "Emergency Sustainment."

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   The Parmesan rind acts as a natural umami amplifier — it leaches glutamates and salt into the sauce over the 6-hour dwell without any chemical additives. Never skip it.
*   The "blip" frequency is your quality control. Too frequent = too hot = the sauce will scorch and taste bitter. One blip per 30–45 seconds is the approved rate of fire.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Freeze surplus in 1.5-cup portions for single-serving emergency meals. Reheats in 8 minutes and is indistinguishable from fresh. This is how Italian grandmothers operate.
