# SOP-050: OPERATION ETERNAL SIMMER
**Objective:** Long-duration extraction of flavor into a "Sunday Sauce" (Meat Ragù).
**Rank:** Commander (Logistics and Endurance)
**Mission Type:** Heavy Lift

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 6–8 Hours
*   **Infil (Prep):** 30 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 6 Hours
*   **Target Macros (per 1.5 cups):** P: 45g | C: 12g | F: 32g
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-035 (Pasta), SOP-019 (Daily Bread), SOP-048 (Beverages).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x 7qt+ Dutch Oven (The Force Multiplier)
- [ ] 1x Wooden Spatula (The Scraper)
- [ ] 1x Tongs

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*   **The Multi-Meat Payload:** 1 lb Italian Sausage, 1 lb Pork Neck Bones or Short Ribs, 1 lb Meatballs (Optional).
*   **The Base:** 2 cans (28oz) San Marzano Whole Peeled Tomatoes (Crushed by hand).
*   **The Trinity:** 1 Large Onion (minced), 4 cloves Garlic (smashed), 1/4 cup Tomato Paste.
*   **The Catalyst:** 1/2 cup Dry Red Wine, 1 Parmesan Rind (Critical for depth).

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Browning Maneuver)
1.  **Engagement:** Brown all meats (sausage, ribs, bones) in the Dutch oven over medium-high heat. 
2.  **Extraction:** Remove meat and set aside. Leave the "Tactical Residue" (Fond) in the pot.

### Phase 2: Building the Foundation
1.  **Sauté:** Add onion and garlic to the pot. Sauté until translucent.
2.  **Paste Integration:** Add tomato paste. Stir for 3 minutes until it turns a deep "Brick Red."
3.  **Deglaze:** Add wine. Scrape the bottom of the pot to release the fond.

### Phase 3: The Long Dwell
1.  **Full Deployment:** Add crushed tomatoes, the browned meats, and the Parmesan rind.
2.  **Simmer Phase:** Reduce heat to the lowest possible setting. The sauce should "blip" occasionally, not boil.
3.  **Endurance:** Cook for 6 hours, stirring every 45 minutes to prevent "Bottom Burn."

### Phase 4: Exfil
1.  **Shredding:** Remove the ribs/neck bones. Shred the meat and return it to the sauce. Discard the bones and the Parmesan rind.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** The Parmesan rind acts as a "Natural MSG," providing intense umami and salt without chemical additives.
*   **Secondary Objective:** This mission produces a surplus. Freeze half for "Emergency Sustainment" missions.
