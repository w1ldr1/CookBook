# SOP-011: OPERATION OVERWATCH BROTH
**Objective:** Total value extraction from leftover skeletal remains and vegetable scraps.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Difficulty:** 1/5
**Mission Type:** Heavy Lift

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 6–12 Hours (Passive)
*   **Infil (Prep):** 5 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 6–12 Hours
*   **Target Macros (per 1 cup):** P: 6g | C: 0g | F: 1g | ~35 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 1/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-009 (Grain Silo), SOP-016 (Iron-Clad Chili), SOP-023 (Delta Slow).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 6qt+ Stock Pot or Slow Cooker
- [ ] 1x Fine Mesh Strainer
- [ ] 1x Large Bowl (for ice bath)

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 1 Gallon of Broth:*
- **The Chassis:** 1–2 Carcasses (Chicken from SOP-007 or Beef bones)
- **The Scraps:** Onion skins, carrot ends, celery butts (Retrieved from "Scrap Storage")
- **Liquid Ammunition:** 4–5 Quarts cold water
- **Acid Catalyst:** 2 tbsp Apple Cider Vinegar (helps extract minerals from bone)
- **Aromatics:** 2 Bay leaves, 1 tsp Peppercorns

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (Loading)
1.  **Deployment:** Place bones and frozen vegetable scraps into the hardware.
2.  **Hydration:** Cover with cold water. Add vinegar and aromatics.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Extraction)
1.  **Boil to Simmer:** Bring to a boil, then immediately drop to the lowest possible simmer.
2.  **Surveillance:** Look for "lazy bubbles." If it's boiling hard, the broth will be cloudy and bitter.
3.  **Endurance:** Cook for a minimum of 6 hours (Chicken) or up to 12 hours (Beef).

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Filtration:** Strain the liquid through the mesh strainer into a large bowl. Discard the solids; their mission is over.
2.  **Rapid Cooling:** Place the bowl in an ice bath to bring the temp down rapidly (Food Safety protocol).
3.  **Defatting:** Once refrigerated, a layer of white fat will solidify on top. Scrape it off to reveal the "Liquid Gold" (Gelatinous broth).
4.  **Storage:** Transfer to airtight containers. Refrigerate up to 5 days or freeze up to 3 months.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   A "gentle blip" is the only approved simmer rate. If the liquid is boiling, the heat is too high — cloudy, bitter broth is the result.
*   Apple Cider Vinegar is critical for pulling minerals and collagen from the bone matrix. Do not skip it.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Roast the bones at 400°F for 20 minutes before adding to the water to achieve a "Dark Amber" high-intensity flavor profile.
