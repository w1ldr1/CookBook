# SOP-016: OPERATION IRON-CLAD CHILI
**Objective:** Production of high-calorie, long-stability "Texas Red" beef chili.
**Rank:** Sergeant
**Mission Type:** Heavy Lift (Dutch Oven Mission)


---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 3 Hours
*   **Infil (Prep):** 20 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 2.5 Hours
*   **Target Macros (per 1.5 cups):** P: 44g | C: 12g | F: 28g | ~480 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 2/5 (Endurance)
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-020 (Cornbread), SOP-040 (Baked Potato).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 6qt Dutch Oven (The Force Multiplier)
- [ ] 1x Wooden Spatula (The Scraper)
- [ ] 1x Chef's Knife

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 6-8 Rations:*
- **The Protein:** 3 lbs Ground Beef (80/20) or cubed Chuck Roast.
- **The Base:** 1 Large Onion (diced), 3 cloves Garlic (minced).
- **The Liquid:** 1 can (28oz) Crushed Tomatoes, 1 cup Beef Stock, 1 small can Tomato Paste.
- **The Rub (Chili Base):** 3 tbsp Chili Powder, 1 tbsp Cumin, 1 tsp Smoked Paprika, 1 tsp Oregano, 1 tsp Salt.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Sear)
1.  **Protein Engagement:** Heat Dutch oven over medium-high. Brown the meat in batches. Do not crowd the pan. 
2.  **The Extraction:** Remove meat once browned, leaving the rendered fat in the pot.

### Phase 2: Building the Base
1.  **Aromatic Deployment:** Add onion to the fat. Sauté for 5 minutes until translucent. Add garlic for the final 60 seconds.
2.  **The Paste:** Add tomato paste and all spices. Stir for 2 minutes to "bloom" the spices (releasing the oils).

### Phase 3: Integration & Dwell
1.  **Full Deployment:** Add the browned meat back in. Pour in crushed tomatoes and beef stock.
2.  **Simmer Phase:** Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to LOW. Cover and simmer for 2 to 2.5 hours. Stir every 30 minutes to ensure no "bottom-burn" occurs.

### Phase 4: Exfil
1.  **The Polish:** Taste for salt. Add a splash of Apple Cider Vinegar to brighten the flavor before serving.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** Chili is a "Day 2" asset; flavors deepen after a 24-hour cold soak.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Serve over a "Tuber Load" (SOP-040) for a high-calorie winter refuel.
