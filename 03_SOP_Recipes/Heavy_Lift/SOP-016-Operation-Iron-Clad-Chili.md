# SOP-016: OPERATION IRON-CLAD CHILI
**Objective:** Production of high-calorie, long-stability "Texas Red" beef chili.
**Rank:** Sergeant
**Difficulty:** 2/5
**Mission Type:** Heavy Lift

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 3 Hours
*   **Infil (Prep):** 20 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 2.5 Hours
*   **Target Macros (per 1.5 cups):** P: 44g | C: 12g | F: 28g | ~480 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 2/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-020 (Cornbread), SOP-040 (Baked Potato).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 6qt Dutch Oven (The Force Multiplier)
- [ ] 1x Wooden Spatula (The Scraper)
- [ ] 1x Chef's Knife

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 6–8 Rations:*
- **The Protein:** 3 lbs Ground Beef (80/20) or cubed Chuck Roast
- **The Base:** 1 Large Onion (diced), 3 cloves Garlic (minced)
- **The Liquid:** 1 can (28oz) Crushed Tomatoes, 1 cup Beef Stock, 1 small can Tomato Paste
- **The Rub (Chili Base):** 3 tbsp Chili Powder, 1 tbsp Cumin, 1 tsp Smoked Paprika, 1 tsp Oregano, 1 tsp Salt

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Sear)
1.  **Protein Engagement:** Heat Dutch oven over Medium-High. Brown the meat in batches — do not crowd the pan or it will steam instead of sear.
2.  **Aromatic Deployment:** Remove browned meat, leaving rendered fat in the pot. Add onion and sauté for 5 minutes until translucent. Add garlic for the final 60 seconds.
3.  **The Bloom:** Add tomato paste and all spices. Stir for 2 minutes to bloom the spices and release their oils.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Simmer)
1.  **Full Deployment:** Add the browned meat back in. Pour in crushed tomatoes and beef stock.
2.  **Dwell:** Bring to a boil, then reduce to LOW. Cover and simmer for 2 to 2.5 hours. Stir every 30 minutes to prevent bottom-burn.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **The Polish:** Taste for salt. Add a splash of Apple Cider Vinegar to brighten the final flavor.
2.  **Storage:** Chili is a "Day 2" asset. Refrigerate overnight — flavors deepen significantly after a 24-hour cold soak.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   Do not add beans — this is Texas Red. Beans are a civilian compromise.
*   Blooming the spices in the tomato paste is non-negotiable. Raw spice powder tastes flat; bloomed spice tastes layered.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Serve over a Baked Potato (SOP-040) for a high-calorie winter refuel. Top with shredded cheddar and sour cream.
