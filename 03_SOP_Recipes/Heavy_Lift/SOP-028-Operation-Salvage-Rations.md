# SOP-028: OPERATION SALVAGE RATIONS
**Objective:** Conversion of stale bread assets into a high-density Bread Pudding.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Difficulty:** 2/5
**Mission Type:** Heavy Lift

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 1.5 Hours
*   **Infil (Prep):** 15 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 45 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per serving):** P: 9g | C: 45g | F: 12g | ~320 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 2/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-019 (Daily Bread), SOP-036 (Liquid Fuel).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 9x13" Baking Dish
- [ ] 1x Large Mixing Bowl
- [ ] 1x Whisk

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 8 Personnel:*
- **The Bulk:** 6–8 cups Stale Bread (SOP-019 preferred), cubed into 1-inch pieces
- **The Custard:** 4 Eggs, 2 cups Whole Milk, 1 cup Heavy Cream, 3/4 cup Sugar
- **Aromatics:** 1 tsp Cinnamon, 1 tsp Vanilla Extract, 1/4 tsp Nutmeg

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Soak)
1.  **Deployment:** Place bread cubes in the baking dish in an even layer.
2.  **Custard Build:** Whisk eggs, milk, cream, sugar, and all aromatics together in the mixing bowl until fully combined.
3.  **Saturation:** Pour the custard mixture over the bread. Press down firmly with your hands to ensure 100% absorption. Let sit for 20 minutes — the bread must be fully saturated before baking.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Bake)
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Preheat oven to **350°F (177°C)**.
2.  **Dwell:** Bake for 40–45 minutes until the custard is set at the center and the top is deep golden-brown. Test by pressing the center — it should feel firm, not liquid.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Extraction:** Remove from oven and let rest 10 minutes before portioning.
2.  **Deployment:** Serve warm. Apply bourbon-butter glaze (Secondary Objective) immediately before serving.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   Stale bread is a hard requirement. Fresh bread has too much internal moisture — it will not absorb the custard and will turn to mush.
*   The 20-minute soak before baking is mandatory. Rushing into the oven with dry patches of bread produces uneven texture.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   Glaze: melt 2 tbsp butter, 1/4 cup powdered sugar, and 1 oz bourbon in a small saucepan. Pour over individual servings immediately before deployment for "Hostage Rescue" level results.
