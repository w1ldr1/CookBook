# SOP-028: OPERATION SALVAGE RATIONS
**Objective:** Conversion of stale bread assets into a high-density Bread Pudding.
**Rank:** Recruit
**Mission Type:** Heavy Lift (Custard Mission)


---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 1.5 Hours
*   **Infil (Prep):** 15 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 45 Minutes
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 2/5 (Requires soak time)
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-019 (Daily Bread), SOP-036 (Coffee).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x 9x13" Baking Dish
- [ ] 1x Large Mixing Bowl

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
- **The Bulk:** 6–8 cups Stale Bread (SOP-019), cubed.
- **The Custard:** 4 Eggs, 2 cups Whole Milk, 1 cup Heavy Cream, 3/4 cup Sugar.
- **Aromatics:** 1 tsp Cinnamon, 1 tsp Vanilla, 1/4 tsp Nutmeg.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Soak)
1.  **Deployment:** Place bread cubes in the baking dish.
2.  **The Mix:** Whisk eggs, milk, cream, sugar, and aromatics in the bowl.
3.  **Saturation:** Pour mixture over the bread. Press down with your hands to ensure 100% hydration. Let sit for 20 minutes before baking.

### Phase 2: Engagement
1.  **Thermal Calibration:** Preheat oven to **350°F (177°C)**.
2.  **Dwell:** Bake for 40–45 minutes until the custard is set and the top is golden-brown.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** Using "SOP-019 No-Knead Bread" creates a superior texture compared to store-bought white bread.
*   **Serving:** Top with a drizzle of maple syrup or melted butter for a "Finish Maneuver."
