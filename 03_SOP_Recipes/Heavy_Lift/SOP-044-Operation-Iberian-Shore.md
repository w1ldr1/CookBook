# SOP-044: OPERATION IBERIAN SHORE
**Objective:** Execution of a traditional Spanish Paella with focus on "Socarrat" crust formation.
**Rank:** Commander
**Difficulty:** 4/5
**Mission Type:** Heavy Lift

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 60 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 20 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 30–40 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per serving):** P: 32g | C: 58g | F: 14g | ~510 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 4/5
*   **Compatible Systems:** SOP-019 (Daily Bread), SOP-048 (Hostage Rescue Beverages).

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
*Required tools for mission success:*
- [ ] 1x 15" Paella Pan or the widest stainless steel skillet available (surface area is critical)
- [ ] 1x Wooden Spoon (The "Distributor")
- [ ] 1x Aluminum Foil (The "Shield")

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*Quantities optimized for 4–6 Personnel:*
- **The Grain:** 2 cups Bomba or Calasparra Rice (short-grain is mandatory — no long-grain substitutes)
- **The Protein:** 1 lb Shrimp (peeled), 1/2 lb Spanish Chorizo (sliced), 1/2 lb Chicken Thighs (cubed)
- **The Liquid Payload:** 4 cups Seafood or Chicken Stock, 1 pinch Saffron threads
- **The Sofrito:** 1 Onion (diced), 1 Red Bell Pepper (sliced), 3 cloves Garlic (minced)
- **The Finish:** 1/2 cup Frozen Peas, 1 Lemon (quartered), Fresh Parsley

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Saffron Steep)
1.  **Stock Prep:** Heat stock in a separate pot. Add saffron threads. Let steep until the liquid turns deep gold — this is the primary flavor and color compound for the dish.
2.  **Protein Engagement:** Heat the paella pan over Medium-High. Brown the chorizo and chicken. Remove and set aside — leave the rendered fat in the pan.

### Phase 2: Engagement (The Build)
1.  **Sofrito:** Add onion, bell pepper, and garlic to the pan fat. Cook for 5 minutes until softened.
2.  **The Toast:** Add rice. Stir for 2 minutes until the edges of each grain are translucent.
3.  **The Pour:** Pour in the saffron-infused stock. Stir **ONCE** to distribute the rice evenly. **DO NOT STIR AGAIN.** Stirring releases starch and destroys the texture.
4.  **Simmer:** Cook on Medium-Low for 15 minutes. Press the chicken, chorizo, shrimp, and peas into the surface of the rice.

### Phase 3: Extraction & Exfil
1.  **Socarrat Formation:** Increase heat to High for the final 3 minutes. Listen for a sizzling-popping sound — this is the "Socarrat" (crispy rice crust) forming on the bottom. Smell for toasted rice, not burnt rice.
2.  **Rest:** Kill heat. Cover with foil and let rest for 5 minutes.
3.  **Deployment:** Serve directly from the pan. Squeeze lemon over the top and scatter fresh parsley. The Socarrat is the highest-value asset — scrape it up and distribute with each serving.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
**Field Intel:**
*   The "DO NOT STIR" rule after adding stock is the single most critical discipline in this mission. Paella is not risotto. Stirring releases starch, creates a gluey texture, and prevents Socarrat formation.
*   Bomba rice absorbs 3x its volume in liquid without breaking down. Long-grain rice does not behave the same way and will produce mushy results.

**Secondary Objective:**
*   For a pure seafood version, replace chicken and chorizo with mussels and squid rings. Add mussels in Phase 3 with the shrimp.
