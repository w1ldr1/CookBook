# SOP-044: OPERATION IBERIAN SHORE
**Objective:** Execution of a traditional Spanish Paella with a focus on "Socarrat" (crust) formation.
**Rank:** Commander (Requires advanced thermal management)
**Mission Type:** Heavy Lift (Hostage Rescue / Social Ops)

---

## 1. INTELLIGENCE (The Stats)
*   **Total Mission Time:** 60 Minutes
*   **Infil (Prep):** 20 Minutes
*   **Thermal Dwell (Cook):** 30–40 Minutes
*   **Target Macros (per serving):** P: 32g | C: 58g | F: 14g | ~510 Cal
*   **Difficulty Rating:** 4/5

## 2. HARDWARE (Standard Issue)
- [ ] 1x 15" Paella Pan or the widest Stainless Steel skillet available (Surface area is critical).
- [ ] 1x Wooden Spoon (The "Distributor").
- [ ] 1x Foil (The "Shield").

## 3. SUPPLIES (Consumables)
*   **The Grain:** 2 cups Bomba or Calasparra Rice (Short-grain is mandatory).
*   **The Protein:** 1 lb Shrimp (peeled), 1/2 lb Spanish Chorizo (sliced), 1/2 lb Chicken Thighs (cubed).
*   **The Liquid Payload:** 4 cups Seafood or Chicken Stock, 1 pinch Saffron threads (The Catalyst).
*   **The Trinity:** 1 Onion (diced), 1 Red Bell Pepper (sliced), 3 cloves Garlic (minced).
*   **The Finish:** 1/2 cup Frozen Peas, 1 Lemon (quartered), Fresh Parsley.

## 4. EXECUTION (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Infil (The Saffron Steep)
1.  **Calibration:** Heat the stock in a separate pot. Add saffron. Let it steep until the liquid is deep gold/orange.
2.  **Protein Engagement:** Heat the paella pan over medium-high. Brown the chorizo and chicken. Remove and set aside.

### Phase 2: Building the Base (Sofrito)
1.  **Sauté:** Add onion, pepper, and garlic to the pan fat. Cook until softened.
2.  **The Grain:** Add rice. Stir for 2 minutes to "Toast the Ordnance." The rice should look translucent at the edges.

### Phase 3: Deployment (The Simmer)
1.  **The Pour:** Pour in the saffron-infused stock. Stir **ONCE** to distribute the rice evenly.
2.  **Field Warning:** **DO NOT STIR AGAIN.** Stirring releases starch and ruins the texture. 
3.  **Dwell:** Simmer on medium-low for 15 minutes.
4.  **Loading:** Press the chicken, chorizo, shrimp, and peas into the surface of the rice.

### Phase 4: The Final Assault (Socarrat Formation)
1.  **Maximum Heat:** For the final 3 minutes, increase heat to high. Listen for a "Sizzling/Popping" sound. This is the rice crust (Socarrat) forming on the bottom. 
2.  **The Smell Test:** If it smells slightly toasted (not burnt), the mission is successful.
3.  **Extraction:** Kill the heat. Cover with foil and let it rest for 5 minutes.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Field Intel:** The "Socarrat" is the highest-value asset in this mission. It should be a thin, crispy, dark brown layer at the base of the pan.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Garnish with lemon quarters and parsley before serving. Serve directly from the pan for "Command Level" presentation.
