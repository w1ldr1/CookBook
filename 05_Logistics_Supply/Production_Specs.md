# CHAPTER 05: LOGISTICS & SUPPLY CHAIN
**Subject:** Technical Specifications for Physical Production
**Mission Objective:** To engineer a kitchen-proof, modular field manual.

---

## 1. THE CHASSIS (The Binder)
The book must be self-standing for hands-free operation and allow for modular page updates.

### [ ] Tactical Easel Binder
*   **Format:** 3-Ring "Break-Back" Easel Binder.
*   **Dimensions:** 5.5" x 8.5" (Standard Field Manual Size) or 8.5" x 11" (High-Visibility).
*   **Material:** Heavy-duty 110pt chipboard wrapped in matte-finish Polypropylene or Canvas (OD Green/Coyote Tan).
*   **Mechanism:** 1" or 1.5" D-Ring for maximum page capacity and flat-laying utility.
*   **Manufacturer Intel:** 
    *   *Unified Packaging* (Specializes in custom easel binders).
    *   *Case Enclosures* (Ruggedized industrial options).

---

## 2. DEPLOYMENT MEDIA (The Pages)
Standard paper fails under kitchen conditions (grease, water, heat). We require synthetic media.

### [ ] Waterproof / Greaseproof Synthetic Paper
*   **Spec:** 100lb or 120lb Cover Weight (for durability).
*   **Technology:** "Rite in the Rain" (All-weather) or Polypropylene-based paper (e.g., YUPO or REVLAR).
*   **Tactical Advantage:** 100% waterproof, tear-resistant, and can be wiped clean of oil and sauce.
*   **Manufacturer Intel:** 
    *   *RELYCO* (REVLAR waterproof paper).
    *   *PrintNinja* (Offers high-durability synthetic options for cookbooks).
