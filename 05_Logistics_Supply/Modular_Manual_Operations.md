# USER GUIDE: MODULAR MANUAL OPERATIONS
**Subject:** Proper Use and Maintenance of the Physical Field Manual
**Mission Objective:** To maximize the utility of the "Easel Binder" and "Synthetic Paper" hardware.

---

## 1. THE STAND-UP MANEUVER (Easel Mode)
The binder is designed for hands-free data viewing.
1.  Open the binder 360 degrees.
2.  Fold the covers back until they meet.
3.  Secure the integrated "Locking Strap" or magnet.
4.  Place on a flat counter surface. The SOP is now at eye-level and away from the "Splash Zone."

---

## 2. MODULAR DEPLOYMENT (Removable Pages)
*   **The Scenario:** You are heading to the grocery store or a remote grill location.
*   **The Maneuver:** Open the 3-ring mechanism. Remove only the required SOP (e.g., SOP-001) and the Pantry Inventory. 
*   **Advantage:** Reduces bulk and protects the rest of the manual from the elements.

---

## 3. CLEANING THE MEDIA (Synthetic Paper)
The pages are printed on waterproof, greaseproof poly-media. 
*   **Spills:** If sauce, oil, or water hits the page, simply wipe with a damp cloth or paper towel.
*   **Sanitization:** For raw meat contact, use a mild dish soap. **DO NOT** use abrasive scrubbers or bleach, which may degrade the ink over time.

---

## 4. UPDATING THE SYSTEM
This manual is a living document.
*   **Expansion Packs:** New SOPs will be issued digitally or via "Expansion Refills."
*   **Personal Intel:** Use a "Permanent Marker" (Sharpie) to write your own AAR notes directly on the pages. It will not smudge once dry.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Storage:** When not in use, store the manual vertically on a shelf to prevent ring-mechanism warping.
*   **Durability Check:** Periodically check the rings to ensure they are aligned for smooth page transitions.
