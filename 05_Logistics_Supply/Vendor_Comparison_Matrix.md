## TORTURE TEST CRITERIA (The Stress Test)
Every sample must be subjected to the following engagement before contract approval:
1.  **Chemical Exposure:** Smear with 1 tsp hot sauce/mustard for 1 hour. Result must be "Wipe-Clean."
2.  **Thermal Exposure:** Place near a boiling pot (steam) for 10 minutes. No warping permitted.
3.  **Tear Resistance:** Attempt manual tear of page edges. Must remain structurally sound.
4.  **Hardware Cycle:** Flip page 100 times in a 3-ring binder. No hole-tear allowed.
