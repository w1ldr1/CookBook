# MARKETING FUNNEL ARCHITECTURE
**Subject:** Traffic Flow and Lead Capture
**Objective:** Convert "Mission Leak" readers into Day 1 Buyers.

---

## 1. THE FLOW (The "Infil" Path)
1.  **Traffic Source:** Social Media (IG/TikTok/X) shows a teaser video.
2.  **The Hook:** "Download SOP-001 (The Steak Mission) for free."
3.  **The Asset:** User downloads the "Mission Leak" PDF.
4.  **The Conversion:** Inside the PDF, the user clicks the link to the **Command Center (Landing Page)**.
5.  **The Capture:** User enters their email to join the "Vanguard" (Waitlist).
6.  **The Close:** On Launch Day, we email the list a direct link to buy on Amazon/Shopify.

---

## 2. LANDING PAGE REQUIREMENTS
*   **Headline:** "The Kitchen is Your Base of Operations. Stop Winging It."
*   **Social Proof:** Images of the waterproof paper and the self-standing binder.
*   **The Lead Magnet:** "Get 5 more SOPs instantly when you join the waitlist."
*   **The Scarcity:** "First 500 units include a limited-edition serialized field plate."

---

## 3. LINK TRACKING (Intelligence)
*   **Tool:** Use Bitly or Rebrandly to track which "Mission Leak" source (Instagram vs. TikTok) is sending the most recruits.
*   **UTM Parameters:** Ensure every link has a source tag to measure ROI.
