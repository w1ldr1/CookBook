# MARKETING FUNNEL ARCHITECTURE
**Subject:** Traffic Flow and Lead Capture
**Objective:** Convert "Mission Leak" readers into Day 1 Buyers.

> **⚠ MODEL UPDATE (2026):** Purchase destination is now **Amazon KDP** (physical) and
> **Gumroad** (digital). No Shopify or Kickstarter. See `05_Logistics_Supply/POD_Strategy.md`.

---

## 1. THE FLOW (The "Infil" Path)
1.  **Traffic Source:** Social Media (IG/TikTok/X) shows a mission execution video or branch reveal.
2.  **The Hook:** "Download SOP-001 (The Steak Mission) for free — link in bio."
3.  **The Asset:** User clicks to Gumroad → downloads the "Mission Leak" 3-SOP PDF (free, requires email).
4.  **The Capture:** Email is captured by Gumroad → syncs to Mailchimp/ConvertKit mailing list.
5.  **The Conversion:** Inside the PDF, a back-page CTA links to the **Command Center (landing page)** and directly to Amazon.
6.  **The Close:** On Launch Day, email the list a direct link to the Amazon KDP listing. Day 1 purchase volume drives Amazon Best-Seller algorithm.

---

## 2. LANDING PAGE REQUIREMENTS (index.html)

*   **Headline:** "The Kitchen is Your Base of Operations. Stop Winging It."
*   **Sub-headline:** "50 field-tested SOPs. No fluff. No soft-focus food styling. Just missions."
*   **Social Proof:** Hero shot of the coil-bound book lying flat on a counter next to cast iron. Customer review screenshots once live.
*   **The Lead Magnet CTA:** "Download 3 Free Missions" button → links to Gumroad free sampler (email required).
*   **The Purchase CTA:** "Order on Amazon" button → KDP listing. "Get the Digital Edition" → Gumroad $14.99.
*   **The Series Preview:** Show all 6 branch edition covers (Army done, others "coming soon") — drives discovery and gifting intent.
*   **No scarcity tactics** — POD has no inventory limit. Use urgency from new edition launches instead.

---

## 3. LINK TRACKING (Intelligence)
*   **Tool:** Use Bitly or Rebrandly to track which source (IG vs. TikTok vs. X) drives the most Gumroad downloads and Amazon clicks.
*   **UTM Parameters:** Tag every link with source + medium + campaign (e.g., `?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=bio&utm_campaign=army-launch`).
*   **Gumroad analytics:** Built-in view/conversion tracking — check weekly.
*   **Amazon KDP dashboard:** Shows page reads (for Kindle Unlimited if enrolled) and print sales by ASIN.
