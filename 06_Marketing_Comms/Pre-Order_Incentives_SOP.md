# MARKETING SOP: PRE-ORDER INCENTIVES
**Subject:** Driving Early Deployment Sales to Secure Best-Seller Ranking
**Mission Objective:** Create "Irresistible Momentum" during the 30 days prior to launch.

---

## 1. THE "VANGUARD" REWARD SYSTEM
People do not pre-order for the book; they pre-order for the **Value Add**.

### [ ] Tier 1: The "Digital Intel" Pack
*   **Cost to Author:** $0.
*   **The Reward:** Anyone who pre-orders gets an immediate PDF download of "SOP-000: Survival Skills" (3 recipes not in the book).

### [ ] Tier 2: The "Serialized Plate" (First 500 Units)
*   **Cost to Author:** $2.00/unit.
*   **The Reward:** A physical, brushed-aluminum or heavy sticker "Property of [Unit Name]" plate to stick on the front of the binder. Serialized 001/500.
*   **Psychology:** FOMO (Fear of Missing Out) drives Day 1 sales.

---

## 2. THE "MISSION LEAK" VIDEO SERIES
*   **Content:** 60-second clips of the book surviving "Extreme Conditions."
*   **Video 01:** The "Pressure Washer" test (Cleaning the waterproof pages).
*   **Video 02:** The "Hot Oil" spill (Durability test).
*   **Video 03:** The "One-Handed Flip" (Easel binder utility).

---

## 3. AMAZON ALGORITHM MANEUVERS
*   **Strategy:** Funnel all "Vanguard" traffic to Amazon on a single 24-hour window.
*   **The Goal:** Hit #1 in "Men's Cooking" or "Outdoor Cooking" categories. This triggers the "Best Seller" badge, which then drives organic sales from strangers.

---

## 4. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Review Extraction:** 7 days after delivery, email every buyer a request for a "Field Report" (5-star review). 
*   **The Bribe:** "Post a photo of your SOP-001 Steak on Instagram, tag us, and we'll send you a free 'Tactical Apron' patch."
