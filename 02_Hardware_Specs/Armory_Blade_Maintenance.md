# HARDWARE SPEC: THE ARMORY (BLADE MAINTENANCE)
**Subject:** Maintaining the Operational Integrity of Primary Slicing Tools
**Mission Objective:** To ensure the "Primary Blade" remains sharp, safe, and ready for deployment. A dull blade is a liability.

---

## 1. THE "DULL BLADE" THREAT ASSESSMENT
A dull knife requires more force to penetrate the target, increasing the probability of a "Slip & Strike" injury. 
*   **Sharp Knife:** Predictable, surgical, requires minimal pressure.
*   **Dull Knife:** Erratic, dangerous, destroys the cellular structure of food (bruising).

---

## 2. MAINTENANCE GEAR (The Honing Rod)
Most people mistake "Honing" for "Sharpening." 

### [ ] 12" Ceramic or Steel Honing Rod
*   **Role:** Realignment. It pushes the microscopic "teeth" of the blade back into a straight line.
*   **SOP:** Hold the rod vertically. Sweep the blade down the rod at a **15-20 degree angle**. Perform 5 strokes per side before every "Heavy Lift" mission.

---

## 3. CLEANING PROTOCOLS (Post-Mission)
*   **NO DISHWASHER:** The high heat, caustic detergents, and "rattling" against other hardware will chip the edge and crack the handle.
*   **Immediate Sanitation:** Wash by hand with warm soapy water immediately after use.
*   **Dry to Store:** Never leave a blade in the sink or air-dry. Water causes "Pitting" (micro-rust). Hand-dry with a towel and store in a sheath or magnetic strip.

---

## 4. THE ARMORY UPGRADE (Professional Sharpening)
Honing maintains an edge; Sharpening *creates* a new one.
*   **Frequency:** Once every 6–12 months, the blade must be taken to a professional or run through a high-quality whetstone progression.
*   **The Paper Test:** A mission-ready blade should slice through a single sheet of vertical printer paper with zero snagging.

---

## 5. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Safety Check:** Always carry a blade "Point Down, Blade Back" when moving through the kitchen.
*   **Surface Discipline:** Only cut on Wood or Poly-boards. Glass, marble, and granite are "Edge-Killers" and will neutralize your blade's effectiveness instantly.
