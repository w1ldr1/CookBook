# HARDWARE SPEC: CAST IRON RESTORATION
**Subject:** Recovering and Re-Seasoning Compromised "Heavy Armor"
**Mission Objective:** To return a rusted or "stripped" cast iron skillet to operational readiness.

---

## 1. DAMAGE ASSESSMENT
*   **Surface Rust:** Orange oxidation on the surface. Fixable.
*   **Carbon Buildup:** Sticky, black "gunk." Needs stripping.
*   **Cracked/Warped:** Structural failure. Asset is non-recoverable.

---

## 2. STAGE 1: THE STRIP (Deep Clean)
1.  **Abrasive Maneuver:** Use steel wool or a chainmail scrubber with warm water and soap. Scrub until all rust and "gunk" are removed. You should see raw, grey/black iron.
2.  **Total Dry:** Place on a stove burner on low heat for 5 minutes to ensure 100% moisture extraction.

---

## 3. STAGE 2: THE SEASONING (Re-Armoring)
1.  **Lubrication:** Apply a very thin layer of Grapeseed or Avocado oil to the entire skillet (inside, outside, handle).
2.  **The Wipe-Down:** Use a clean paper towel to wipe *all* the oil off. You want the surface to look dry, but have a microscopic film of oil. Too much oil creates a sticky mess.
3.  **Thermal Bond:** Place upside down in a **450°F (232°C)** oven for 1 hour. Place a sheet of foil on the rack below to catch drips.
4.  **Cool Down:** Let it cool inside the oven. 

---

## 4. MAINTENANCE SOP
Repeat the seasoning process 2-3 times for a new skillet to build a "Non-Stick Armor" that rivals Teflon.
