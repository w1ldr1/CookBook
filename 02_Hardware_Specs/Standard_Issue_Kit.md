# CHAPTER 02: STANDARD ISSUE HARDWARE
**Subject:** Essential Gear for Sustainment Operations
**Philosophy:** "Buy Once, Cry Once" (Quality over quantity)

---

## 1. MISSION OBJECTIVE
To provide for the Unit, an operator must maintain high-quality, durable tools. We prioritize "Buy Once, Cry Once" utility—gear that lasts a lifetime and performs under high thermal stress.

---

## 2. THE CORE KIT (Standard Issue)

### [ ] 01. THE PRIMARY BLADE (8" Chef's Knife)
*   **Mission Role:** All-purpose slicing, dicing, and processing.
*   **Tactical Spec:** High-carbon stainless steel, full tang (metal runs through the handle).
*   **Maintenance:** Must be honed every 3-5 uses. Professionally sharpened annually.
*   **Warning:** Never place in the dishwasher. This is a precision instrument.

### [ ] 02. THE HEAVY ARMOR (12" Cast Iron Skillet)
*   **Mission Role:** High-heat searing and oven-to-stove transitions.
*   **Tactical Spec:** Pre-seasoned American-made cast iron (e.g., Lodge or Field Company).
*   **Maintenance:** Clean with hot water and a brush. Dry immediately. Re-apply a light coat of oil to prevent oxidation.

### [ ] 03. THE RANGEFINDER (Digital Instant-Read Thermometer)
*   **Mission Role:** Critical data acquisition. Eliminates guesswork.
*   **Tactical Spec:** Minimum read time <3 seconds with ±0.7°F accuracy.
*   **Field Intel:** This is the difference between a "Successful Mission" and "Dry Rations."

### [ ] 04. THE FORCE MULTIPLIER (6-Quart Dutch Oven)
*   **Mission Role:** Slow-cooking, bread baking, and large-batch stews.
*   **Tactical Spec:** Enameled cast iron (e.g., Le Creuset or Lodge Enameled).
*   **Utility:** Provides massive thermal retention for long-duration "Heavy Lift" missions.

### [ ] 05. THE EXTRACTION TOOLS (Heavy Duty Tongs)
*   **Mission Role:** Manipulation of hot targets without piercing the surface.
*   **Tactical Spec:** 12-inch stainless steel with a high-tension spring.
*   **Avoid:** Silicone or plastic tips for high-heat steak missions (SOP-001).

---

## 3. SECONDARY SUPPORT GEAR
*   **Wood Cutting Board:** Large surface area (minimum 18"x12"). Never use glass or marble; it destroys the blade edge.
*   **Chainmail Scrubber:** For cleaning the "Heavy Armor" without stripping the seasoning.
*   **Half-Sheet Pans:** Industrial grade aluminum for "Field Ration" prep.

---

## 4. AFTER ACTION REPORT (AAR)
*   **Standardization:** Organize hardware so you can find it in the dark. 
*   **Readiness:** A clean station is a safe station. Hardware must be neutralized (cleaned) immediately after the mission ends.
