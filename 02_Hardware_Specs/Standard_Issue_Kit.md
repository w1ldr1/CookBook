# CHAPTER 02: STANDARD ISSUE HARDWARE
**Subject:** Essential Gear for Sustainment Operations

To provide for the Unit, an operator must maintain high-quality, durable tools. We prioritize "Buy Once, Cry Once" utility—gear that lasts a lifetime.

## 1. THE PRIMARY BLADE (8" Chef's Knife)
*   **Mission Role:** All-purpose slicing, dicing, and processing.
*   **Spec:** High-carbon stainless steel with a full tang.
*   **Maintenance:** Must be honed every 3 uses and professionally sharpened annually.

## 2. THE HEAVY ARMOR (12" Cast Iron Skillet)
*   **Mission Role:** High-heat searing and oven-to-stove transitions.
*   **Spec:** Pre-seasoned American-made cast iron.
*   **Maintenance:** Never use a dishwasher. Re-oil after every deployment to prevent oxidation.

## 3. THE RANGEFINDER (Digital Instant-Read Thermometer)
*   **Mission Role:** Critical data acquisition. Eliminates guesswork in protein internal temps.
*   **Spec:** Minimum read time <3 seconds with ±0.7°F accuracy.
*   **Tactical Advantage:** Ensures food safety and prevents "over-dwell" (dry meat).

## 4. THE FORCE MULTIPLIER (Dutch Oven)
*   **Mission Role:** Slow-cooking, bread baking, and large-batch stews.
*   **Spec:** 5.5 to 7 Quart capacity, enameled cast iron.

## 5. THE EXTRACTION TOOLS (Heavy Duty Tongs)
*   **Mission Role:** Manipulation of hot targets without piercing the surface (preserving juices).
*   **Spec:** Stainless steel with high-tension spring. Avoid silicone tips for high-heat missions.

---

### AFTER ACTION REPORT (Maintenance SOP)
*   **Standardization:** Keep all hardware in the same location for "Muscle Memory" during high-speed prep.
*   **Cleanliness:** A clean station is a safe station. Hardware must be neutralized (cleaned) immediately after the mission ends.
